An Introduction ★★★★★

Dara Kesh wants to introduce you to her contacts in pirate space. She has a gift that will open doors — literally.

Dialog

Offer

You've been running my errands. Time to graduate. The real supply chain starts in pirate space. The strongholds produce most of the contraband in the galaxy — moonshine from the stills, void dust from the labs, ghost rounds from the armament benches. If you want to be a smuggler and not just a courier, you need to buy from the source. Problem is, pirates don't let strangers dock. They require reputation — proof you're not a threat. The usual way to earn that is... violent. Kill some people, prove you're dangerous, they respect that. But I know another way. I have a standing arrangement with Commandant Voss at Alhena. A case of starshine and nerve burn — his personal favorites — delivered as a gift, and he'll vouch for you. No blood required. Take this package to Voss Redoubt. Tell them Kesh sent you. While you have this job active, the pirates won't bother you. Consider it a letter of introduction.
