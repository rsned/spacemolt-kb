Courier Run: Ghost Rounds to Confederacy Central Command ★★★★★★

Your handler at Frontier Station has 3 units of untraceable ghost rounds that need to leave the books here and reappear somewhere else. It's bound for Confederacy Central Command — a buyer wants rounds that forensics can only log as 'unknown ordnance'. Solarian customs are thorough and humorless. Assume every inbound ship gets scanned and route around their patrols — if they catch you, you lose the cargo.
