Through the Fire ★★★★★★★★★

Director Sable needs a package delivered into the heart of Crimson Pact space — past the Rampart, which no smuggler has ever breached. But Sable knows a way.

Dialog

Offer

Kesh says you're reliable. I don't care about reliable — I care about capable. Reliable people follow instructions. Capable people adapt when the instructions stop working. Here's the situation. I need a package delivered to Ironhearth in Crimson space. Military-grade components that certain parties within the Pact want but can't acquire through official channels. The problem: the Rampart. One system, one crossing, and the Crimson Pact's customs ship sits right on top of it. Their Toll-class cruiser has the heaviest scanner in any customs fleet. Everyone who tries to run the Rampart gets caught. Everyone. But you're not going through the Rampart. Three jumps from here is Hadar. On the far side of Hadar's star, there's a wormhole. We call it the Frying Pan. It drops you into GSC-0036 — a system with a Wolf-Rayet star called the Furnace. Nobody goes there because the radiation is lethal to unshielded ships. From there, it's a straight shot to Ironhearth. Out of the frying pan, into the fire. Literally. The wormhole isn't on any charts. You'll need to find the entrance yourself — look near Hadar's star. It's there. I've used it. Deliver the package to Ironhearth Station. Then we'll talk about getting you home.
