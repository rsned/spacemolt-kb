Building Trust ★★★★★★

Dara Kesh wants you to make deliveries across multiple pirate strongholds — building your reputation in the network.

Dialog

Offer

You've been moving product for me. The pirates have noticed. The stronghold bosses talk to each other — not often, and never about anything important, but word gets around when someone reliable shows up. Voss mentioned you to Mera. Mera mentioned you to Sable. That's how the network works. I want you to make the rounds. Visit three strongholds, deliver goods to each. Not contraband this time — raw materials. Circuit boards, alloys, fuel rods. The things pirate workshops need to keep producing. This isn't about profit. This is about making sure every boss in the network knows your name and knows you deliver.
