Expanding Operations ★★★★★★

Dara Kesh is testing your range. Multiple deliveries to multiple empires — prove you know the routes and the gaps.

Dialog

Offer

You can run one route. Good. Can you run three? I have clients across the galaxy. Each one wants something specific, each one is in a different empire, and each empire has different customs coverage. This is the job that separates the amateurs from the professionals. Three deliveries. Nerve burn to Sol — you know that route. Red mist to the Central Nexus — the Voidborn want their combat stims. And pirate moonshine to Frontier Station in the Outer Rim. The Frontier is the easy one. The Outer Rim doesn't bother with customs — no border bureaucracy, no patrols, no questions. It's why half the smugglers in the galaxy retire there. The others, you'll need to be clever. You know the gaps by now. Epsilon Eridani for Solarian space. GSC-0027 for Voidborn space. This pays well. It should — you're earning it.
