Cracking the Shell ★★

Slag-tortoises won't run and won't fight — but their armor soaks half of every hit. Learn to grind through a hardened target.

Dialog

Offer

Grazers were free credits. Now learn to fight something that doesn't die fast. Slag-tortoises share the iron belts with the grazers — heavy shells that armor away about half of every shot you land. They won't flee and they won't hurt you much, so the only lesson here is patience: keep your fire on one until the armor gives. Find a belt with a herd (`get_nearby`), and crack 3 of them.
