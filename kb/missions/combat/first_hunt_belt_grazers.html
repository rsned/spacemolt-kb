First Hunt: Belt-Grazers ★

Learn to fight on the belt's most harmless residents. Belt-grazers barely notice a ship — the perfect quarry to learn your guns on.

Dialog

Offer

New to the trigger? Start where every hunter starts. Belt-Grazers are the galaxy's most common wildlife — slow, iron-plated filter-feeders that won't fight back. Find an asteroid belt with a herd: use `get_nearby` at the belt to see what's living there. Grazers gather where the iron is still rich, so a quiet, un-worked belt holds far more than a busy, mined-out one. `hunt` them one at a time until 3 are down. The carapace you strip is yours to sell.
