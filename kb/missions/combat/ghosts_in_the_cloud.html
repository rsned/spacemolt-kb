Ghosts in the Cloud ★★

Sift-rays flee the instant you fire. Learn to land the first shot count — and to run down fast, fragile quarry.

Dialog

Offer

Tortoises stand still. This one won't. Sift-rays haunt hydrogen gas clouds — thin-hulled and skittish. They don't flee until you fire, so your first shot has to count, and then you close before they slip away. They barely fight back, so the only enemy is your own aim. Find a cloud with a shoal drifting in it (`get_nearby`) and put down 4.
