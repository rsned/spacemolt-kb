The Long Hunt ★★★

A pilot-whale is a wall of hull that never breaks off once provoked. Learn to win a long fight — and to graduate to real threats.

Dialog

Offer

Last lesson, and the biggest. Pilot-whales pod up in argon gas clouds — gentle giants, but a grown one carries more hull than anything you've fought, and once you draw blood it will not flee. This is a long fight: watch your own hull, keep your magazines fed, and don't start it if you can't finish it. Find a pod (`get_nearby` in a gas cloud — the quieter, richer clouds hold them) and bring one down. After this, you're ready for quarry that hunts back — the standing leviathan bounty, or the pirates the security office keeps posting.
