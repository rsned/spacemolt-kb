What Are They Buying? ★★★★★

Market Director Kasim wants to hold some of what the Voidborn are paying for. Mine 5 seam glass past the rim and deliver it to the Grand Exchange.

Dialog

Offer

I read the Collective through its spending. Central Nexus is paying pilots for recordings, and every one of those recordings comes from the same direction: out past the rim, where the glass is. The Collective is buying silence about a place. When a buyer pays for silence, the product is valuable. I would like to hold some of the product. Seam glass forms in the shells of dead stars in the frontier. Mine five units yourself, so I know where it came from, and bring them to the Exchange.
