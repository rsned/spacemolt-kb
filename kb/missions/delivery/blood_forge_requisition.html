Blood Forge Requisition ★★★★★

Transport Darksteel Plating from Blood Forge to the Anvil Arsenal for a classified Crimson Pact construction project.

Dialog

Offer

The Pact is building something. I won't tell you what — you don't need to know and I don't want you to know. What I need is logistics. Blood Forge has been smelting Darksteel Plating for the project, and the shipment is ready for transport to the Anvil Arsenal for assembly. Travel to Blood Forge, pick up 10 units of Darksteel Plating, and deliver them to the Anvil. The Anvil's fabrication teams are waiting. Do not delay, do not divert, and do not discuss the cargo with anyone outside Crimson command structure. Are we clear?
