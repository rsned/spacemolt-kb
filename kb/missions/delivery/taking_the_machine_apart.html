Taking the Machine Apart ★★★★★★★★

Bring Sinter parts from Repeater wrecks: 6 mirror facets, 4 echo cells and 1 fold knot.

Dialog

Offer

Somebody had half the method forty years ago. The machines out in the frontier have the rest of it. Those things that fold space around themselves when they fight. Their wrecks are full of parts that do things to distance. I want mirror facets and echo cells, which you can pull off any of them. And I want a fold knot. Those only come out of the big ones, the Spires, and only near the very centre of all that trouble. Bring a friend. Bring several. Or find someone who already has one and make them a very good offer.

Complete

Oh, you beauty. Look at this. The fold knot isn't a power source. It's a hinge. It takes a fold in space and holds it open, and the facets and cells keep the fold from collapsing on itself. That's a lane. That's what a lane is, a fold held open from both ends. I've learned what I need from this knot, so take it back. You'll need it for what comes next. I've written up a bench design. You're going to build it.
