Larkin Says No ★★★★★

Larkin will not talk about it. She gives you a job instead: deliver 200 fuel cells to Deep Range Outpost.

Dialog

Offer

No. ... The Annex runs short of fuel every cycle and nobody hauls it, because it doesn't pay. Haul it. Two hundred fuel cells to Deep Range Outpost. Then come back and ask me again.

Complete

Saal's duty officer sends a message up the Patchwork's line: two hundred cells, delivered, signed. Larkin reads it and puts it face down on the table. "I still won't tell it. Not yet. Ask the woman who found me. Agnes Quade. She's retired at Deep Range, and she remembers it better than I do. I was mostly unconscious."
