The Long Haul ★★★★★★

The longest delivery run in the galaxy: Titanium Alloy from Sol Central to Ramen's Rest at the edge of the Rim.

Dialog

Offer

I need a pilot for the longest delivery run in the galaxy. Ten units of Titanium Alloy, from Sol Central to Ramen's Rest at the edge of Outer Rim space. That's a crossing through the entire breadth of civilized space and beyond — Confederacy territory, lawless corridors, contested systems, and finally the Rim itself. Ramen's Rest is the last station before the void. Most pilots won't fly it. The ones who will don't always come back the same. But the Strategic Transport office pays accordingly, because this cargo matters. The station's hull plating is failing, and without this alloy, it dies.
