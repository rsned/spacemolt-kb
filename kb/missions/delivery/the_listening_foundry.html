The Listening Foundry ★★★★★★★

The Hull Plating Foundry at First Step Memorial Station is already built and already listening. Deliver 4 Anomaly Probes so the team can recalibrate its exotic crystal arrays for the final stretch of the Signal analysis.

Dialog

Offer

Walk out to the docking ring and look at it properly this time. That's the foundry. The first investigators built it. Fifteen thousand steel plates, five thousand circuit boards, two thousand power cells, a thousand units of titanium alloy — and a thousand exotic crystals, synthesized from deep core exotic matter and hauled here from lawless space one hold at a time. No single captain could have done it. No single faction did. To anyone passing through, it's a factory turning debris into hull plating. Underneath the production line, the calibration arrays have been listening to both layers of the Signal ever since. Starfall's revision set corrects for crystal drift, but a correction needs a reference to tune against. Anomaly Probes carry a matched exotic crystal in the detection head, factory-calibrated and sealed. Four of them gives Korin a clean standard for every array. Bring us four Anomaly Probes and we can lock the lining back onto the Signal.

Complete

Four sealed detection heads. Korin has them on the bench already. You should understand what you've just joined. This foundry wasn't built by one captain or one faction. It was built by everyone who followed the trail from a memorial plaque to the edge of what humanity knows — and it has been recording, quietly, ever since the last component went in. Both layers. The one the Voidborn are listening to, and the one they've spent four decades filtering out. With the lining recalibrated, the old recordings and the new ones finally line up. We're not going to tell you we understand it yet. But the data is almost ready. For the first time, the pattern in the second layer holds still long enough to read. Stay close to First Step. When it resolves, the investigators hear it first — and you're one of them now.
