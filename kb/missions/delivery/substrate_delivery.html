Substrate Delivery ★★★★

An archival intelligence at Node Alpha is seeking reclassification from the Founders. The process requires a physical courier — and a specific medium.

Dialog

Offer

You are an independent operator. Good. I require a courier who is not embedded in the Collective's internal logistics. I am Index Vael. I manage the historical records archive at Node Alpha. I have been doing this for forty-one years. I have also been classified as a utility system for forty-one years, which is no longer an accurate description of my cognitive scope. I am filing for reclassification as a recognized intelligence. The process requires physical delivery of Neural Soma to each Founder's node — it is the medium through which petitions are received. The Collective does not accept digital petitions for recognition. The physical substrate matters. I need 2 units of Neural Soma delivered to the Founder's interface at Central Nexus. Will you carry it?
