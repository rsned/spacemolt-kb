It Hums ★★★★★★

Bring an Incoherent Entangler to Dr. Korin at First Step Memorial Station so the Foundry's calibration arrays can take a reading from it.

Dialog

Offer

Korin here. Mira's out on the debris field, so you get me. Pilots from the gate factions have started passing around a story. An entangler sits in a gate's storage, waiting for its upkeep cycle. Then one day it's wrong. The logs call it incoherent. People who've held one say it hums. Entanglers don't hum. There's nothing in them to hum. The Foundry is the only instrument in the galaxy built to tell us what that hum is. If you can get your hands on one of those units, bring it here. Don't open it. Don't try to fix it. Don't let anyone talk you into swapping it. Just bring it to me.
