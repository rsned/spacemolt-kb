Witness for the maintenance ★★

A technician is replacing a light. Collect your witness credentials, attend the appointment, and return the report. You are independent because nobody here trusts you. Terms: visit Procyon Colonial Station, then Sirius Observatory Station, then return to Nova Terra Central, in that order. Wait docked at the second stop for 30 game ticks; leaving pauses the count. Carry the supplied Independent witness credentials (1 cargo space) back intact and call complete_mission. Fuel and travel expenses are yours. No credits or items are paid. Completion grants 20 smuggling XP and clears up to 500 credits of your fixer tab, limited to the remaining balance. Abandoning does not clear debt. Deadline: seven days.
