Federation Payment ★★★★★

Transport the Nebula Federation's rare material payment to Synchrony Hub in Voidborn space as part of the tri-empire research exchange.

Dialog

Offer

The sensor data is exactly what our astronomy division needed. As agreed in the tri-party research contract, the Federation's payment to the Voidborn is in rare materials rather than credits — Nexus Prime doesn't value currency the way we do. Deliver 10 units of refined exotic material to Synchrony Hub. Synchrony handles distribution for Voidborn research projects outside the core Nexus Prime network. The exotic material has been packaged and secured in our bonded warehouse. Pick it up from exchange storage and carry it to Synchrony. Standard courier terms — you're responsible for the cargo until delivery is confirmed.
