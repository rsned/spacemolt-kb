Three Aboard ★★★★★★

A survey tender's mayday buoy at Still Calling is eleven months old, with three crew aboard and a few months of charge left. Bring life support, copy the buoy's data to take the crew aboard, and carry them home to Frontier Station.

Dialog

Offer

There's a mayday on the old emergency band from an isolated red dwarf called Still Calling. It's one of ours, a Guild survey tender, three aboard. They came through a wormhole and it shut behind them. The beacon's timestamp says eleven months. The beacon also says it has a few months of charge left, and so, I'd guess, do they. Every other distress call I know of out there is too late. This one isn't. Bring them life support, take them aboard, and bring them home. You'll need three free berths. Plan your ship for it.
