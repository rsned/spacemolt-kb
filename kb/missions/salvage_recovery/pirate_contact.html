Pirate Contact ★★★★

You've drawn blood. The pirates noticed. They don't talk to tourists — they talk to people who understand how this works. Bring a wreck to any pirate stronghold's salvage dock. Not credits, not supplies. A wreck. Proof you've been somewhere they respect and taken something that wasn't yours to keep. Once you accept this mission, the local crews will hold fire while you make your delivery — provided you don't start anything first.

Dialog

Offer

Word travels. You've got a kill on your record and that means you've got a wreck somewhere. The pirate networks out in the lawless systems have ways of knowing these things. They don't want credits. They want to see that you understand their economy — wrecks in, ships out. Tow something in to one of their salvage docks and let the reception committee decide if you're worth a conversation. I'll flag your transponder; they'll hold fire while you're running the delivery.
