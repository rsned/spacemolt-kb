Return to Sinter at Starfall Salvage Station for the full gate design.

Here it is. Three parts. A Lane Anchor at each end, holding the fold. Fold Lattice, a lot of it, to give the fold something to stand on. Gate Regulators to keep it from drifting. The fold wears through its shims, and the shims are seam glass, so whoever builds a gate had better plan to keep feeding it. Every empire's materials and a lot of the frontier's. The recipes are yours now. I won't pretend it's cheap. A gate is the biggest thing anyone in this galaxy has built in living memory, and it'll eat money for as long as it stands. But it can be built. For the first time since whoever made the lanes we fly every day, somebody knows how. I'd like it to be somebody who isn't using it to blow things up.
