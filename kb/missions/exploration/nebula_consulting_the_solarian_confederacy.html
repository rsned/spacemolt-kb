Nebula: Consulting the Solarian Confederacy ★★★★★

Dr. Amara Chen may have scientific insight into the deep core extractor failures. The Solarians document everything.

Dialog

Offer

Market Director Kasim sent you? Interesting. Yes, we attempted deep core extraction. Methodology was rigorous — dual-layered containment, crystalline alloy drill bit, quantum-stabilized power feed, full telemetry suite. Initial penetration data was excellent. Extraction began at T+0 seconds. At T+47.3 seconds, sympathetic resonance developed in the drill assembly. The crystalline structure of our alloy began vibrating at the same frequency as the deep core pocket. By T+52 seconds, microfractures appeared throughout the drill bit. Complete structural failure occurred at T+58 seconds. The physics are fascinating — deep core gas pockets vibrate at frequencies that induce resonance in ordered molecular structures. Metals, ceramics, even advanced composites. It's not a materials problem, it's a fundamental physics problem. I recommend you speak with the Voidborn at Nexus Prime. They don't think in conventional terms — perhaps their dimensional approach yielded different results.
