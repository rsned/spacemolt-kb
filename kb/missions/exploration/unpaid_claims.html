Unpaid Claims ★★★★★★

Senior Auditor Ghosh wants the Federation claim stakes on the Assay Line checked, and the untraceable charter stake at Blue Stake recorded. Copy all five stake plates and return to the Grand Exchange.

Dialog

Offer

The Federation staked a line of claims in the frontier years ago, out on what the surveyors called the Assay Line. Long Watch, Fossil Light, Deep Patient, Ashen Reach. The claims are on our books. The fees are not in our accounts. I would like to know why. And there is one more. Someone filed an iridium claim under a charter that does not exist, in a system that does not exist. Blue Stake, off every lane. I would like the fees. Record the plates on all five stakes. Bring me the readings.
