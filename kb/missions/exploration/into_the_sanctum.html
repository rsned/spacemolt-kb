Into the Sanctum ★★★★★★★

Fly 144.2 degrees from Nexus Prime to the system at the end of Phos-2's bearing. Recover data at the Receiver Housing and at Node Zero.

Dialog

Offer

The bearing ends at a system with no lane in, a cold white dwarf with a wrecked Voidborn station beside something older. Korin thinks the older thing is a receiver. If it is, it is older than any Voidborn structure on the charts, and the Collective has kept the power on beside it for two hundred years. Fly there and recover what you can at both: the receiver housing and the station. The Voidborn station has a designation in Phos-2's ledger. Node Zero.

Complete

Korin has run the receiver fragments through every decoder we own, and they don't decode. They don't need to. Read them again. Mira: "The station is a watchtower. It watches the receiver, and nothing else, and it has done that for as long as there's been a Collective. Two hundred years of watching one dead machine. Whatever happened at that receiver, the Collective has never stopped caring about it. And they keep it watched and empty." Korin has found one more thing in the station's log. A sensor at the system's edge that the array was never pointed at.
