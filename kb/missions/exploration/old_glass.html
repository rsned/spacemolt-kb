Old Glass ★★★★★★

Take samples from three old listening sites: the sensor mast at Timberline, the outpost remnants at Bharani and the impact crater at TRAPPIST-1. Bring them to Sinter.

Dialog

Offer

I found it. TRAPPIST-1 is in the old survey records from the First Step people, the preservation society. They went looking at some decommissioned listening posts years ago: a sensor mast at Timberline, a bunker at Bharani, and a crater at TRAPPIST-1 where a third post used to be. The reports say the posts had filter housings lined with a crystal nobody could identify. Precision-cut. Unusually regular. Now, I've spent the last few weeks staring at precision-cut glass that does strange things. Go and get me samples. All three sites. If you've been there before, you know the way. If you haven't, you'll want to read what the preservation society wrote afterwards.

Complete

Right. Sit down. The filter crystal from Timberline and Bharani is seam glass. Not like seam glass. It IS seam glass, cut to a lattice and tuned. Somebody, forty years ago, knew enough about this stuff to build listening posts out of it, and they were listening to something through the lanes. That's half of the method I've been chasing, sitting in a decommissioned bunker. The crater is worse. That wasn't an impact. Everything in the wall points back to one spot, because a fold opened inside whatever was there and then slammed shut. Somebody took lane science and made a weapon out of it. The fused plating in the wall isn't Solarian, it isn't Crimson. I checked Nebula and Rim records too, before you ask. No match. And that's as far as I'm going to think about it, because I'm an engineer. Engineers ask how. The how is bad enough.
