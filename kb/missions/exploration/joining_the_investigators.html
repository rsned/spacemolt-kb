Joining the Investigators ★★★★★

Join the coalition of pilots that formed around the First Step investigation. Fly the picket route through The Telescope so the watch logs your transponder, then return to First Step Memorial Station.

Dialog

Offer

We've finished the cross-reference analysis. Before we show you what it says, you should know you are not the first pilot to stand where you're standing. Some months ago we put out a call. We'd followed this trail as far as three researchers could follow it, and what came next meant going deeper into Collective territory than anyone goes alone. We said we would not move until twenty investigators stood with us. We thought it would take a year. It took weeks. Pilots came from Sol, from the Rim, from Crimson space — people who had never met, who had each read the same evidence and reached the same conclusion. That coalition still exists. It keeps a picket at The Telescope, one jump from here, watching the approaches the Collective would use if it decided to make First Step disappear a second time. Every investigator flies that route once. It's how the watch learns your transponder, and how you learn who's out there with you.

Complete

The picket has you logged. You're on the roster now — one more name on a list that started with twenty and hasn't stopped growing. Here is what we told the others. Phos-2's allocation records confirm that the Voidborn's classified Signal expenditure didn't just continue after First Step — it spiked. Three times in four decades. The most recent spike started before the coalition formed and it has not stopped. They're not winding down. They're scaling up. The first investigators went into Voidborn space to find out why. You're going to follow the same route, because we need current readings to set against theirs.
