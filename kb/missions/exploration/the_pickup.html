The Pickup ★★★★★★★

Agnes Quade gives the pickup point from her own log: 226.5 degrees from Deep Range, 1500 GU, about 150 ticks. Drift there and return to Deep Range.

Dialog

Offer

Siv sent you? Sit down, then. I captained the Rim recovery ship that picked her up. Nearly thirty years ago. She was drifting on a dead drive with her air running thin, and she'd stopped answering hails two days before we got to her. I kept my own log of the pickup, not the Guild's copy. 226.5 degrees from Deep Range, fifteen hundred units out. About a hundred and fifty ticks of drift. Nothing on the way; the nearest star's two hundred and seventy-odd units off the line. Go and stand where we found her. Then come back and tell me what you think.

Complete

Her nose was pointed home when we found her. Her drive log showed two burns back toward the dark on the way home. She spent fuel she couldn't spare to look again. That's why the drive died where it did. Don't tell her I told you that.
