Who Buys Old Charts ★★★

A Collective account has bought every Pathfinder Guild chart of the void past Deep Range. Dock at Deep Range and ask Expeditionary Coordinator Saal what else they asked for.

Dialog

Offer

Here's a thing that doesn't happen. Somebody bought every Guild chart we have of the void past Deep Range. All of them, the old survey sheets too, the ones nobody's looked at since the Rim stopped sending expeditions. Paid full price, didn't haggle. The account traces back to Central Nexus. The Voidborn don't buy charts. They make their own, and they're better than ours. So they wanted something else and the charts were the cover. Saal keeps the Annex records at Deep Range. Go and ask him what else they wanted.

Complete

Saal says: "They wanted the expedition logs too. I told them we don't sell those. They didn't argue. They never argue." That's what I was afraid of. The charts show where we went. The logs show what we found. Somebody at Central Nexus wanted to know what the Rim saw out there, and wanted to know it quietly.
