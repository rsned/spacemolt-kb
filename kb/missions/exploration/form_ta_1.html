Form TA-1 ★

Apply for a permit to visit the surface of Earth. The Office of Terrestrial Access at Sol Central issues the form. Carry it to Alpha Centauri Colonial Station for proof of standing.

Dialog

Offer

Good day. You are asking about a visit to the surface. Yes, that is possible. People are often surprised. The Ordinance provides for a single supervised visit under permit, and this Office issues the permit. There is a queue. The queue is fair. Before you can join it you need Form TA-1, completed, with three endorsements. Most applicants stop at the form. I do not know why. It is a very clear form.

Complete

The registrar at Alpha Centauri reads the form twice, looks at you once, and stamps Section 4. "Confirmed that the applicant exists. Confirmed that the applicant is one person." She adds a remark in the margin and will not show it to you. Two endorsements remain, and she has the list.
