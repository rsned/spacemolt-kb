Look, Don't Touch ★★★★★★★★★

Recover data at the Threshold in Gloaming without starting a fight, then return to the Patchwork with the readings. A cloak is suggested, not required.

Dialog

Offer

I sent two pilots before you. Good ones. Neither came back, and neither called. I don't know what happened to them. I know they didn't fold out. So don't let anything out there see you. Fly cloaked if you have one. Plot your way out before you go in, and leave the tick you have the readings. Get the readings. Come home.

Complete

Larkin reads the Threshold data standing up. Then she sits. "It isn't pointed at us. It's pointed out. Something means to come in that way, and it isn't in a hurry." She folds the chart away. "There's an engineer at Starfall who says the lanes were built. Before today I'd have laughed. Go and listen to him."
