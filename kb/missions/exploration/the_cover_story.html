The Cover Story ★★★★★★★

Collect the recalibration revisions for the Hull Plating Foundry at First Step from the Outer Rim engineers at Starfall who designed it. The foundry secretly houses Signal monitoring equipment.

Dialog

Offer

Last time you stood here we said we needed to hear what the Voidborn are blocking. We can. We have been able to for some time, and you've earned the right to know how. The last time humans built Signal monitoring equipment, the Voidborn destroyed it and classified the results. Anything that looks like a listening post is an invitation for the Collective to shut it down. So the first investigators didn't build a listening post. First Step sits next to the Colony Debris Field — thousands of tons of unprocessed wreckage. A Hull Plating Foundry is a completely unremarkable addition to a station next to a debris field. Every mining outpost in the Rim has one. The Voidborn haven't looked at it twice. But its sensor calibration systems are lined with exotic crystals tuned to the Signal's frequency bands, and the environmental monitoring equipment works as a passive receiver. No antenna arrays, no suspicious power draws. Just a factory with very precise sensors. It has been running a long time, and a lining tuned that finely drifts. The engineers at Starfall Salvage Station drew the original blueprints, and they've prepared a revision set to correct for it. That is not something we can have sent over an open channel. Go to Starfall, consult with their engineers, and carry the revisions back.

Complete

The revision set checks out. Starfall's engineers handed it over without a second glance — to them it's a routine maintenance package for a foundry the Outer Rim development board approved long ago. You should know what that foundry cost. The structure took standard industrial materials — steel, circuits, power cells, titanium. Any station in the Rim could supply them. The calibration lining was another matter. Exotic crystals in that quantity weren't available on any market. They had to be synthesized from deep core exotic matter, which meant miners with deep core extractors working deposits in lawless space. It was more than any single ship or faction could supply. Every pilot following the investigation contributed, and together it was enough. It looks like a factory. It functions as a factory. But underneath the hull plating production line, the calibration arrays are listening — and with these corrections we can tune them back onto the Signal. There's one thing the revisions call for that we don't have. Come and see us about it.
