Unseal It ★★★★

Councillor Bryn will call the Patchwork Council vote to unseal Larkin's report when 25 pilots have confirmed her seven systems and joined the call.

Dialog

Offer

Larkin filed a report when Quade brought her home. The Council sealed it. Some of that was kindness, and some of it was that nobody wanted to pay for an expedition to go and check. It's been sealed ever since. I'll call the vote to unseal it. But the Council won't open thirty years of sealed record on one pilot's say-so. Bring me twenty-five pilots who've seen those seven systems with their own eyes, all on the record at once, and I'll put it to the floor.

Complete

Bryn: "Four votes to unseal. Fenn wanted to know what it costs. I told her we've been paying for forty years." The Council releases the report to the Patchwork's public record. It is short. It was written by a young pilot in a recovery ship's sick bay, and it reads like it. "Report of S. Larkin, Pathfinder. Seven stars on a line past the Turnback marker. I have their positions. None has a planet. None has a belt. I found no comets and almost no gas between them. The stars are old, and old stars keep things. These kept nothing. I do not know what took it. I looked twice. I would have looked a third time if the drive had held. Recommend a full survey with a ship that can come back."
