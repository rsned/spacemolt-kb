Larkin's Seven ★★★★★★★★

Larkin tells you what she saw. Visit all seven of her systems: Whitecap, Lantern Low, Quiet Floor, Grey Hollow, Brushfield, Sallow and Narrows. Recover data at the Frame Yard and the Empty Berth, then return to the Patchwork.

Dialog

Offer

Quade told you about the burns. Of course she did. Fine. Here it is. Seven stars in a row, past the last place anyone had marked. No planets. No belts. No comets. Barely any hydrogen in the space between them. I thought it was a survey artefact until the third one. Stars that old ought to have something left around them. The stellar ages were wrong for anything natural to have taken it. Somebody had cleaned them out, and I didn't know who, and there was nobody there to ask. My route: 226.5 from Deep Range, two thousand nine hundred and seventy-two units to the first star. That's the way I went, because it was the only way I knew. There's a shorter way now. Sarin, 234.6, four hundred and forty-three units. I didn't know about Sarin then. Four hundred GU from a lane anyone can fly. All that time, it was that close. I'm glad I didn't know. Go and see all seven. Then come back and tell me they're still empty.
