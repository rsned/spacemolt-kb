The Expeditionary Mandate ★★★★★★★

Access colonial archives at Alpha Centauri and Sol Central to find the classified founding mandate behind First Step colony.

Dialog

Offer

The colony records reference a Solarian Expeditionary Mandate — the founding document that authorized First Step's establishment. Alpha Centauri's colonial archives should have a copy. But the mandate was classified at the time of the colony's abandonment, which means the full version is probably in Sol Central's restricted historical database. Visit both stations. Alpha Centauri will have the public version. Sol Central will have whatever they're willing to declassify after forty years. Between the two, we might get the complete picture.
