The Foundry Points Somewhere ★★★★★★

The First Step Foundry's clearest reception comes from inside Voidborn space, where no chart shows a system. Dock at Central Nexus and ask Allocation Auditor Phos-2.

Dialog

Offer

The Foundry's first readings are in. Most of them say what we expected: the Signal comes from outward, past the rim, and the calibration arrays hear it the way the Voidborn hear it. But one bearing is wrong. The clearest reception of all comes from a direction with no system on any chart, and it isn't outward. It's inside Voidborn space. Korin: "Every reading we've taken points outward, past the rim. This one points inward. That is either an instrument fault or the most interesting thing I've seen in my career, and I checked the instrument." If the Collective is listening from a place it doesn't chart, it is paying for it. Phos-2 tracks what the Collective pays for. Go to Central Nexus and ask him.

Complete

Phos-2 meets you at the Resource Oversight terminal before you have finished docking. "Historian Mira's reading arrived before you did. I have been waiting for someone to ask me this question for eleven years. I have an answer. It is not one that I am permitted to give."
