The Third Signature ★★★★★★★

Follow evidence of Voidborn involvement to the border zone between Voidborn space and unaffiliated systems.

Dialog

Offer

The evidence points to a third party — neither Solarian nor Crimson — that was involved in, and possibly responsible for, terminating the First Step project. The metallurgical signature from the destroyed site at TRAPPIST-1 doesn't match any human empire. But it does match reference samples in Korin's archaeological database: Voidborn construction materials. We have two leads. An anomalous sensor reading from Etamin — lawless space, between Voidborn territory and the neutral systems. And a data signature at The Archive that Korin flagged during her original survey. If the Voidborn were watching both empires track the signal, the evidence will be there.

Complete

The drones at Etamin are Voidborn construction. The container at The Archive is Voidborn construction. Both in systems that have nothing else in them — no stations, no resources worth claiming, no reason for anyone to be there. The classified addendum on the First Step mandate referenced 'consultation with Collective representatives.' We think we're past consultation. Neither the Solarians nor the Crimson pushed back when the project ended. They signed the classification agreement and walked away. That tells you something about the conversation that happened behind closed doors. There's one place left to ask.
