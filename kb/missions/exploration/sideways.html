Sideways ★★★★★

With a survey scanner fitted, launch a Pathfinder drift from The Telescope on bearing 356 and cross the Starfall - Void Gate lane at its midpoint.

Dialog

Offer

Here's my theory, and it's either brilliant or it's going to get you lost. If a lane is made of something, you can measure it. But you can't measure it by flying along it. That's like trying to measure a river by floating down it. You cross it. Sideways. So: get a survey scanner on your ship. Any survey scanner will do, including that deep core one if you still have it from my extractor days. Fly to The Telescope. Plot a Pathfinder bearing of 356 degrees. That line takes you straight across the middle of the Starfall to Void Gate lane, nearly side-on, and nothing else is close enough to pull you out of the drift before you get there.

Complete

There it is. Right in the middle of the lane, your scanner lit up with the same trace as the glass on my bench. Same bend. The lane is full of it. Or the lane IS it. I don't know which yet and I don't like either answer. One lane could be a coincidence. I need more.
