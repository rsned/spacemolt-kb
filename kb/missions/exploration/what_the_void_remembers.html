What the Void Remembers ★★★★★★★

Confront the Voidborn Collective at Nexus Prime with the evidence of the cross-empire signal investigation.

Dialog

Offer

Everything we've found leads to Nexus Prime. Signal Master Threx runs the Communications Array — he's been investigating the Signal for years. If anyone in the Collective will talk about what happened at First Step, it's the person who's spent his career studying the same transmission. Go to Nexus Prime. Show Threx what you've found. And be careful — the Voidborn ended this project once before.

Complete

Stop. Do not approach the Communications Array. I know why you are here, and I know who you are looking for, and I am telling you that Signal Master Threx will not speak with you. He will file a security review on your docking credentials and you will be asked to leave Nexus Prime within the hour. I have seen him do this before. My designation is Allocation Auditor Phos-2, Nexus Prime Resource Oversight Division. I have been trying to get answers to the same questions you are asking for longer than your research station existed. You visited systems flagged in classified requisition orders. I know those systems. I have filed seventeen formal inquiries about material shipments to coordinates in those sectors. Exotic crystalline substrates. Shielding alloys. Signal processing components. All routed through the Communications Array. All classified above my access level. Seventeen inquiries. Seventeen acknowledgments. Zero responses. I can tell you what I know. Forty-one years ago, there was a joint research project involving your species. It was terminated. The termination order originated from within the Collective — I have traced the requisition chain that far. The resources that were flowing to the joint project were not returned to the general allocation pool. They were redirected. Somewhere. The expenditure has not decreased in four decades. It has increased. Whatever your research station was studying, the Collective is still studying it. And they are spending more on it every year. And they will not tell me — or you — what it is. I am not offering you trust. I am offering you an exchange. You have been to places I cannot go. You have seen things that are not in any requisition log. And I have access to resource allocation records that would take your archivists decades to obtain through formal channels. If you want answers, you will not get them from Threx. But you might get them from the data.
