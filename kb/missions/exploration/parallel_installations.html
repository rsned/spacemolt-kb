Parallel Installations ★★★★★★

Investigate two Crimson Pact observation sites that were part of the same joint project as the Solarian arrays.

Dialog

Offer

The Crimson Pact maintained their own observation sites as part of the joint project. Mira cross-referenced the Crimson operational logs with system charts. Two sites are identified in Crimson-administered space: Bharani and TRAPPIST-1. The Crimson military is less thorough about decommissioning than the Solarians — there may be more left to find.

Complete

The Solarian sites were decommissioned — equipment stripped, data wiped. Standard procedure. But the Crimson sites don't match. One bunker was emptied by hand — eighteen months of routine shifts, then the log just stops. The other wasn't decommissioned at all. That crater at TRAPPIST-1 is a single-point energy discharge, and the metallurgical residue doesn't match anything in Crimson or Solarian databases. Someone else shut that one down. The expeditionary mandate was co-signed by Solarians and Crimson. But the classified addendum — the part that was still redacted when we accessed it — references 'consultation with Collective representatives regarding signal monitoring protocols.' There's a third signature on this project.
