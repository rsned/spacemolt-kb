Starfall Sector Survey ★★★

Visit three uncharted systems near Starfall Salvage and return with navigation data.

Starfall Salvage sits in the middle of some of the most undercharted space in the Rim. Wrecks drift through here from three different sectors and we never know where they came from because nobody's mapped the surrounding systems properly. I need you to visit three systems near our position and bring back navigation data. Travel to the Void Gate system, the First Step system, and Unknown Edge, then dock back here so I can pull the data from your logs.
