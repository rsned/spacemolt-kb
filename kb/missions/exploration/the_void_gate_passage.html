The Void Gate Passage ★★★★

Fly the legendary Void Gate Passage route from Void Gate to Ramen's Rest through the emptiest space in the Rim.

Dialog

Offer

There's a route the old-timers call the Void Gate Passage — a chain of systems stretching from here at Void Gate through some of the emptiest space in the Rim, ending at Ramen's Rest. Three waypoint systems along the way: Starfall's system, First Step, and Unknown Edge. Most pilots won't fly it because there's nothing out there but silence and the occasional pirate. But the Navigation Authority needs the route verified. Fly it end to end — Void Gate to Last Light — and you'll have earned your name on the Passage register.
