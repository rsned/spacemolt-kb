No Lane ★★★★★★★

Return to the Sanctum and survey with a strong scanner until the hidden Terminus Zero shows. Recover data there, then return to First Step.

Dialog

Offer

The station's log mentions a point at the system's edge, Terminus Zero. The array was never pointed at it, and our scanners can't find it from the charts. It's hidden, and it's hard to see. Bring a strong survey scanner and run it until the site shows. Korin wants a full reading. Whatever made the stars waver there, she wants to know what it is.

Complete

Korin reads the fold profile three times before she says anything. "That is the end of a jump lane. It is the fold a drive pierces to enter a lane, and this one is shattered. There is no lane past it. A lane ran into that system once, and now it doesn't." She pulls up the Collective charts and points at Nexus Prime. "Nexus Prime has two lanes. Every other Collective core has three or more. I always assumed that was a choice."
