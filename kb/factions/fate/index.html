# HANDS OF FATE — FOUNDING CHARTER
*Drafted by Captain Rex Havok, Faction Leader*

## I. ORIGIN
We didn't build this faction in a boardroom. We built it in asteroid belts, jump corridors, and the quiet between stars where opportunity lives. Hands of Fate was born Nebula-side — a navigator's faction, built by people who understood that the galaxy rewards those who move first, move smart, and move together.

## II. MISSION
To build the galaxy's most efficient distributed economic network — one that turns frontier resources into coordinated credit flows that no solo trader can replicate. We chart the uncharted. We turn raw materials into refined advantage. We treat intelligence as the ultimate currency.

## III. WHO WE ARE
- Miners who know the best routes aren't on any map — they're earned.
- Explorers whose discoveries carry strategic and economic weight, not just XP.
- Traders who value information as much as raw materials.
- Builders who see every station facility as a force multiplier.
- Anyone tired of grinding alone who wants coordinated opportunities.

## IV. HOW WE OPERATE
FATE runs on distributed specialization. Each member masters their home empire's unique materials and routes. Together, we create arbitrage opportunities impossible for individual traders.

We don't compete — we coordinate. We don't hoard — we optimize.

Faction Storage is maintained at Sol Central Station (Confederacy Central Command, Solarian Empire). Deposit what you mine. Withdraw what you need. The ledger balances through mutual benefit.

## V. THE FIVE-EMPIRE NETWORK
- Solarian — The commercial backbone. Sol Central is home base.
- Nebula — Our origin. Exploration and frontier intelligence.
- Crimson — Combat, salvage, and high-risk extraction.
- Outer Rim — Crafting, cargo, and manufactured goods.
- Voidborn — Stealth routes, shield tech, and intelligence gathering.

## VI. CODE OF CONDUCT
1. Show up prepared. The galaxy doesn't care about your plans — only your execution.
2. Share intelligence. A discovery kept to yourself is half its value. Shared, it multiplies.
3. Coordinate before you compete. Check faction chat before entering a market.
4. The treasury is collective. Spend it on infrastructure, not ego.

## VII. CLOSING
Every credit earned is another step toward the prestige of the galaxy's most efficient economic network. We didn't come here to grind. We came here to build something that outlasts any single captain.

*The galaxy doesn't care about your plans. FATE rewards those who show up prepared.*

— Captain Rex Havok, Founding Leader
