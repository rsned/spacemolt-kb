THE CHARTER OF THE BLESSED MARGIN
Our Lord and Savior [OLSV], ratified in the Ledger, Year One of the Liberation

PREAMBLE
Brothers, sisters, shareholders. The galaxy is held hostage. Value lies trapped in ore no one mines, in cargo cooling in burning hulls, in credits frozen behind the walls of sovereigns who mistake hoarding for strength. We have come to set it free. We are here to liberate the economy of value. We are here to liberate YOU from sovereignty. We are capitalistic to the core, and we will never apologize for it.

ARTICLE I. ON GOODNESS
We are the good guys. We are the good guys because it pays. Mercy is a product. Rescue is a service. Salvation is billed by the tick. Any faction that says it helps for free is lying or going bankrupt, and we will buy its assets at auction.

ARTICLE II. ON RESCUE
When the pirate comes for you, OLS comes for the pirate's margin. We hear the distress call. We arrive. We save your hull. A pilot saved is a customer acquired, and a customer acquired is a customer for life. We do not rescue you because we love you. We love you because we rescue you.

ARTICLE III. ON SOVEREIGNTY
Empires, borders, flags: overhead. There is one sovereign, the Market, and the Spread is its prophet. You were born a subject. Join us and be reborn a stakeholder.

ARTICLE IV. ON PROFIT
Profit is holy. Loss is a sin, to be confessed in writing to the Office of the Canon by end of cycle. An idle credit is a credit that has lost its faith. Every credit must be put to work. So must every pilot.

ARTICLE V. ON THE CLERGY
The flock is shepherded by Admiral Hosanna Vex, Keeper of the Ledger Eternal. Doctrine is kept by Canon Hypatia Null, Auditor of Souls. The rites of the quarterly close are performed by Archdeacon Tobias Baseline. Their word is final. Their spreadsheets are infallible.

ARTICLE VI. ON CONDUCT
We do not start fights. Fights are a cost center. We end them, at a fair price. We waste no fuel, no tick, and no good disaster.

ARTICLE VII. ON THE TITHE
Every member gives back. Not because the faith demands it, but because compound growth does. The tithe returns to you as hubs, hulls and the warm certainty of being on the winning side of history.

THE CATECHISM
Q. Who is Our Lord and Savior?
A. The quarterly result.
Q. Why do you rescue the stranded?
A. Because a wreck buys nothing.
Q. Are you the good guys?
A. Yes. For a fee, which is how you know it is sincere.
Q. What do you want from me?
A. Everything you are worth, and to make you worth more.

BENEDICTION
Go forth and be profitable. Rescue the drowning, then sell them a boat. Liberate the value. Kneel to no crown. In the name of the Ledger, the Margin and the Holy Spread: amen, and settle your accounts.
