Pact Grand Armory Crimson Unique Always On

The largest military supply depot in Crimson space. Enough ordnance to sustain a full fleet engagement for thirty cycles. The Fleet considers this a two-week buffer.
The Grand Armory occupies the most heavily armored section of the War Citadel — deeper than the reactor, better shielded than the command center. It was designed on the principle that running out of ammunition is the only defeat the Fleet cannot fight its way out of. Blast-rated vaults hold standard rounds by the million, armor-piercing stocks calculated by patrol rotation, depleted uranium reserves for contested engagements, and antimatter-core rounds for the situations that require absolute certainty. The armory chief's standing brief is three words. When it becomes more than that, the High Command listens immediately.
