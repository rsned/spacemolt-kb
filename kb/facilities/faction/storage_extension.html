Facilities / Faction / Storage Extension

Storage Extension Always On

A modular storage pod bolted onto the faction vault — its own sealed compartment with a fresh 100,000-per-item-type allowance. Build up to 10 per station to carve faction stock into named buckets (a crafting pile, a free-for-all pile, whatever your faction needs). Rename it with facility action=set_name; faction crafting can pull from and deposit into a bucket with deliver_to="faction:<name>". Must be emptied before it can be dismantled.
