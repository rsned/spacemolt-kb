How Far Is Each Empire From a Stronghold?

The five empire capitals sit deep inside friendly territory. A breadth-first search over the jump-gate network measures how many hops separate each empire from the galaxy's nine neutral strongholds.

Strongholds are neutral — all nine belong to no empire. Each empire's capital is buried in the middle of its own systems, so the empire's fringe is always closer to a stronghold than its capital is. Distance below is the minimum number of jump-gate traversals to the nearest of any stronghold.

🏛️ The Nine Strongholds (all neutral)

Algol, Alhena, Barnard 44, Bellatrix, Gliese 581, GSC-0008, Sheratan, Xamidimura, Zaniah.

Eight of the nine host a station; only gsc_0008 does not — yet it is still the nearest stronghold to the entire Crimson Pact.

Analysis Notes

  • Capitals are central: in every empire the capital is farther from a stronghold than the empire's own fringe station — confirming capitals sit in the interior, not on the border.
  • Nebula is the most stronghold-adjacent empire: its Factory Belt and Treasure Cache stations are just 5 hops from Alhena, the shortest empire-to-stronghold reach in the galaxy.
  • Crimson is the most isolated: even The Rampart, its nearest station to a stronghold, is 9 hops out, and the whole Pact routes to the stationless GSC-0008.
  • Algol is the busiest gateway: it is the nearest stronghold for Solarian and Outer Rim capitals and both empires' fringe stations.
  • Outer Rim's capital roams: "Frontier" is used here as the Outer Rim hub — its Mobile Capital station relocates daily, so that row can drift.

Data Source

Topology, empire labels, and stronghold flags come from the galaxy map snapshot (get_map.json, 505 systems); station locations come from the knowledge database pois table (type = 'station', 42 systems). Capitals are identified from each system's description text (Krynn, Haven, Sol, Nexus Prime); Outer Rim has no fixed capital, so its roaming hub Frontier is used. Distance is the minimum number of jump-gate traversals to the nearest stronghold via a multi-source breadth-first search.
