Warp Flight Simulator

Pick any origin and destination and fly the Pathfinder route in first person. Stars are colored by spectral class; the translucent spheres are each system's 100 GU capture bubble. If the route is blocked you'll drop out at the first bubble you hit. The address bar updates with your route — copy it to share a link.

Pick two systems and press Fly.
