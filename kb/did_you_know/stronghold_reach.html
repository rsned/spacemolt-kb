Reach of the Nine Strongholds

Nobody in this galaxy lives very far from a pirate stronghold. A multi-source breadth-first search over the jump-gate network measures every system's distance to the nearest of the nine, then grows that reach one jump at a time.

14 jumps
is all it takes to reach every one of the 505 known systems from the nearest stronghold

Red territory marks systems within the selected number of jumps. Dim stars are out of reach at this radius. The nine strongholds are the bright red dots.

Analysis Notes

  • Nine blobs become one. Each patch of reach always contains a stronghold, so the count can only fall: 9 at 1, 8 at 4, 6 at 6, 4 at 7, 2 at 8, a single blob at 9.
  • Algol dominates. It is the nearest stronghold for 142 systems, far more than any other.
  • The last holdouts. 82 Eridani, Blackthorn, GSC-0036, Muscida, Windmere sit 14 jumps out — the deepest anyone in this galaxy gets from pirate territory.

Data Source

System positions, jump-gate connections, and stronghold flags come from the knowledge database (505 systems, 1065 jump gates). The nine strongholds are the systems flagged is_stronghold; all nine are neutral, with no empire and no police presence. Distance is the minimum number of jump-gate traversals to the nearest stronghold via a multi-source breadth-first search.
