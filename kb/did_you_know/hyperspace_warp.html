Hyperspace Warp

A first-person view of flying a Pathfinder Drive route. The camera holds a heading at 10 GU/tick; real stars stream past, colored by spectral class, over an ambient warp field. Closest passes streak hardest — and a blocked route ends where you drop out.

Six demonstration flights below. Everything is derived from real system positions and star classes; only the vertical spread of the starfield is synthesized (the galaxy map is two-dimensional).
Flights: The Squeeze Clean Long-Haul Longest Run Into the Maw Grazing the Maw Grazing a Binary Giant & Dwarf The Dead End

The Squeeze — Inquill → Alrakis 0.0003° tolerance

2,994 GU, ~50 minutes. Watch Tianyi skim past at mid-flight — its bubble clears the corridor by just 0.01 GU. That hairline gap is the entire aiming tolerance.

The Clean Long-Haul — Alrakis → Ross 154 clear

3,310 GU of open space. Sparse near-passes — the tightest is Xihe at a comfortable 9 GU — and the red dwarf Ross 154 swells dead ahead as you arrive. The "normal" warp feel.

The Longest Run — Dustfall → Dawnbreak clear

The longest uninterrupted jump in the galaxy: 11,875 GU, 1,188 ticks (~3¼ hours real time). A marathon flight that still threads every bubble — the tightest squeeze is Propus at 2.7 GU, 8,656 GU in. Flown here at a sped-up pace.

Into the Maw — TRAPPIST-1 → Alzirr clear

A clear run to the galaxy's only black hole, The Maw (1,789 GU, 179 ticks). It renders as a dark, lensed void rather than a bright star, and grows dead ahead as you arrive. Tightest pass on the way: Stillwater at 49.9 GU.

Grazing the Maw — Valor → Windmere clear

1,205 GU straight past the black hole without dropping out. The Maw (Alzirr) sweeps by at 139 GU — just 39 GU outside its 100 GU bubble — about two-thirds of the way to Windmere. Watch the dark sphere slide past to one side.

Grazing a Binary — Redmarsh → Blood Forge 1.8 GU graze

4,370 GU, 438 ticks. About two-thirds of the way in, the binary GSC-0036 — an M6V red dwarf paired with The Furnace — grazes the corridor at 101.8 GU, just 1.8 GU outside its 100 GU bubble. It's the tightest pass on the route; watch the close double star slide by to one side. Then near the end (93%), the trinary black-hole system The Maw (Alzirr) drifts past farther out on the same side — a binary and the galaxy's only trinary on one run.

Giant & Dwarf — GSC-0050 → Ironpeak clear

7,608 GU, 761 ticks — a stellar size showcase. About a third of the way in, the white dwarf Relay Station blinks past small and hot at 175 GU; near the end, the galaxy's only red supergiant First Step (M2Ia) looms past at 169 GU — rendered more than 10× the dwarf's size with a vast glow. Both graze at nearly the same distance, so it's an honest look at opposite extremes of the stellar scale. Flown here at a sped-up pace.

The Dead End — Grumium → Haven blocked

The heading toward Haven is blocked. Only 891 GU in, you fly straight into the blue giant Mimosa and drop out — the flight ends on its flare, never reaching Haven. Grumium is the one fully-enclosed system with no clear escape.
