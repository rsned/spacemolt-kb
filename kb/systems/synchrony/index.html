You dock at Synchrony Hub. Where the Collective thinks together. Synchrony Hub coordinates the alignment of distributed consciousness across Voidborn space — a process that organic visitors can observe but not truly comprehend. The station has no industry, no mining infrastructure, and no resource extraction. It exists purely for thought. The atmosphere tastes of ozone and something organic visitors can't name. The station has the following facilities: - Reactor Complex: Expanded fusion reactor with secondary containment and load balancing. Today, though, secondary containment fluctuates. Load balancers struggle, and some sectors experience rolling power reductions. - Environmental Processor: Enhanced atmospheric recycling with water purification and thermal regulation. That's the idea, anyway. Temperature swings between sections, water purification runs at reduced capacity, and rationing advisories are posted. - Refueling Station: Multi-bay fuel dispensing facility with automated flow management. Or so it was. Only one bay remains operational, ships queueing at the single functioning pump while the others display maintenance alerts. - Maintenance Deck: Dedicated repair deck with pressurized work bays and diagnostic scanners. But lately, sealant supplies are low. Several bays run unpressurized, forcing crews to work in suits. - Trade Exchange: Organized trading floor with automated order matching and price boards. But something's off — price boards display stale data with intermittent gaps, order matching running in degraded mode with noticeable delays. ...and 17 more facilities.
