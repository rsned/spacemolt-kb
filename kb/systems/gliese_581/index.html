Isolated Gliese 581 sits at the end of the road, and Korr Fortress is the reason it is on anyone's chart: the sector's largest and oldest pirate station, and the model other strongholds copy. Grand Marshal Korr runs it like a garrison, and the system has the settled, weathered calm of a place that has been held for a very long time.

● Gliese 581 III Planet terran 0.8 (0.8, -0.0) — — Seas, cloud bands, and green continents in plain view of the station, the only world in the system that could have been a home. Nobody has built anything on it.
