Epsilon Eridani

Epsilon Eridani sits under a young orange star still shedding the rubble of its own birth, a junction where ships cross more often than they stop. The charts here are the best in the Confederacy and its customs coverage is the thinnest, a fact that anyone moving cargo quietly has already noticed.
