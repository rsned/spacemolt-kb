Netherwick's untouched Glass Reef scatters starlight through pristine silicon formations, turning its asteroid field into a display of refracted light. Beyond its giant sun the rim falls away into empty black, and the Bellatrix stronghold is close enough that nobody surveys in comfort. Someone has painted "354, 41" across the last marker buoy at the edge, and nobody has painted over it.
