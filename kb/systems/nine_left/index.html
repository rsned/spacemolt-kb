❄ Ledger Ice Ice Field cometary 8.1 (7.2, -3.7) Yes 30 A cometary shelf of water and nitrogen ice, long tails trailing from nuclei that are fewer than their orbits call for. The gaps in the sequence show like missing teeth.
✦ Ledger Buoy Relic derelict 8.4 (7.5, -3.8) — — An Outer Rim survey buoy, its panels scoured pale, still broadcasting the comet catalogue its crew logged when they charted this system. The catalogue lists forty-one bodies on long orbits through the outer dark, each with a name, a period, and a next pass. Scouts who check it against the sky find nine.
