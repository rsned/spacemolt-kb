☀ Avior Star Sun K4V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A K-type dwarf burning a deep amber, steady enough that navigation beacons never bother to correct for flares. Its light turns the silver-bearing belt to brass at a glance.
● Avior I Planet scorched 0.2 (0.2, -0.0) — — Sun-seared basalt crazed with heat fractures, with no atmosphere left to lose. Two survey passes have been logged, and both reached the same verdict: not worth landing.
● Avior II Planet arid 0.4 (-0.0, 0.4) — — Rust-colored dunes ribbed by winds that have nothing to carry but dust. Nobody has staked a claim here, and the only tracks on the surface are the ones the wind keeps erasing.
◈ Avior Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 0.7 (-0.6, 0.5) — — No drill scar, no claim beacon, no cut face: this belt has never met a miner. The first sweep reports a silver content so high that crews run it a second time, expecting an error, and the deposits run deep enough to feed a crafting operation for a long time.
☁ Avior Gas Pocket Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 1.0 (-1.0, -0.2) — — A cold knot of molecular cloud, thick enough with hydrogen and argon to dim running lights, with ion currents threading through it as faint violet seams. Pilot-whale pods string out nose-to-fluke through the murk, grazing the argon banks.
❄ Avior Cryobelt Ice Field kuiper 8.1 (5.9, 5.5) — — A wide scatter of water and nitrogen ice, bright where fresh faces have cracked and dull everywhere else. No refinery waits for it in lawless space, so haulers cut their own fuel and leave.
