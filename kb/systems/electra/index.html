Electra holds the Confederacy's far edge under a blue-white giant so bright that frontier chart-makers use it as a fixed lamp for the lanes around it. The customs cutters rotated in here treat it as a posting to be served, and the planets are cooked too hard to give the crews anything to do on their days off.
