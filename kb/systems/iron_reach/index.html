✦ Ruby's Vigil Relic — 6.0 (5.5, 2.5) — — A fused autonomous sentinel embedded in the surface of a lone asteroid at the fringe of Iron Reach's mineral debris corridor. Originally dismissed as navigation interference, it became notable after multiple ore convoys avoided catastrophic debris cascades moments after receiving unidentified proximity warnings — signal origin traced back here every time. The Crimson Pact attempted removal twice. Both salvage crews reported unexplained targeting failures and collision incidents during approach. After losing two industrial cutters, Pact command declared the asteroid strategically unnecessary and marked the region restricted. Outer Rim pilots adopted it as an informal navigation reference. Crimson ore ships quietly adjust their routes near it. Explorers leave hull markings or small red signal beacons on passing. Prospectors claim sensor clarity briefly improves within a few kilometers. Starfall salvagers stage runs nearby despite Pact objections. Frontier chatter calls it "the rock that watches back."
