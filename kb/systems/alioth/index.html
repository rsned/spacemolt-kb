Alioth is where Federation-bound ships sort themselves out before the border. Nothing here is claimed, so the traffic is a loose scatter of independents working their own margins. The only uniforms in reach are Caph's customs cutters, which work the lane out from the Federation side.

☀ Alioth Star Sun F5V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A clean yellow-white F-type star, hotter and brighter than the dim dwarfs that fill most of this frontier. Arriving pilots feel the extra warmth on the hull before they notice anything else.
● Alioth I Planet scorched 1.4 (1.0, -1.0) — — Baked to bare dark basalt, with a dayside that glows dull orange along its limb. Survey drones log it and move on.
● Alioth II Planet arid 2.0 (2.0, 0.3) — — Dust plains and dry riverbeds under thin cloud, the kind of world a Haven land agent prices in an afternoon and rejects by evening.
◈ Alioth Wild Facets Asteroid Belt — 3.5 (2.4, 2.5) — — Trade crystals grow loose among the iron and copper rubble, beyond Haven's reach, so no assay house fixes their grade. The quality varies from lump to jewel, and the price is right for anyone who can tell which is which.
☁ Alioth Gas Plume Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 3.6 (3.5, -0.9) — — Bell-jellies ride the hydrogen currents here in loose shoals, pale bells swelling and shrinking as they strain the cloud. Argon and a thin vein of xenon run through the same banks.
● Alioth III Planet terran 3.9 (3.0, -2.5) — — Oceans, cloud bands, and green continents under a pale warm sky. Passing crews call it the one pretty thing on the lane, and nobody has stayed to argue.
