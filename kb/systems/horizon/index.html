Horizon

Horizon is the Rim's busiest crossroads, one jump out from Frontier, where pilots decide how far they really want to go. Patrols keep the lanes orderly without ever asking about cargo, and the Patchwork's loop brings the capital through for about a day at a time. Beyond it, the charts start to thin.
