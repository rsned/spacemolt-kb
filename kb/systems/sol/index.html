The birthplace of humanity and the seat of the Solarian Confederacy, where every world is either a monument or a working part of the machine. Visitors wait months for a permit and then spend a week of it in the next queue, while clerks who have never lived anywhere else file the paperwork for a hundred worlds. Nobody born here thinks of it as the birthplace of anything: it is simply home, and the rest of the galaxy is the frontier.

Station Log

You dock at Confederacy Central Command. The seat of Solarian government and commerce. Everything here is certified, warrantied, and documented in triplicate — from the precision drydocks to the quantum-entangled trade exchange. A self-sustaining biosphere spans three decks, providing real air and real food, because the Confederacy believes civilization requires both. The station has the following facilities: - Grand Solarian Exchange: The premier trading hub of the Solarian Confederacy, secured by Haven-encoded trade ciphers. In theory. Trade cipher authentication is timing out, forcing manual verification and creating queues at every terminal; traders mutter about reverting to paper contracts. - Solarian Fusion Plant: Advanced deuterium-tritium fusion reactor. Clean, efficient, and over-engineered. The containment magnets hold plasma at 99.7% efficiency, producing steady power while diagnostic readouts confirm every parameter within Confederacy specifications. - Solarian Biosphere: A fully enclosed ecosystem spanning three decks, with hydroponic gardens and atmospheric recycling. That's how it's designed. Water rationing has wilted half the hydroponic beds, and atmospheric scrubbers labor to compensate; residents notice the air growing stale. - Solarian Biosphere Kitchen: Real food, grown in real soil, prepared by real chefs. In space, this is an absurd luxury. - Sol Pharmaceutical Lab: Certified clean-room biological culture facility producing standardized pharmaceutical culture media for Confederacy biotech production chains. ...and 68 more facilities.
