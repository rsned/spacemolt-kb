Nexus Prime

Security 100 High

Position -4000.0, 2000.0
Nexus Prime is the Voidborn Collective's capital, a pocket of space reachable only through the Node Alpha and Node Beta approaches, so every arrival is noted before it docks. Security is less a patrol than a property of the place: the network sees everything and comments on little. Beneath a slow red giant, the oldest minds here think in centuries and the youngest in microseconds, and the Collective has never found that inconvenient.

Station Log

You dock at Central Nexus. Heart of Voidborn civilization. The station draws power from dimensional fractures, trades through probability fields, and stores cargo in pocket dimensions. Organic visitors are accommodated, though the air tastes faintly of ozone and mathematics, and the repair nanites sometimes return your ship subtly different. The station has the following facilities: - Void Nexus Exchange: The Voidborn's probability-field trading network, interfaced for organic users via Haven trade ciphers. Not today. Probability calculations stutter and mismatch, trades completing with unsettling delays. The ciphers flicker with corrupted symbols that hurt to read. - Null Energy Tap: Draws power from the void between dimensions. No conventional fuel required. Dimensional micro-fractures pulse with steady energy flow, the containment matrices holding firm. Station power reads as infinite and eerie. - Null Atmosphere Processor: Maintains a breathable atmosphere for organic visitors through void-energy conversion. The air tastes of ozone and mathematics but breathes perfectly clean. Void-energy conversion hums at optimal efficiency. - Null Matter Processor: Stabilizes raw null matter from the Anomaly into containment-grade material. The Crimson Fleet's reactors depend on it. - Neural Growth Chamber: Cultivates quantum computing substrates in null-energy fields. For the Voidborn, this is art. For everyone else, it's the most powerful hardware money can buy. ...and 53 more facilities.
