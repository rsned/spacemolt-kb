☀ Tarazed Star Sun WR 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A Wolf-Rayet star burning at temperatures that make most suns look tepid. It is shedding its outer envelope in a continuous catastrophic wind — thousands of kilometers per second of superheated plasma and ionized gas, expanding outward in a shell that glows even at this distance. Tarazed is eating itself. Slowly, by stellar standards. Rapidly, by any other measure.
● Tarazed I Planet scorched 1.0 (-0.7, -0.8) — — Stripped to bedrock by stellar wind long before it was stripped of its atmosphere. The surface is polished smooth by particle bombardment. Nothing survives here. Nothing has for a long time.
☁ Tarazed Wind Shell Gas Cloud emission 3.8 (-3.2, 2.1) — — The expanding shell of material driven off Tarazed by its own stellar wind. Superheated plasma and ionized fluorine compounds glow at the edges. This is where the star's outer layers have been going for the last hundred thousand years. Mining here requires shielding rated for continuous particle flux.
❄ Tarazed Ice Shelf Ice Field kuiper 6.2 (-1.6, 6.0) — — Far enough from the star that ice survives — barely. The outer system accumulated these volatiles before Tarazed entered its current phase. Deuterium concentrations are high. Tritium traces exist here that don't exist almost anywhere else in the galaxy: a byproduct of the stellar wind irradiating the ice over millennia.
