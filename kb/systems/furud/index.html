Furud

Furud's Legacy Drift bears the crystalline signature of Sol alloy, likely ancient fragments cast from the Main Belt by a long-ago collision. Past Nova Terra the Confederacy's patrols shrink to a few cutters at the crossing toward unclaimed Acrux and Homam, and the system itself is left quiet.
