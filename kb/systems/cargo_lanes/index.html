The station has the following facilities: - Reactor Complex: Expanded fusion reactor with secondary containment and load balancing. Primary and secondary containment fields hold steady. Load balancers distribute power evenly across all station sectors. - Environmental Processor: Enhanced atmospheric recycling with water purification and thermal regulation. That's the idea, anyway. Temperature swings between sections, water purification runs at reduced capacity, and rationing advisories are posted. - Refueling Station: Multi-bay fuel dispensing facility with automated flow management. Multiple bays operate simultaneously, automated flow controllers balancing supply across docking ports. - Maintenance Deck: Dedicated repair deck with pressurized work bays and diagnostic scanners. Pressurized bays hiss with atmosphere. Diagnostic scanners sweep arriving ships, queuing repairs efficiently. - Trade Exchange: Organized trading floor with automated order matching and price boards. But something's off — price boards display stale data with intermittent gaps, order matching running in degraded mode with noticeable delays. ...and 19 more facilities.
