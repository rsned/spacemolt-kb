Redmarsh

Security 0 Lawless

Redmarsh is a starless sprawl of mineral-bearing debris and diffuse gas, stained rust-red in the combined light of distant suns. Surveyors kept it on the map because its broad fields made a useful frontier landmark and a practical stop along the outer lanes.
