Distant Light

Security 55 Medium
Empire Outerrim
Position -2925.7, -2903.4
Distant Light is a survey waypoint on the quiet side of the Rim, where a pilot can go hours without hearing another ship on comms. Surveyors still hang their charts from its steady yellow star, and a pilot in trouble has little help close at hand. The Patchwork's loop touches it for about a day, and then the quiet closes back up.

☀ Rigel Minor Star Sun G5V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A plain yellow dwarf, the kind surveyors hang their charts from because it never does anything unexpected. The waypoint sits around it as a fixed point.
● Rigel Minor I Planet arid 1.0 (1.0, 0.3) — — Dry rock held close to the star and baked on a steady schedule. Surveys logged it, shrugged, and moved on.
● Rigel Minor II Planet tundra 2.0 (2.0, 0.3) — — Frost-heaved tundra under a pale sun, the outer of the two worlds anyone has bothered to chart here. Old survey markers stand on the ridges, and the snow is slowly burying them.
○ Superposition Pocket Nebula — 2.5 (2.1, -1.4) — — A dense pocket where quantum fragments sit in superposition until a mining laser disturbs them, so no two extractions come out quite alike. Iron and copper settle in the same dust, and quantum-moths trail their thread through it, strung in several places at once.
