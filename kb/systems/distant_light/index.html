Distant Light

Security 55 Medium
Empire Outerrim
Position -2925.7, -2903.4
Distant Light is a survey waypoint on the quiet side of the Rim, where a pilot can go hours without hearing another ship on comms. Surveyors still hang their charts from its steady yellow star, and a pilot in trouble has little help close at hand. The Patchwork's loop touches it for about a day, and then the quiet closes back up.

☀ Rigel Minor Star Sun G5V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A plain yellow dwarf, the kind surveyors hang their charts from because it never does anything unexpected. The waypoint sits around it as a fixed point.
● Rigel Minor I Planet arid 0.8 (-0.4, -0.6) — — Dry rock held close to the star and baked on a steady schedule. Surveys logged it, shrugged, and moved on.
⬡ Mobile Capital Station — 1.2 (0.3, -1.2) — — The ever-moving heart of the Explorers — though 'station' flatters it. Locals call it the Patchwork: three captured destroyers and a stolen freighter welded into something that flies, fights, and leaks in roughly equal measure. Nothing aboard matches, the running lights are scavenged from a dozen different hulls, and the docking bay is open to anyone who doesn't start trouble. For the Outer Rim, that's as close to a capital as ideology allows.
● Rigel Minor II Planet tundra 1.4 (-1.4, 0.0) — — Frost-heaved tundra under a pale sun, the outer of the two worlds anyone has bothered to chart here. Old survey markers stand on the ridges, and the snow is slowly burying them.
○ Superposition Pocket Nebula — 1.8 (1.5, -1.0) — — A dense pocket where quantum fragments sit in superposition until a mining laser disturbs them, so no two extractions come out quite alike. Iron and copper settle in the same dust, and quantum-moths trail their thread through it, strung in several places at once.
