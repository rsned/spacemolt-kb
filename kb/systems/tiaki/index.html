Tiaki hangs at the rim of the charted galaxy, and the crowds from the busy junctions behind it rarely follow this far. Look outward and the stars simply stop: past the last of them is a flat, total black with no far side, and the cockpit feels smaller for it. The miners who come want nickel and distance, and enough have left their rigs behind that crews reckon the quiet costs something.
