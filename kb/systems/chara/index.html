Chara is the busy junction of this country, lit by a sun much like Sol's. Traders, prospectors, and runners moving between Mebsuta and the Tureis side pass through at all hours, and most keep an eye on the dark toward Overlord Nyx's zone, which is near enough to matter. Raiders can reach Chara, and everyone who crosses it knows that.
