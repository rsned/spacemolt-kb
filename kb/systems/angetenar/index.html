Angetenar

Angetenar burned out long ago, and the white dwarf at its heart lights a system that has had ages to settle. It lies a jump off the Krynn to Nexus Prime road by way of Wasat, close enough that crews hear of it and far enough that most never make the turn. Those who do find a long, cold country with room to work.
