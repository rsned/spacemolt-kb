A wormhole entrance sitting uncomfortably close to the Hadar star. The stellar magnetic field has pinned the aperture in place — a freak alignment that nobody with functioning survival instincts would have discovered on purpose. Sensors can barely distinguish the wormhole signature from the stellar noise. Named by smugglers: you leave the frying pan, and come out next to something worse.
