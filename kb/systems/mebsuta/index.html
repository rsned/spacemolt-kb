Mebsuta

The Crimson-to-Voidborn run goes straight through Mebsuta, and the system has never once been the reason for a trip. Ships from Krynn and Nexus Prime cross in both directions at the lane's pace rather than the system's, and the star has watched every one of them go by without comment.
