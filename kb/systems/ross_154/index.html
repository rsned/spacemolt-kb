Ross 154 is the first unclaimed dark a Solarian crew meets on the way out, and the Confederacy's charts treat it as the edge of the known. Prospectors from the Confederacy's frontier systems come out to work the belts, and a few of them stay. The star is a notorious flare star, and everyone passing through hears about it.
