Blue Glare Uncharted

Security 0 Lawless
Empire Independent
Position ≈ 6896.0, 257.0
An A-type star floods this system with hard ultraviolet, and the inner worlds have been scoured pale by it. Out past the belts the light thins to something a pilot can look at, and the dark beyond feels almost restful.
