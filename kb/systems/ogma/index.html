Ogma is a plain working system on the Nebula-to-Outer-Rim road, where the freight keeps moving and the haulers keep their heads down. Miners here pay their licenses and grumble about them. It is the kind of place the Federation counts on to stay boring.

☀ Ogma Star Sun K3V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A K-type star burning copper-orange, unbothered and unwatched. Nobody has filed a flare report here in years, and the survey office has stopped asking.
● Ogma I Planet arid 0.4 (0.3, -0.3) — — Ochre flats and wind-leaned buttes under a thin, dusty sky, dry from pole to pole. It makes a good landmark and a poor investment.
● Ogma II Planet tundra 0.8 (-0.4, 0.6) — — Frost lies in long streamers across dark, frozen plains, tinted gold by the low sun. In that thin light the whole surface looks like old brass.
◈ Ogma Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 1.6 (-0.3, -1.6) — — Dense, dark rock with veins of vanadium and tungsten running through the carbon, plus aluminum in the paler fragments. Federation-licensed crews work it steadily, and the licence fee is the only part of the job they never have to dig for.
