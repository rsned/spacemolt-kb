Gacrux is the red star at the head of the Southern Cross in old Earth skies, and the name carries more weight than the system does. It sits at the rim, within reach of the Bellatrix stronghold, and few crews see reason to stay. The lanes are quiet and the charts are older than the pilots reading them.
