Thabit

Thabit is a name on the route sheet more than a place: the Krynn-to-Sol and Krynn-to-Frontier runs both pass through it. Crews give it a line in the log and move on, and the sun is quiet enough that there is little more to write. It is lawless, and the pilots who cross it make their own arrangements.
