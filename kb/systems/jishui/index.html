● Jishui I Planet scorched 1.0 (-0.3, -1.0) — — A crust of cooled slag, veined with bright metal along ancient faults. It looks richer than it is, and the survey record agrees.
● Jishui II Planet arid 2.0 (-1.3, -1.6) — — Wind-sculpted sandstone ridges under an orange sky, dry in every season. The wind has nothing to do but move the same dust again.
● Jishui III Planet terran 3.0 (0.8, 2.9) — — Green hills and thin rivers under a low sun, with one cold landing pad on a river terrace where a survey camp once stood. The pad still carries the scorch ring from its last lander.
