☀ Alnilam Star Sun K4V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — Alnilam is Arabic for a string of pearls, a promise this plain orange K-dwarf makes no attempt to keep. The light is steady and unremarkable, bright enough to work by.
● Alnilam I Planet scorched 0.2 (-0.1, -0.1) — — Slag-grey and cratered, baked by a sun it sits too close to. The few prospectors who flew a pass logged it and turned for the belt.
◈ Alnilam Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 0.5 (-0.0, -0.5) — — A loose band of iron and copper rock, spaced wide enough that a ship can work it without watching its flanks. Ordinary ore, ordinary yield, and no prospector's mark on any of it.
☁ Alnilam Gas Pocket Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 0.7 (0.6, -0.3) — — A cold molecular knot of hydrogen, argon, and neon, with traces of xenon and krypton that spark across a scanner like struck flint. Drift-rays hang in the argon banks, slack-winged and indifferent to passing hulls.
❄ Alnilam Frozen Drift Ice Field kuiper 8.1 (8.1, 0.4) — — A long drift of water and nitrogen ice with a little helium frozen into the oldest fragments. Out here the star is one bright point among many.
