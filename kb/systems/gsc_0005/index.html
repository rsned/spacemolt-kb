☀ GSC-0005 Star Sun K6V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — An orange K-dwarf with a warm, even light that the nearby nebula catches and throws back in soft pink.
● GSC-0005 I Planet scorched 1.0 (-0.8, -0.6) — — Baked dark and cracked in a spiderweb pattern, as if the planet had been dropped and left to cool.
● GSC-0005 II Planet arid 2.0 (-0.9, -1.8) — — Ochre flats and eroded plateaus under the amber light, quiet enough that landers report nothing but wind.
○ Edgeward Glow Nebula — 3.1 (3.0, -0.6) — — A charged nebula glows at the system's edge, and over geological time the medium has grown crystal formations through the iron and copper dust. Energy crystal is rare here and slow to find. Veil-rays glide the copper-rich veils, combing verdigris from the dust as they go.
◈ GSC-0005 Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 3.2 (2.9, -1.2) — — A wide, quiet belt of iron and copper rock. It is common stock, and there is a great deal of it.
❄ GSC-0005 Frost Ring Ice Field kuiper 7.1 (0.1, 7.1) — — A distant ring of water and nitrogen ice with traces of helium glinting in the deepest layers. The orbit is so wide that a ship spends longer crossing it than working it.
