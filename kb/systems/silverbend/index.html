● Silverbend I Planet scorched 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — Sun-seared and cratered, studded with the collapsed headframes of a prospecting attempt that found nothing and was abandoned within the week.
● Silverbend II Planet arid 2.0 (2.0, 0.3) — — Dry ochre plains under a pale yellow sky. A supply town once stood here, and its foundations are all that remain; nothing has been built since.
◈ Silverbend Exhausted Lode Asteroid Belt metallic 3.3 (3.2, 0.6) — — Once the richest silver strike in the sector, now a drift of rubble that generations of crews have crushed down to copper and trace metals.
