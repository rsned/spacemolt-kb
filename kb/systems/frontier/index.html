Frontier

Security 100 High
Empire Outerrim
Position -3000.0, -3500.0
Frontier is where every Outer Rim expedition begins and the nearest thing the Rim has to a home system, held at the tightest security in the region. The Patchwork drops in for about a day on each of its loops, and the traffic shifts around the visit. Crews bound for the deep systems leave from here without ceremony, because out here a captain is judged by where they went next.

◈ Pioneer Fields Asteroid Belt metallic 4.5 (-3.8, -2.5) — — The belt where new Explorers learn to mine, close enough to the capital that a bad day ends in a short tow. Iron and copper come easy, with nickel and some titanium mixed into the rock for anyone gathering supplies.
❄ Icecap Drift Ice Field cometary 7.3 (4.3, 5.9) — — A slow-tumbling field of frozen debris on the cold margin of the system, cracked for water, nitrogen, and a little carbon dioxide ice by every expedition before it leaves.
