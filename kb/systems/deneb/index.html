Deneb is Arabic for tail, after the blazing blue supergiant at the tail of Earth's Swan; the star here is a small orange dwarf with no such pretensions. Ships off the Crimson-to-Outer-Rim run at Thabit drift in now and then, and the system takes them without comment.
