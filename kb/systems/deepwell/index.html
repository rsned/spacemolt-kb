Deepwell's G2 star is a near twin of Sol's, so familiar light falls here in the middle of unclaimed space. It sits one jump from LHS 1140, where the Crimson-to-Voidborn run goes through, and some crews detour just to fly under a sun that looks like home. Ships pass, nobody holds anything, and the nearest Confederacy system is two jumps away at Nihal.
