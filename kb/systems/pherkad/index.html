Pherkad is the Voidborn frontier where patrols thin out and the Collective's order fades into unclaimed space. A charged nebula bends light here, and an isolated pocket is where reality grows thin. Neither is guarded. Customs cutters watch the Synchrony lane instead, since Solarian and Crimson traffic bound for the capital comes through this way.
