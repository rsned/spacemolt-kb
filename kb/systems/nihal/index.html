Nihal is a Confederacy frontier system, and a customs picket watches the way out to unclaimed Bluerift. Antimatter leaks from one of its belts, and the picket crews, who hold the thinnest social lives in the Confederacy, count that as the only excitement on the posting.

☀ Nihal Star Sun G5II 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A yellow bright giant, old and oversized, putting out many times Sol's light from a surface that is slowly coming apart. Pilots from half the district can pick it out by its colour alone, which makes it the best-known lamp for a long way around.
◈ Nihal Containment Fracture Asteroid Belt — 3.0 (-1.3, -2.7) — — Antimatter traces leaking from a natural containment lattice in the asteroid field. Less stable than Sol's primary deposits, but harvestable with the right equipment.
☁ Nihal Gas Plume Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 3.5 (2.9, -1.8) — — Argon streams out in a long, ragged ribbon, with a core of nebula gas that flushes rose-pink wherever a hull crosses it. Hub traffic cuts through daily and barely thins it.
