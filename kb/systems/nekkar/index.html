Nekkar lies on the Krynn-to-Haven road between GSC-0039 and Segin, and the freight on it flows in both directions at once. The system has more to offer than most of the stretch, which is why a few crews stay a day or two and the rest just burn through. There is no law, and the road keeps its own order.
