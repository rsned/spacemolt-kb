Driftwood sits on the long road between the Nebula empire and the Outer Rim, and the name fits a place where ships collect between legs of the journey. Haulers, scouts, and the occasional stranded crew gather here, then drift on again. With no law to lean on, crews trade help and spare parts the old way.
