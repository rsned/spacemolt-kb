Yarrowfield

Security 0 Lawless

Early surveyors named Yarrowfield for the dust bands that lie across its sky like a meadow gone to seed. The dust turns a small red sun dull and hazy, and the miners who settle in for a season say the place grows on you the way a quiet town does.
