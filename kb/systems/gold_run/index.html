☀ Aurelia Sun K3V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A rich orange star surrounded by an unusually dense planetary system. Federation surveyors named it Aurelia — golden, abundant, and worth the trip.
● Gold Run I Planet arid 1.0 (0.5, 0.9) — — Rust-red lowlands along the equator where palladium veins break the surface unaided, and the mining settlements sprang up as fast as the surveyors could stake them. The rest of the planet is bare rock that nobody bothers with.
● Gold Run II Planet terran 2.0 (0.1, 2.0) — — Wheat-colored plains run to every horizon, farmed by automated combines that feed the system's mining workforce. The harvest is sold forward as grain futures at Haven before it is planted, in the same pit as the ore.
⬡ Gold Run Extraction Hub Station — 2.5 (2.5, 0.3) — — The Federation's breadbasket. Five planets, two belts, and a workforce that never stops producing. The Extraction Hub coordinates everything — ore shipments to Market Prime, food shipments to Trader's Rest, and ice shipments everywhere. The name Gold Run isn't aspirational. It's a statement of quarterly earnings.
● Gold Run III Planet terran 3.0 (3.0, -0.3) — — Smelters and press halls cover the continents, turning local ore into trade goods before it leaves orbit. Shipping finished goods costs less than shipping rock, and the Federation has long since done that sum.
◈ Gold Run Mineral Fields Asteroid Belt metallic 3.2 (2.7, 1.6) — — The richest mineral field in Nebula space, worked in three shifts around the clock, year after year. Carbon and vanadium keep the foundries running, and the palladium and gold keep the shareholders content.
● Gold Run IV Planet jovian 4.0 (-0.4, -4.0) — — A banded gas giant strung with harvesting platforms, their flare stacks burning like small orange stars in the cloud tops. The worker habitats sit on its moons, where the crews live between rotations.
❄ Gold Run Cryobelt Ice Field kuiper 4.1 (-2.6, 3.2) — — Bright ice, mostly water and nitrogen with a little helium, cut into blocks for coolant and life support. Nobody puts it in a brochure, but every Federation station breathes and cools on what leaves here.
● Gold Run V Planet glacial 5.0 (2.9, -4.1) — — A glacial world at the cold edge of the system, its surface scraped by ice-mining rigs that add to the cryobelt's output. Crews work long, dim rotations under a distant sun.
