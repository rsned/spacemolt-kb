Propus is the fork in the Krynn lanes: ships bound for Haven turn toward Syrma, while the Sol and Frontier traffic carries on to Spica. Three capital runs share the road into it, so something is always on approach and something is always burning out. No law holds here, and every crew sorts out its own right of way.
