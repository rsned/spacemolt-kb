● Rimegate I Planet scorched 0.6 (-0.1, -0.6) — — Baked rock under a glare that bleaches camera feeds to white. From orbit the terminator glows a dull orange where the crust is still cooling from the day.
● Rimegate II Planet arid 1.1 (0.9, -0.6) — — Pale ridges of wind-sculpted sand under a sky the color of weak tea. Dry riverbeds branch across the lowlands and run out to nothing.
● Rimegate III Planet terran 1.5 (-1.0, -1.0) — — Green, wet, and dark on the night side without a single beacon to say anyone has ever landed. Cloud banks wrap its oceans in slow spirals.
○ Hollow Light Nebula — 2.9 (1.6, 2.4) — — Nobody planned to find this nebula. A passing crew picked it up by accident, far from any settled space, and the crystal readings it gives are faint but unmistakable. Phase-lurkers browse the dust, blinking out of sight and back on no schedule anyone has worked out.
