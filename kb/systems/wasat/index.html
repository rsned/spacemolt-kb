Wasat has a yellow Sol-class star, rare on this stretch of the road between BD+20 2457 and Westmark, and crews coming off the red dwarves on either side notice the warmer light. Ships pass in both directions between Krynn and Nexus Prime, and for most of them it is only a pause in the burn.
