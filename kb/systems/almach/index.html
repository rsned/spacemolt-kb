☀ Almach Star Sun G2V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A G-type star of the same stamp as Earth's, steady and unremarkable. Homesick crews tend to linger on the viewscreen a moment before committing to the burn.
● Almach I Planet scorched 0.4 (0.0, 0.4) — — Tide-locked and black on its night side, with a dayside cooked to a polished crust. Sensors read it as a very large, very patient oven.
● Almach II Planet arid 0.8 (-0.5, 0.6) — — Rust-colored plains, thin air, and long shadows from low ridges. Whoever ran the numbers on a settlement here ran them once.
◈ Almach Stray Facets Asteroid Belt — 1.0 (-0.1, 1.0) — — Geology scattered trade crystals through this field of iron and copper rubble without any design. There is no deposit to find, so a miner works the metal and counts every crystal as a bonus.
● Almach III Planet terran 1.1 (-0.6, -0.9) — — Ocean and forest under a mild sky, close to the template a Federation colonist would ask for. The cost in labour and freight is where the conversation ends.
● Almach IV Planet jovian 5.2 (4.9, 1.8) — — A banded gas giant whose upper storms shear past each other in amber and cream. Pilots who time their transit can catch a good one on the limb.
