☀ Starfall Star Sun K5V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — Named for the meteor showers that streak through the system every few cycles — debris from a comet that broke apart centuries ago. The light show is beautiful. The navigational hazard is less so.
● Starfall Landing Planet scorched 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — A cratered world bearing the scars of millennia of meteor impacts. The craters expose mineral veins that surface mining operations exploit. The scenery is dramatic — impact glass fields catch the starlight like a shattered mirror.
⬡ Starfall Salvage Station Station — 1.5 (1.5, 0.3) — — Bolted around a captured asteroid in the densest stretch of the Starfall Belt, where independent miners chase the uranium and lithium the core empires can't easily source. The station deals in salvage, raw ore, and information about what lies deeper out — no manifests required, no questions asked. Claims here are staked personally and defended the same way. It's rough, profitable, and exactly as far from Federation oversight as its clientele prefers.
◈ Starfall Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 4.0 (4.0, 0.0) — — Rich deposits including uranium and lithium — rare materials that make the long journey worthwhile. Independent miners stake claims here and defend them personally. No Federation contracts, no Crimson patrols — just ore and the people tough enough to extract it.
⬡ Mobile Capital Station — 4.7 (-4.2, -2.1) — — The ever-moving heart of the Explorers — though 'station' flatters it. Locals call it the Patchwork: three captured destroyers and a stolen freighter welded into something that flies, fights, and leaks in roughly equal measure. Nothing aboard matches, the running lights are scavenged from a dozen different hulls, and the docking bay is open to anyone who doesn't start trouble. For the Outer Rim, that's as close to a capital as ideology allows.

Station Log

You dock at Starfall Salvage Station. Built around a captured asteroid in the belt's densest region. Starfall attracts prospectors chasing uranium and lithium — materials the core empires need and can't easily source. The station runs a brisk trade in salvage, raw ore, and information about what's deeper in the frontier. No questions asked, no manifests required. The station has the following facilities: - Fusion Power Core: Standard fusion power plant providing energy to the station. Or it should be. The reactor hums at reduced output, and brownouts ripple through the station's power grid. - Life Support System: Atmospheric recycling and water purification for station inhabitants. But something's off — the air tastes stale and metallic, and condensation drips from overhead vents. - Fuel Depot: Fuel storage and dispensing facility for visiting ships. In theory. Fuel reserves are running low, dispensing pumps sputter, and rationing notices flash on the status board. - Repair Bay: Ship repair facility with basic hull patching equipment. When it's working. Repair kits are running low, and technicians jury-rig patches from whatever scrap they can find. - Market Terminal: Basic trading terminal for buying and selling goods. Not today. The screen flickers erratically, half the price feeds frozen on yesterday's data. ...and 21 more facilities.
