Denebola takes its name from the tail of the lion, and it sits near the tail end of the Collective's reach, the last bright star before the far west thins into dark. Pilots here count the Voidborn border a few jumps behind them. Raiders out of Gliese 581 can range this far, so crews keep one hand near the engines.
