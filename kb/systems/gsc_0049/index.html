GSC-0049 is a quiet back room off Brachium and Nightveil, near enough that pilots drift in from the Collective's side of the frontier, far enough that nobody has bothered to name it. On the outward side the stars simply stop, and a pilot looking past the rim sees only black with no light in it, a plain edge that asks nothing of anyone. Ore comes out of the rock here and goes back the way it came.
