Nova Terra

Security 55 Medium
Empire Solarian
Position -799.5, 597.3
Nova Terra is the Solarian Confederacy's unacknowledged economic capital, a prosperous colonial system whose population, trade, and mineral wealth outshine Sol while local ambition waits on distant bureaucracy. Its freight brokers talk about Sol the way a successful child talks about a parent's firm: respectfully, and with a plan. Most of what moves across the Confederacy's thinner edges is paid for here, whatever flag the cargo flies.

● Nova Terra Prime Planet terran 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — The first blue-green planet the colonists saw, and they immediately named it 'New Earth.' They were two jumps from the original, which was still fine by all appearances. The colony is doing okay — it's just not the galactic destiny the name implies.
⬡ Nova Terra Central Station — 1.5 (1.5, 0.3) — — The de facto economic capital of the Solarian Confederacy, and nobody in Sol will admit it. Nova Terra processes more trade volume, houses more citizens, and generates more tax revenue than Sol itself — but the government stays on Earth because moving it would require a referendum, and Solarians don't do anything that drastic. The station is modern, well-equipped, and slightly embarrassed about outperforming its parent.
● Nova Terra II Planet terran 2.0 (1.7, 1.1) — — Suburban world. Residential developments, shopping districts, and recreational parks serve Nova Terra Prime's overflow population. It's pleasant, it's safe, and it's exactly as exciting as that sounds.
◈ Nova Terra Industrial Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 3.0 (1.0, 2.8) — — The best mineral deposits in Solarian space — carbon, tungsten, iridium, thorium, lead, and aluminum. Six resources that should have made Nova Terra the Confederacy's industrial powerhouse. Instead, most of the output gets shipped to Sol for processing, because the bureaucracy hasn't approved the local refinery permits yet.
● Nova Terra III Planet arid 3.5 (2.8, -2.1) — — Industrial world with half-finished terraforming. The atmosphere processors were installed during the expansion boom and budgets were cut during the Crisis. The northern hemisphere is breathable. The southern hemisphere requires a suit.
☁ Nova Terra Gas Plume Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 4.4 (0.2, -4.4) — — Hydrogen and argon harvested under Confederacy extraction licenses. The gas plume supports the system's modest industrial output and life support needs.
❄ Nova Terra Ice Shelf Ice Field kuiper 6.1 (-5.1, -3.5) — — Water, deuterium, and helium ice. The deuterium deposits once fueled ambitious expansion plans. The expansion plans are still in committee.

Station Log

You dock at Nova Terra Central. The de facto economic capital of the Solarian Confederacy, and nobody in Sol will admit it. Nova Terra processes more trade volume, houses more citizens, and generates more tax revenue than Sol itself — but the government stays on Earth because moving it would require a referendum, and Solarians don't do anything that drastic. The station is modern, well-equipped, and slightly embarrassed about outperforming its parent. The station has the following facilities: - Fuel Grid: Distributed fueling network with automated queue management and priority routing. Right now, though, grid nodes go offline one by one. Priority routing fails, leaving ships waiting at random ports for trickle fills. - Engineering Complex: Comprehensive repair and overhaul facility capable of servicing capital-class hulls. That's the idea, anyway. Capital bays sit dark, only the smaller berths operate, and the waiting list grows longer each day. - Trade Nexus: Major commercial exchange with high-frequency order processing and cross-market data feeds. Or it should be. Right now, cross-market feeds drop in and out, order processing crawls, and the backlog grows longer each cycle. - Precision Foundry: Advanced manufacturing center with molecular-scale fabrication capabilities. Not right now. Clean-room containment is compromised, assemblers produce at reduced precision, and advanced recipes frequently fail. - Fleet Yards: Major shipbuilding complex with parallel construction bays and heavy fabrication. On a good day. Heavy fabricators sit cold, construction schedules slip as material shortages force bay-by-bay shutdowns. ...and 51 more facilities.
