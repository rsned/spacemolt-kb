Every Crimson ship bound for Sol, Haven, or Frontier by the long way runs through Alphecca, between Scheat and Ankaa, which makes it a corridor first and a system second. The star is faint, so the lane shows mostly as running lights strung out ahead. Nobody keeps order here, and the pace of the road does it for them.
