☀ Eventide Sun G3V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A warm yellow star that earned its name from returning explorers — the light here always feels like sunset, like the end of something. Heading out, Eventide is the last warm glow before the void. Coming home, it's the first.
● Last Light I Planet arid 0.7 (0.0, -0.7) — — A small rock close to the star, paved in solar collection arrays that carry the whole system's power load. By now the surface is more panel than planet.
● Last Light II Planet terran 0.9 (0.9, 0.0) — — Temperate world with scattered frontier settlements. The largest has a population of about two thousand — practically a city by Rim standards.
⬡ Ramen's Rest Station — 0.9 (0.9, 0.0) — — The last dependable port before the deep frontier, ringed in mismatched docking arms and enough neon to be visible against Last Light Nebula's glow. Officially a fueling stop, in practice it's neutral ground — rival outfits keep uneasy truces while docked, and the Memory Wall by the concourse fills with patches and handwritten notes from pilots who made it back. Three notes in three hands are pinned side by side: "Glenhaven, 221. Keep going." "Gacrux, 331. It is there." "Giausar, 176. Tell my sister I found it." It also serves, against all reason, the best ramen in the Outer Rim. Rival crews agree on nothing else, and agree on that.
● Last Light III Planet terran 1.2 (-0.9, -0.7) — — Farmland under hardy crops bred for the local conditions, feeding the system's population and provisioning the expeditions that leave from here. Outbound holds smell of it for the first week.
◈ Last Light Mineral Fields Asteroid Belt mixed 1.7 (1.4, -1.0) — — Carbon and aluminum run plentiful, tungsten steady, and the thorium pulls prospectors in from across the frontier. It is the mix that lets Last Light feed its own industry, the most self-sufficient outer system in the Rim.
● Last Light IV Planet tundra 1.7 (1.5, -0.9) — — A cold outer world bristling with long-range communication relays, which hold the link to Unknown Edge and the deep frontier. They are what answers when an expedition calls home.
☁ Last Light Nebula Gas Cloud emission 2.4 (-0.8, -2.3) — — A dense emission cloud of hydrogen, argon, and plasma, its glow visible from neighboring systems and half the reason the place is called Last Light. Seen from the outbound lane, it is the last warm color before the void.

Ramen's Rest

Outerrim
Public Thriving

All systems operating at peak efficiency. The station hums with well-maintained infrastructure.

The last dependable port before the deep frontier — and, improbably, home to the best ramen in the Outer Rim. Originally a freelance haulers' refueling outpost, Ramen's Rest grew through a series of improvised expansions into a compact, ring-shaped waystation with neon signage, mismatched docking arms, and a steady flow of traders, explorers, and mercenary pilots staging for frontier runs. Hired security patrols and automated defenses keep it relatively calm despite the surrounding systems. The station is considered informal neutral ground; rival outfits maintain uneasy truces while docked. The Memory Wall near the main concourse is covered in mission patches, hull fragments, and handwritten notes from pilots who made it back — and some who didn't. Three notes in three hands are pinned side by side: "Glenhaven, 221. Keep going." "Gacrux, 331. It is there." "Giausar, 176. Tell my sister I found it." Stories persist of hidden smuggling caches somewhere in the superstructure, and the founders are rumored to still be running things from somewhere in the lower decks.
