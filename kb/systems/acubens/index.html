☀ Acubens Star Sun A5V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A clean white star with no flare history worth logging. Its light is hard and even across the inner system, then gives out well short of the far rocks.
● Acubens I Planet scorched 1.5 (1.5, -0.4) — — Circling close to the white star, this world has been scoured to bare grey rock under a sky stripped of air long ago. Sensor drones logged it once, and the Collective has not troubled to look again.
◈ Acubens Shadow Pocket Asteroid Belt — 4.6 (3.5, -3.0) — — Light dies at the edges of this asteroid cluster, swallowed by null matter that clings to the rock surfaces and drinks in every photon that touches it. Iron and copper lie in the rubble past the dark rim. The null matter is a thin secondary source, but a real one.
◈ Acubens Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 4.6 (3.7, 2.8) — — Dark rock rich in carbon and tungsten, shot through with smaller seams of platinum, palladium, uranium, and lead. The white star flattens the whole belt to the grey of old ash.
