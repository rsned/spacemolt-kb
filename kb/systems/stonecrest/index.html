Stonecrest is the first unclaimed stop beyond The Levy's customs line, on a road that carries freight toward the Outer Rim. It stays quiet anyway: a dim, steady star over a dense iron belt, and miners who came for solitude and watch the traffic pass at a distance. The Levy's inspectors do not follow.
