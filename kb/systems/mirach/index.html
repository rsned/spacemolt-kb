☀ Mirach Star Sun M2V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A red dwarf whose name means girdle in Arabic. Behind it, the sky holds a pale smudge, a distant galaxy navigators call Mirach's Ghost.
● Mirach I Planet scorched 1.0 (0.5, 0.9) — — Black basalt hugging the red star, its dayside cracked into plates the size of cities.
● Mirach II Planet arid 2.0 (-0.2, -2.0) — — Gravel desert and dry gullies under a bruise-coloured sky, windless in every survey pass.
● Mirach III Planet terran 3.0 (2.3, 1.9) — — Too far from so faint a sun to be this green, except that a heavy cloud blanket holds the warmth. Dark ferns and standing water fill the twilight.
● Mirach IV Planet tundra 4.0 (-1.2, 3.8) — — Frost plains and low wind-scoured ridges. On one ridge top a stripped hulk sits where a prospecting ship put down and never lifted, with the frost working on it for years.
◈ Mirach Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 4.1 (-4.1, -0.7) — — Iron and copper rock with a seam of lithium, where slag-tortoises plough the rubble and leave trails of slag across the larger fragments. Miners learn to fly around the longest ones.
● Mirach V Planet glacial 5.0 (5.0, 0.4) — — Blue-white ice sheets cracked into plates and crowded together like pack ice. Nothing moves across it but light.
