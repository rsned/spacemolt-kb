Beid

Beid is the last unclaimed stop before the Outer Rim begins, and it has the loose, unhurried feel of a place at the edge of someone else's territory. Nebula-bound and Rim-bound haulers pass through on the long capital run, and some drop out of the lane to refuel or work the belt for a season. Nobody keeps the peace and nobody keeps books, so the system runs on common sense and favours.
