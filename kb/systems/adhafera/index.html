Adhafera is the first unclaimed stop past Markab, and the crews who cross into it tend to let their shoulders drop. Haulers coming off the Markab lanes pause here to sort out their next leg, and independent crews drift in and out of the gas. Nobody holds the place, and so far nobody has needed to.

☀ Adhafera Star Sun G8V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A calm G-type dwarf, a shade more orange than Sol and far better behaved than the glare back at Markab. Pilots coming across the border often notice the relief before they notice the system.
● Adhafera I Planet scorched 0.3 (0.2, -0.3) — — Cinder-dark rock turning slowly under constant heat, its dayside hot enough to soften landing struts. Surveyors logged it once and filed it under scenery.
● Adhafera II Planet arid 0.6 (-0.5, -0.2) — — Dust-colored plains broken by wind-carved mesas, under a sky thin enough to turn the star white at midday. Prospectors who landed found stone and more stone.
● Adhafera III Planet terran 0.8 (0.4, 0.7) — — Real oceans and real weather, seen from orbit as a bright blue world wrapped in slow white spirals. Settlers have looked it over often enough, and the lack of any charter or law keeps them in their ships.
☁ Adhafera Vapor Fields Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 1.1 (-1.0, 0.2) — — Pale banks of hydrogen and argon, with xenon glinting through the thicker pockets like frost on glass. Drift-rays hang in the argon with their fronds trailing, and mostly ignore the ships that pass.
