Khambalia

Security 55 Medium

Position 1651.4, -5368.7
Khambalia is the Federation's crossroads between the workshops and the fringe. Ore from the fringe funnels through here toward the workshops, and the credits it earns drift on to Trader's Rest, where money goes to rest. Under the white star hulls fade fast, and a Khambalia hauler is known by its paint.

☀ Khambalia Star Sun A1V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A white A-type star, hotter and brighter than the modest suns around it. Hull paint fades quickly in this light, and freight crews learn to budget for repainting.
● Khambalia I Planet scorched 2.3 (0.2, 2.3) — — Baked dark and crazed with heat fractures, with no air left to soften the light. Against the white star it looks black and reflects almost nothing.
● Khambalia II Planet arid 4.8 (-4.5, -1.7) — — Salmon-pink desert under a bleached sky, its dune fields combed into regular ridges. A Federation assessor once listed it as arable, and the entry has never been corrected.
● Khambalia III Planet tundra 9.0 (-8.4, 3.3) — — Cold plains and glaciers lit hard and white by the star. The ice throws back so much light that sensors report it as a second, dimmer sun.
◈ Khambalia Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 12.4 (-8.1, -9.4) — — Mixed rock heavy with lead and carbon, with veins of gold and vanadium and a dangerous glitter of uranium and radium. Miners here carry radiation meters and read them often.
◈ Khambalia Crystal Market Asteroid Belt — 14.4 (11.4, 8.7) — — Trade crystals form in the mineral-rich veins of this belt, well beyond the Grand Exchange's reach. Haven has not yet set up extraction rights here, so the crystals still change hands the old way: a handshake and a scale.
❄ Khambalia Ice Fields Ice Field kuiper 25.7 (-14.2, 21.4) — — Water and nitrogen ice with helium and methane layered through it. Pressblisters crawl the nitrogen faces, swelling pale and taut as they boil the ice inside their sacs.
