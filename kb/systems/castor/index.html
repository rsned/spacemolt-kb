Castor lies a jump off the busy Beid lane, close enough to see the traffic and far enough not to be part of it. Its orange sun has the warm cast of a sunset that never finishes, and nobody has bothered to fight over the ground. It suits miners who want a quieter claim than the crossroads next door.
