Solarian and Crimson hauls bound for Voidborn space funnel through this dim star, so the lanes carry more hulls than the system has room to park. The name means rival of Mars, a joke at the expense of a red dwarf that would rival nothing. No authority holds Antares, and crews work out their own right of way.
