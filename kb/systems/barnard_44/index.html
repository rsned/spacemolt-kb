Barnard 44 was a catalogue entry nobody cared about until Director Sable took it, and the impersonal system has been turned into reliably profitable territory. The one lane in runs through Zubenelgenubi, and everything here is kept the way a well-managed firm keeps a branch office: quiet, orderly, nothing wasted. Visitors without business at Sable Port will find the system has no use for them.
