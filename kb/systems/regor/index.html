☀ Regor Star Sun WR 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — Gamma Velorum — one of the most spectacular stellar systems in the sky. A Wolf-Rayet star paired with a hot O-supergiant, their combined luminosity reaching hundreds of thousands of times the Sun. The Wolf-Rayet component is shedding mass catastrophically, its outer layers stripped by its own radiation.
◈ Dimensional Rift Vein Asteroid Belt — 5.4 (3.5, -4.1) — — Void ore crystallized along a dimensional rift. The ore phases in and out of visibility.
☁ Regor WR Bubble Gas Cloud emission 6.3 (-6.2, 1.4) — — A vast expanding shell of gas blasted directly from the Wolf-Rayet star's outer layers at thousands of kilometers per second. The collision between this fast wind and the surrounding medium heats the interior to millions of degrees. This material is the raw stuff of future stars.
◈ Regor Inner Debris Asteroid Belt mixed 40.6 (-2.1, -40.6) — — Rocky material at the ragged outer edge of the Wolf-Rayet bubble, bombarded continuously by hard radiation and fast particles. Mining here is hazardous — equipment degrades quickly — but the yields are exceptional.
