Revati is a junction just past Zibal, where the road from Haven to Sol and to Nexus Prime settles into the long haul. Ships arrive from Zibal and leave for Alfirk in a steady flow, and the other lanes feed side traffic into the stream. Crews know it as a place to pass through.
