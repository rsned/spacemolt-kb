Every Crimson ship bound for Sol, Haven, or Frontier by the long way comes through Rasalgethi from Fumalsamakah and leaves for Nashira. By here the Crimson patrols are behind them and nobody has taken their place. The name is old Arabic for the head of the kneeling one, a grander title than this dim star has earned.
