GSC-0039 lies on the long haul between Krynn and Haven, a catalogue number the crews never bothered to turn into a name. Ships from both capitals cross it in a steady stream and pass each other with little to say. Raiders out of Zaniah can range this far, so crews watch their scanners on the way through.
