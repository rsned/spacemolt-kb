Spica takes its name from the ear of grain in Virgo's hand, though this dim red star offers nothing to harvest. Krynn-to-Sol and Krynn-to-Frontier ships cross it in a steady file, and the traffic here rarely goes quiet. It is lawless space, and the crews who pass through treat it as one more link in a long chain.
