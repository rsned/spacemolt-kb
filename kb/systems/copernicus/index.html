Copernicus is the Federation's door toward Solarian and Voidborn space, and the freight road to them runs straight through it. Freight bound for those empires gets its last Federation scrutiny here, and arriving cargo gets its first. Federation crews think of it as the last stop where everyone still quotes the same prices.
