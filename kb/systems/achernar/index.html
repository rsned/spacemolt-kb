Achernar is the last stretch of unclaimed space before The Experiment, and long-haul traffic between the Collective and the far empires threads through it. Nothing keeps order here, so ships going each way keep their distance and their own counsel. The Collective's customs cutters hold the line a jump inward, at The Experiment.
