Altais is the first real junction past Frontier, and every expedition leaving the capital passes under its aging yellow giant. The giant sheds a wind that is slowly enriching the outer system, while the farthest ice stays cold and out of its reach. The Patchwork passes through on its loop, and the lanes get busy while it is here.

☀ Altais Star Sun K2III 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — Delta Draconis — a yellow giant that has expanded well past its main-sequence size. Its cooler outer layers have begun to shed into an extended stellar wind, slowly enriching the outer system.
○ Cycling Drifts Nebula — 3.1 (-2.4, -1.9) — — A nebula pocket where phase crystals form and dissolve in cycles, so a rich patch of dust can be thin by the time a second pass arrives. Iron and copper ride the same drifts, steady and patient.
❄ Altais Outer Ice Ice Field kuiper 6.5 (5.8, -3.0) — — Water and nitrogen ice in the cold outer system, bright where nothing has ever cracked it. The giant's wind has not yet reached this far.
◈ Altais Still Shoals Asteroid Belt metallic 10.1 (-10.0, -1.2) Yes 60 Like the outer ice, these shoals lie beyond the reach of the giant's wind. The iron, copper, nickel, and titanium here are as cold and undisturbed as the day the system formed.
