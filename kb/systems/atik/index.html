Atik keeps bad company: the systems around it all had their turn with prospectors and came out quiet. Ice and metal are both here in quantity, and so is a long stretch of lawless space between any crew and help. Director Sable's stronghold at Barnard 44 is within reach, and not every crew that tried the belt left again.
