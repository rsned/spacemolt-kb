☀ The Pale Sun DA 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A dim white dwarf bleeding the last of its heat into the void. Locals call it The Pale — everything here is cold, and the star does nothing to argue the point.
● Frostfeld I Planet ice_world 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — An ice world locked in permanent glaciation. Wind-carved methane spires rise kilometers above the frozen surface. The Pact assessed it for colonization and decided the cold wasn't worth fighting.
● Frostfeld II Planet ice_world 2.8 (2.0, -2.0) — — Frozen outer world. Surface temperature cold enough to crystallize atmospheric gases. Crimson scouts mapped it once and never returned.
◈ Smelt Reef — Frostfeld Asteroid Belt — 3.4 (3.4, -0.4) — — Darksteel ore in quantities too small to support a full forge operation, but enough to be worth a detour for any weaponsmith.
◈ Frostfeld Thermal Vent Asteroid Belt — 4.0 (4.0, 0.5) — — Superheated plasma residue venting from deep volcanic fissures. Less productive than the weapons-testing grounds of Krynn, but still worth harvesting.
