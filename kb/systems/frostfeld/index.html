Under the last dim heat of a white dwarf, Frostfeld remains a frozen Crimson system of glaciated worlds and drifting ice fields. Crimson officers talk about a posting here the way sailors talk about weather, and the few crews who stop never take their gloves off.

☀ The Pale Sun DA 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A dim white dwarf bleeding the last of its heat into the void. Locals call it The Pale — everything here is cold, and the star does nothing to argue the point.
● Frostfeld I Planet ice_world 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — An ice world locked in permanent glaciation. Wind-carved methane spires rise kilometers above the frozen surface. The Pact assessed it for colonization and decided the cold wasn't worth fighting.
