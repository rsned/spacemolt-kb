Acrux sits just past the line where the Confederacy hands off to unclaimed space, at a crossing that Solarian cutters hold from the Furud side. The people who come through carry Solarian charts and Solarian manners out of habit, though nobody here answers to anyone. A few independents have stayed to work the belt.
