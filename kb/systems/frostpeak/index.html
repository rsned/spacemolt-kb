Frostpeak

Security 0 Lawless

Position 47.4, -3920.0
Frostpeak sits on the long road between Federation space and the Outer Rim, and most of the ships passing through are freight at one end of a contract or the other. Nobody holds the place, so the crews who pause here do it for their own reasons and the weak red light does not argue. The Levy is only two jumps behind, which is as near as some crews care to be.

☀ Frostpeak Star Sun M4V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A cool red dwarf, barely warm enough to deserve the name, so frost forms on hulls left idle too long. The system's name needs no other explanation.
● Frostpeak I Planet scorched 1.0 (1.0, 0.3) — — Tide-locked to its star, one face is charred black and the other is frosted white, with a narrow band between where neither extreme quite wins.
● Frostpeak II Planet arid 2.0 (2.0, 0.3) — — Flat grey dust plains under a dim red sky, with nothing on them taller than a boulder. Outer Rim crews use it as a landmark and little else.
◈ Unskimmed Belt Asteroid Belt — 3.4 (3.4, -0.4) — — Rough trade crystal sits in this belt ungraded, never put before Haven's assessors and so never marked up. It fetches less per unit than the stock that has been, but nothing is skimmed off the top. Iron and copper rock surround it.
