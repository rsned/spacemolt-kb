HR 8832 is a landmark on the Krynn-to-Haven run, a yellow sun that crews check their position against before the next long jump. Ships pass in a near-constant file with the cloud off to one side, and raiders out of Zaniah are close enough that pilots keep a hand near the throttle. There is no law here, and no one waits to enforce one.
