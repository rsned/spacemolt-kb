☀ Adhara Star Sun K2V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — An amber K-dwarf that neither flares nor sulks. Transit crews glance at it once to confirm the jump landed where it should, then go back to their manifests.
● Adhara I Planet scorched 0.2 (-0.2, 0.0) — — Baked basalt under a skin of cracked slag, scoured bare by the nearness of the star. It is not worth the fuel to land.
● Adhara II Planet arid 0.4 (0.4, 0.2) — — Tan dunes and wind-bare ridges under a thin brown haze. The surface keeps no markers, and every footprint is gone by the next watch.
◈ Adhara Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 0.6 (0.3, 0.5) — — Iron, copper, and aluminum in plain quantities, with gold flecked through the heavier rock. Crews on the long run dip in to fill a hold and rejoin the traffic, and the gold-bearing stone is the part they argue over.
● Adhara III Planet terran 0.6 (0.4, 0.5) — — Open ocean and broad continents under a high white cloud deck. It is the best view in the system, and lane crews give it one look through the viewport before the next jump.
