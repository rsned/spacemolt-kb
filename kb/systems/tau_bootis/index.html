Tau Bootis

Security 0 Lawless
Empire Independent
Position 2528.5, -2771.7
Tau Bootis is the last unclaimed system before Alrakis on the road from Crimson space, and ships coming in from the dark stop here to sort themselves out before they reach the Federation. The belts are productive and one of them carries gold, which is why a few independents have stayed rather than pass through.
