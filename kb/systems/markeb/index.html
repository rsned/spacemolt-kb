Markeb is where the Outer Rim begins for anyone arriving from the Beid side, a blue-white star over a border that nobody guards. Haulers cross the line without a stop and carry on toward First Step and Void Gate, squinting through the glare. Police presence is thin, and the rest is a matter of manners.

☀ Markeb Star Sun B2IV 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A B-type subgiant burning blue-white, young and hot enough to be gone in a geological blink. Its ultraviolet fogs sensor lenses on the long approach, and pilots coming in off the Beid lane learn to tint early.
● Markeb I Planet scorched 2.9 (-2.7, 1.1) — — Stripped to bare rock, with nothing left to say it ever had air or water. The ultraviolet has sanded even the dust to a uniform pale grey.
● Markeb II Planet scorched 3.9 (-3.6, -1.4) — — Lava fields that have frozen and remelted so many times the crust lies in overlapping plates of black glass, ridged like scar tissue. The terminator glows a dull orange all the way around.
● Markeb III Planet scorched 6.9 (2.4, 6.5) — — Old dune seas, fused by the glare into bone-white ridges beneath a sky gone black with the air long stripped away. Nobody has claimed it, and the Rim keeps no registry that would care.
● Markeb IV Planet jovian 11.7 (-0.1, -11.7) — — A deep cobalt giant whose cloud tops fluoresce faintly under the star's ultraviolet, so its night side glows like a dying lamp. Its storms turn slowly and have never been named.
● Markeb V Planet scorched 34.7 (34.5, 4.0) — — A white world sheathed in ice, so far from the star that it is only the brightest point in the sky. The light that reaches it is a hard blue, and it tints the frost to match.
○ Markeb Quantum Eddy Nebula — 37.3 (31.9, -19.4) — — Quantum fragments pile up in a gravitational eddy inside the nebula, a smaller deposit than Frontier's but every bit as potent. Iron and copper ride the same current.
◈ Markeb Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 41.7 (-17.7, -37.7) — — Dosimeters tick faster here than a belt of carbon and aluminum has any right to. Lead, uranium, and trace radium lie in the same dark rock, and the sensible crews work short shifts.
