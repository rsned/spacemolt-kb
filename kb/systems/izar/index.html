Izar sits on the road between the Outer Rim and the Solarian and Crimson capitals, a quiet red dwarf that sees more traffic than its size deserves. Haulers and long-range scouts top up here before the stretch toward Aldhibah, and nobody asks them to leave. The Rim's easy habits hold, and so does its habit of minding your own cargo.
