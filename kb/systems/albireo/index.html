The Rampart's checkpoint is one jump from Albireo, so everything bound for Crimson space or the long routes beyond it passes through. Crews top up, trade news, and put their papers in order before the checkpoint while Pact-built hulls with extra plating pass the other way. It is where the Pact's rules end and everyone else's improvise.
