Seginus

Seginus has no star, only drifting belts, clouds, and ice laid across several useful transit lines. Prospectors and haulers kept coming back until the charts gave up and drew it in, too valuable as a working ground and a waypoint to leave blank.

◈ Seginus Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 3.2 (0.4, 3.1) — — A loose metallic belt tumbling slowly through the starless dark, iron-heavy with copper in the seams. Work lamps are the only light, so every crew sees the others from far off.
☁ Seginus Haze Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 4.0 (3.9, 0.8) — — A hydrogen and neon haze shot through with plasma and xenon, with rare fluorine in its thicker pockets. It glows faintly where the plasma runs hot, the only warm color in a starless system.
❄ Seginus Ice Fields Ice Field kuiper 7.5 (-1.6, -7.3) — — Wide-spread ice, water and nitrogen mostly, with helium frost in the deeper pockets. The field is big enough that the ships topping off tanks or waiting on the next lane need never meet.
