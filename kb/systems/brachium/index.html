● Brachium I Planet scorched 1.0 (-0.3, -1.0) — — Black rock baked to a glassy skin on its day side. Scanners return the same readings on every pass.
● Brachium II Planet arid 2.0 (2.0, 0.2) — — Dust-yellow plains and shallow canyons under a pale, hot sky. The wind has been sorting the sand into fine ridges for longer than anyone has watched.
● Brachium III Planet terran 3.0 (-1.4, 2.6) — — Blue seas and broken cloud under a bright star, the closest thing to a comfortable view in the system. It looks all the more comfortable for the neighbourhood around it.
● Brachium IV Planet tundra 4.0 (-3.6, 1.8) — — Ice-glazed plains stained dark by windblown dust, brittle underfoot and bright overhead. The frost line runs straight through the middle of the continent.
● Brachium V Planet glacial 5.0 (4.2, -2.8) — — A white world sealed under ice, its surface seamed with blue cracks that open and close with the long seasons. It reflects the star so well that it reads on passing scanners as a smaller, colder sun.
