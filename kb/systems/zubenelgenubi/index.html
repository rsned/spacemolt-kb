Every ship bound for Sable Port passes through Zubenelgenubi, and everyone here knows it. It is a crossroads that should be thriving; instead pilots cross in a straight line, say little, and keep their eyes on the lane to Barnard 44. The Director's business is the only business, and the system is quiet in a way that suggests it prefers that.
