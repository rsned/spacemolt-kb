Porrima

Security 0 Lawless
Empire Independent
Position 1169.0, 3070.9
Porrima takes its name from a double star on old charts, though only a single orange sun answers the scanners here. Adhara and the Krynn to Nexus Prime traffic lie one jump away, so Porrima sees more passing ships than its remote look suggests. A few stop for the metals and leave before the lane has a chance to miss them.
