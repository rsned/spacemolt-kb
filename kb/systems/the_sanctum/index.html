The Sanctum Uncharted

Security 0 Lawless
Empire Independent
Position ≈ -4478.0, 2345.0
A white dwarf long past its heat, with an iced world, a ring of brittle rubble and a thin grey veil of gas. A wrecked Voidborn station drifts beside the broken shell of an older receiver. No lane reaches the system, and every Voidborn chart that should show one is blank in the same place.

No lane leaves the system.
