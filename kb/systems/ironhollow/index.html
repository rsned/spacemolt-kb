Ironhollow lies just past the Voidborn frontier, where prospectors chase two faint echoes of Sol: antimatter readings buried beneath background radiation and traces of Sol alloy carried by ancient ejecta. Prospectors who go home empty-handed tell the story anyway, which is how the echoes got their reputation.
