☀ Merope Star Sun K4V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A K-type dwarf in dull copper, its light warm enough to flatter a hull and little else.
● Merope I Planet scorched 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — Heat-fused ridges of dark stone that tick as they cool on the night side. Nothing has ever landed to hear them.
● Merope II Planet arid 2.0 (-1.9, -0.8) — — Wind-carved mesas stand over pale salt basins, the whole landscape the colour of old parchment.
◈ Merope Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 4.1 (2.3, -3.4) — — Iron and copper rubble in plenty, and the leftovers of those who came for it: a sheared tether line trailing from one rock, and a hull frame drifting in the debris, picked clean ages ago.
○ Merope Dim Phase Pocket Nebula — 4.2 (3.1, 2.8) — — Phase crystals grow in the dust of this small nebula pocket at rates a survey instrument can barely register. What a crew pulls out is low in quantity and reliable from one visit to the next.
