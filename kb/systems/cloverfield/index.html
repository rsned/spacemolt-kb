Cloverfield

Cloverfield sits on the long road between Haven and the Outer Rim, and ships cross it in a steady current, outbound and inbound alike. It has a crowd of worlds and clouds that most crews fly past. The name is the friendliest thing about it.

● Cloverfield I Planet scorched 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — Starlight has baked this innermost world to a bronze crust, split in places by vents that glow dull red at night.
● Cloverfield II Planet arid 2.0 (2.0, -0.3) — — Sand seas the colour of straw roll toward a flat horizon with no cloud above them. Ships passing on the lane read it as the system's only yellow.
● Cloverfield III Planet terran 3.0 (3.0, -0.3) — — Green-brown continents under a thin band of cloud, with seas that catch the orange light and throw it back as copper. Crews on the lane look down with envy and keep flying.
