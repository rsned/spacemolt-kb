The Archive

Security 0 Lawless
Empire Independent
Position -4405.8, 1153.7
The Archive lies within the Collective's influence but outside its law, and its patrols are few. Voidborn instruments keep logging dimensional anomalies from this quiet red-dwarf system, and navigation can go unreliable near the oddest of them. Survey crews come for the readings, miners come for the plain ore, and few of either stay long.
