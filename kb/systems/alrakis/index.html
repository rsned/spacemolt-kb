Alrakis is a crossroads on the frontier edge of Nebula space, and the road from Crimson space passes through on its way to Cargo Lanes. The independent miners who settled here came for the trade crystals and stayed because the work was good. The Federation is content to wait, because trade tends to fill whatever room it is given.

☀ Alrakis Star Sun K4V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A K-type dwarf burning a low, steady orange. Freight pilots arriving from the unclaimed lanes read its light as the first sign they have reached Federation space.
◈ Alrakis Commerce Vein Asteroid Belt — 4.3 (3.2, -2.9) — — A smaller cousin of Haven's Commerce Fields, rich in iron and copper with a productive seam of trade crystals running through it. Haven's taxation does not reach this far, which is half the reason the miners stay.
❄ Alrakis Ice Fields Ice Field kuiper 8.1 (8.1, -0.4) — — Broad fields of water and nitrogen ice, glazed with a thin layer of helium frost on the oldest faces. Frost-moths skim that glaze on pale wings, their abdomens swelling as they feed. Crews from the vein come here when their tanks run low.
