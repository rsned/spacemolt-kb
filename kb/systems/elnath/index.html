☀ Elnath Star Sun K2V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — The belt only flashes silver because of this star's steady orange light, which catches each tumbling asteroid as it rolls into view.
● Elnath I Planet scorched 0.2 (0.2, -0.0) — — Baked to bare grey stone by a star that sits close and bright. It holds nothing the belt does not hold better.
● Elnath II Planet arid 0.5 (0.1, 0.5) — — Pale dust plains and wind-cut mesas, a fine place to park a tender out of the belt's traffic. Korr's raiders are close enough that nobody leaves one parked long.
◈ Elnath Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 1.0 (0.9, 0.4) — — Massive silver-bearing asteroids move through the field in a stately line, and a scan lights up on rock after rock. The concentration is remarkable, and pilots who have seen other fields say this may be the richest silver in known space.
❄ Elnath Ice Belt Ice Field kuiper 10.0 (-8.8, 4.8) — — Water and nitrogen ice in slow-turning shards, mirror-bright against the dark. Silver crews top off their tanks here, and every shard carries scorch marks from the tenders that braced against it.
