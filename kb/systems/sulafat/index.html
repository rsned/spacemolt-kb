Sulafat

Security 0 Lawless
Empire Independent

Sulafat sits between Starfall's independent miners and Void Gate's last berths, a red-dwarf system with a belt, a gas haze, and room for both. Traffic here is rigs and small haulers rather than convoys, and nobody checks a hold or a name.
