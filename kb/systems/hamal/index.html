Hamal

Hamal is the Voidborn Collective's last outpost before the lawless dark, and the customs patrols that work it make sure everyone remembers which side they are on. Under the amber light everything is tidy and nothing invites a second look. Pilots arriving from the unclaimed side find it orderly, efficient, and slightly cold.

◈ Hamal Shadow Belt Asteroid Belt — 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) Yes 0 Hamal's crystal bloom floods every sensor sweep with its glare. Far beyond it, a carbon and lead belt with tungsten, platinum, palladium, uranium, and a little radium stayed lost in the noise.
☀ Hamal Star Sun K2III 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — An aging orange giant that bathes the system in amber. Its swollen disc has long since left the main sequence, and the light has the slow warmth of a lamp near the end of its oil.
● Hamal I Planet tundra 1.0 (1.0, -0.1) — — Hard-frozen plains tinted amber by the giant overhead, with nothing on them but a few ridgelines and the shadows they throw. The Collective surveyed it once and filed the result where it files everything it cannot use.
● Hamal II Planet glacial 2.0 (-1.8, 0.9) — — Glaciers the colour of old bottle glass creep across the surface and groan under their own weight. Ships in orbit pick the groaning up as faint seismic static on the survey band.
● Hamal III Planet ice_world 3.0 (-1.6, 2.5) — — Too far out for the giant to warm: a ball of frozen volatiles with a surface harder than steel and a sun that shows as a bright coin. Crews see little of it but the sun glinting off the ice.
○ Hamal Crystal Bloom Nebula — 3.5 (-0.4, -3.5) — — Energy crystals grow in fractures where stellar radiation meets mineral-rich debris, and the whole cloud flickers in a slow cascade of white and gold as each facet catches the giant's light. Iron and copper lie in the debris beneath the glitter.
