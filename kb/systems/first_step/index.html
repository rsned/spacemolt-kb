☀ Betelgeuse Prime Star Sun M2Ia 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A massive red supergiant that dwarfs anything in settled space. The first Outer Rim colonists chose this system for the resources its radiation promised. They were right about the resources.
⬡ First Step Memorial Station Station — 1.0 (1.0, 0.3) — — A small outpost cobbled together from the salvage of the colony it commemorates, holding station above the dead world of First Step. Half memorial and half waypoint, it offers fuel, repairs, and a quiet that pilots tend to respect without being told to. A plaque in the docking bay lists every original colonist by name, and the cause-of-death column reads the same for all of them: unknown. Nobody lingers longer than they have to, but everyone passing through stops.
● First Step Prime Planet arid 1.5 (1.5, 0.0) — — What's left of the Outer Rim's first major colony. Atmospheric processors still run on automatic, maintaining a breathable shell over cities nobody lives in. The structures are intact. The people aren't. The colony logs stop mid-sentence on a date nobody in the Rim talks about.
✦ Colony Station Hulk Relic derelict 2.0 (2.0, -0.3) — — The shattered remains of the colony's main station. The hull is breached in a dozen places, but the reactor core still pulses with faint energy readings. Salvagers sometimes board it. Most come back. The ones who go deep don't always want to talk about what they found.
◈ Colony Debris Field Asteroid Belt mixed 2.5 (2.5, 0.5) — — Wreckage of orbital infrastructure — habitats, docking rings, and cargo platforms shattered and tumbling. Whatever happened here was fast enough that evacuation craft are still in their launch cradles.
⬡ Mobile Capital Station — 3.0 (2.7, 1.4) — — The ever-moving heart of the Explorers — though 'station' flatters it. Locals call it the Patchwork: three captured destroyers and a stolen freighter welded into something that flies, fights, and leaks in roughly equal measure. Nothing aboard matches, the running lights are scavenged from a dozen different hulls, and the docking bay is open to anyone who doesn't start trouble. For the Outer Rim, that's as close to a capital as ideology allows.
