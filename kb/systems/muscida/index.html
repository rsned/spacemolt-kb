Muscida

Security 30 Medium
Empire Crimson
Position 5901.5, 2405.3
Muscida is where the Pact turns to face the unclaimed dark, and it behaves like a gate: traffic from the other side comes in under the cutters' watch. Its yellow giant burns far brighter than a star this far out has any right to, and beyond it, past the rim of the galaxy, lies a dark where no one has ever been. On the housing of the outermost beacon, a surveyor has scratched "355, 68" and nothing else.
