Oakridge

Security 0 Lawless

Position -2237.2, -1722.2
Oakridge lies on the roads between the Outer Rim and both Sol and Krynn, and ships pass through it in a near-constant file from Aldhibah to Hatysa and back. Nobody here keeps order, and the road does not seem to need it. The name suggests a forest, which the system has never had.

● Oakridge I Planet scorched 1.0 (0.8, 0.7) — — A charcoal ball whose crust has fractured into plates, with edges that glow dull red where the heat has not yet let go.
● Oakridge II Planet arid 2.0 (1.9, -0.7) — — Ochre scree slopes and dry gullies fan out from a single broad plateau. The ground is loose stone and nothing else.
● Oakridge III Planet terran 3.0 (-1.8, 2.4) — — Wet green lowlands under heavy cloud, stained so dark by the red light that forest and ocean look nearly alike from orbit.
○ Coinflip Nebula Nebula — 3.5 (2.4, -2.5) — — Quantum fragments hang through this nebula in states no one can pin down, so a mining pass can come back rich one time and nearly empty the next.
● Oakridge IV Planet tundra 4.0 (-2.5, -3.2) — — Snow plains and black outcrops under a low red sun, with long shadows that never shorten. It looks cold even to pilots who have only known cabin air.
◈ Oakridge Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 4.1 (0.8, -4.0) — — Iron and copper rock in a wide ring with a thinner vein of zinc. Miners here work in plain view of the lane and rarely look up.
