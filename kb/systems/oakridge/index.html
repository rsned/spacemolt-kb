Oakridge

Position -2237.2, -1722.2
Oakridge lies on the roads between the Outer Rim and both Sol and Krynn, and ships pass through it in a near-constant file from Aldhibah to Hatysa and back. Nobody here keeps order, and the road does not seem to need it. The name suggests a forest, which the system has never had.
