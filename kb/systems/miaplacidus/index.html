Miaplacidus is the first unclaimed system past Mimosa on the road from Sol, and the traffic shows it: haulers outbound from the Confederacy, crews bound for Haven, and the occasional prospector who stopped and stayed. Crews coming through still carry the Confederacy's habits, tidy manifests and a low tolerance for anything unscheduled, and drop them somewhere along the road to Haven.
