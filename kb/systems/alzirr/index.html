Alzirr is a lawless black-hole binary on the Pact's doorstep, with four Crimson systems bordering it. Customs hold the lane at Windmere and the garrison at Blood Forge watches the lane on its side, so whatever crosses has been looked at twice. The Pact catalogued Alzirr decades ago, built nothing, and has not explained why.
