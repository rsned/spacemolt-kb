☀ The Maw Sun BH 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A stellar-mass black hole. Long-range charts log Alzirr as an orange giant — that's the companion star, orbiting at safe distance. The Maw itself emits nothing visible. You know it's there by what bends around it, by what gets pulled in, by the ring of bleeding light that marks its boundary. The Crimson Pact cataloged it decades ago and built no stations here. That should tell you something.

☁ The Bleeding Ring Gas Cloud emission 0.8 (0.3, -0.7) — — The disk of matter spiraling into The Maw. Superheated plasma, crushing magnetic fields, radiation that plays havoc with sensors and shields. Also the only publicly known accessible source of raw fury crystal — conditions here match nothing else in Crimson space. Darksteel ore forms in the outer disk too, compressed beyond what any volcanic process can achieve. The Crimson Pact knows what's here. They've chosen not to build a permanent presence, which means the ore belongs to whoever's willing to mine it.

◈ Fury Core Vein Asteroid Belt — 11.0 (-9.2, 6.1) — — A dense fury crystal vein at the outermost reach of the Alzirr system. Even at this distance from The Maw, its magnetic field has accelerated crystal growth beyond anything seen in standard deposits — the black hole's influence permeates the entire system. Survey equipment struggles with persistent interference. Standard sensors report the field as empty space.
