Canopus is a bend in two long roads, the Krynn-to-Sol and the Krynn-to-Frontier, which run together here between Spica and Thabit. The hulls come through in near-constant file, mostly the same few freighter designs, and lane crews rarely have a reason to look beyond the next waypoint. No law reaches this far into unclaimed space.
