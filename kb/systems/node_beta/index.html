Four resources feeding the Collective's industrial chain. The automated extraction rate would exhaust most asteroid fields in decades, but the Collective's harvesters are precisely calibrated to match natural replenishment.
❄ Node Beta Frozen Drift Ice Field kuiper 27.0 (-16.9, 21.0) — — Water ice maintained at cryogenic temperatures by the outer system's distance from the star.
