☀ Gliese 876 Star Sun M4V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A dim red dwarf just 15 light-years from Sol. Despite its modest appearance, it packs four confirmed planets into an orbit smaller than Mars — including two gas giants in a precise 2:1 resonance and a rocky super-Earth skimming the stellar surface.
● Gliese 876 d Planet scorched 0.8 (0.8, -0.2) — — A rocky super-Earth at 0.02 AU — closer to its star than Mercury to the Sun. Surface temperatures keep the dayside molten; the nightside is cold enough to be geologically distinct. One of the first confirmed rocky exoplanets.
● Gliese 876 b/c Planet jovian 1.9 (1.8, 0.5) — — Two gas giants locked in a 2:1 orbital resonance — for every orbit of the outer world, the inner completes two. The gravitational coupling amplifies both planets' eccentricities in a visible celestial clockwork.
◈ Stillwork Belt Asteroid Belt — 2.3 (2.3, -0.4) — — Asteroids filmed with null matter, a faint stain that dulls scan returns without hiding the rocks. Crews who worked this belt described the space around it as thinner than it should be, and the work stopped when the crews did.
◈ Gliese 876 Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 3.3 (3.2, -0.6) — — Rocky debris beyond the resonant pair, stirred into a chaotic cloud by repeated gravitational pumping from the giant planets below.
