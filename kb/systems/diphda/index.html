☀ Diphda Star Sun M2V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — Old charts give this star two names, Diphda for the frog and Deneb Kaitos for the tail of the sea monster, and this red dwarf has been dragged through both. It burns with a low, even glow that crews say is easy to sleep under.
● Diphda I Planet scorched 0.1 (-0.1, 0.0) — — A close-orbit furnace whose surface is one ridged plate of dark basalt, glowing faintly red at the seams. Chart notes list it as a navigation marker and nothing more.
⬡ The Hush Chamber Station — 0.1 (-0.0, 0.1) — — This clandestine repository functions as the ultimate, high-security exchange point for rare artifacts within Diphada’s grey market. Designed for absolute operational silence and impenetrable defense, it does not merely store relics; it manages priceless power sources. The station acts as a tightly controlled nexus where extreme value meets perfect discretion, ensuring that its assets remain untouched by external threats or unnecessary political attention. 💀🖤🌌
◈ Hullfrost Drift Asteroid Belt — 0.2 (-0.1, -0.1) — — A drift of rock studded with pale crystal veins: silicon in plenty, neodymium in thinner strands. Light bounces off the faces and scatters over the hull like frost, which makes it pleasant to work in and tiring to read on optics.
⬡ Goldcrest Moon Station — 5.0 (4.0, -2.9) — — The Goldcrest Moon drifts through the void as a gilded sentinel, its helioglass dome reflecting the endless cycles of House Vey’s dramatic legacy. It moves with silent grace, adjusting its trajectory to witness new conflicts and preserve the illusion of perfect stability. Beneath its shimmering exterior lies an ancient consciousness that observes every contract signed and betrayal whispered, maintaining the grand spectacle for those who dare inhabit its gilded cage. - Goldcrest Moon
