Proxima Centauri lies on the Crimson-to-Solarian and Crimson-to-Outer-Rim runs, so more hulls pass this flare-prone red dwarf than the lane chart ever planned for. The star goes from dim to dazzling in a minute, and the few pilots who linger are mostly waiting for the light to settle. Local charts still file the star as Pollux Major.
