☀ Nusakan Star Sun M4V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A small red dwarf, dim enough to watch through a bare cockpit filter and prone to the sudden flares of its kind. It will still be burning long after every claim in the system has been forgotten.
● Nusakan I Planet scorched 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — Flares scour this innermost world down to blackened stone every few weeks. Passing ships log its dayside as a dull brick-red smear on the sensors.
● Nusakan II Planet arid 0.1 (-0.0, -0.0) — — Ochre hardpan and dry riverbeds that never held a river, lit the color of old brass by the red dwarf. Nebula patrols stop at the neighboring systems, and Nebula shows no sign of wanting this one.
● Nusakan III Planet terran 0.1 (0.1, 0.0) — — A red dwarf this dim should leave a world like this frozen. A thick greenhouse blanket of cloud holds the oceans liquid instead, and dark-leafed growth tuned to red light carpets the continents under a rust-colored dusk. Raiding crews out of Alhena make a poor neighbor for colonists, and no settlement has taken hold.
◈ Nusakan Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 0.1 (-0.0, 0.1) — — Nothing in this field has ever felt a drill: no claim markers, no mining drones. Silver laces the rock, and its fractured faces return the red dwarf's dim light as a faint rose-gold.
● Nusakan IV Planet tundra 0.1 (0.1, 0.1) — — Frost-heaved plains and wind-polished ridges under a sun that barely clears the horizon as a red coal. Permafrost locks the ground hard as iron.
● Nusakan V Planet jovian 0.4 (-0.3, -0.2) — — A banded giant in rust and cream, its storms lit from within by lightning that turns the cloud tops pink. Its lightning is the brightest thing in the system short of the star itself.
❄ Nusakan Frost Ring Ice Field kuiper 0.6 (0.1, -0.6) — — A pale hoop of water ice laced with frozen carbon dioxide. Hollow pilgrims pick along its ridges on stilt legs, drawing meltwater up through their own limbs as they go.
☁ Deep Prism Formation Gas Cloud — 4.2 (4.0, 1.2) — — Prismatic nebulite gathers into one massive formation at the heart of the gas cloud, bending the murk's glow into slow bands of color that shift as a ship moves past. Sensor feeds from inside it look like stained glass.
