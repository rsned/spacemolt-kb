Aldhibah sits on two long roads out of the Outer Rim, the one toward Sol and the one toward Krynn, and ships cross it in both directions without pause. Crews call it the dull part of the trip, which is the best thing a lane system can be called. No law reaches this far, and none is missed.

☀ Aldhibah Star Sun F5V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A pale F-star, brighter than the dim suns along the road, easy to pick out from far back on the lane. Pilots use it as a landmark and rarely look at it twice.
● Aldhibah I Planet scorched 0.7 (-0.7, -0.0) — — Scorched to bare slag by a star that runs hotter than it looks. The night side cools fast, and crusts of dark glass flake off along the terminator.
● Aldhibah II Planet arid 1.5 (1.3, 0.7) — — Flat bleached plains under a thin dry sky, scored by ancient impact basins. Wind has erased everything that might once have stood on it.
○ Askance Nebula Nebula — 3.5 (2.1, 2.8) — — Flecks of phase crystal flicker through this nebula's dust and vanish the moment a sensor turns to face them. A scan swept past at an angle catches more than one aimed straight on.
❄ Aldhibah Ice Shelf Ice Field kuiper 32.7 (0.1, 32.7) — — A broad shelf of water and nitrogen ice, crossed by a seam of denser deuterium ice that shows grey against the white. Sunlight arrives thin and cold, and the surface looks unchanged since the first survey.
