For centuries BNKI was a catalog footnote, a dim flare star logged as empty and never visited twice, until a swarm of interstellar debris fell into its gravity well and made the old survey a joke. What crews learned from that swarm became a whole way of prospecting, and the BNKI signature is now the first thing surveyors look for when they hunt captured rock anywhere else. The system itself stays a lawless dead end off Pipirima, and its flare storms see to it that nobody builds here.
