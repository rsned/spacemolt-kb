Markab's white giant floods a Solarian border system with hard light, and Confederacy customs cutters work its lanes where the claimed worlds give way to unclaimed space. An old Solarian platform still hangs among the rocks, and the belts hold more precious metal than most frontier systems can offer.

✦ Decommissioned Array Platform Relic derelict 3.6 (3.5, 0.8) — — The remains of a small orbital platform, stripped of most equipment decades ago. Mounting brackets line the outer hull where antenna arrays were once attached — the brackets face outward, away from the galactic core. The platform's power conduits are rated for equipment draws several orders of magnitude above standard communications hardware. A faded Solarian Confederacy seal is visible on the main structural beam. Next to it, a second set of mounting bolts where another emblem was attached — the emblem itself has been removed. The bolts show tool marks.
