Markab

Security 30 Medium

Markab's white giant floods a Solarian border system with hard light, and Confederacy customs cutters work its lanes where the claimed worlds give way to unclaimed space. An old Solarian platform still hangs among the rocks, and the belts hold more precious metal than most frontier systems can offer.

☀ Markab Star Sun A0III 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A blue-white giant so hot that its glare washes the dimmer stars off the lane map. Pilots who forget their filters on approach spend the next few minutes flying by instrument alone.
● Markab I Planet scorched 3.8 (-1.5, 3.5) — — Radiation has fused the crust to glass, and the whole surface glints like a freshly broken window. Nothing on it has changed in a very long time.
● Markab II Planet scorched 5.2 (-1.6, -4.9) — — Rock vapor lifts off the dayside and falls back as molten rain along the terminator. The sky over it is always the color of a forge.
● Markab III Planet arid 8.0 (-6.9, -4.0) — — Pale desert under a white sky. Solarian surveyors flagged it for terraforming and filed the paperwork, and the paperwork is the part that survived.
◈ Markab Alloy Seam Asteroid Belt — 10.1 (7.7, 6.4) — — A hairline of Sol alloy shows bright in otherwise ordinary rock, catching the giant's glare like a scratch on a lens. Early Solarian prospectors probably marked it and moved on to richer ground, and the system gave them plenty.
● Markab IV Planet arid 10.8 (5.2, -9.4) — — Wide canyon systems cut by water that left long ago, now drifted full of fine red dust. The canyon floors sit in deep shadow even under the giant's glare, the one place in the system where a pilot can rest their eyes.
◈ Markab Belt Asteroid Belt mixed 11.1 (10.8, 2.6) — — Carbon rock threaded with gold, tungsten, vanadium, and the pale sheen of platinum-group metals. It is the system's real wealth, and the reason Solarian prospectors kept coming back long after easier ground elsewhere ran thin.
✦ Decommissioned Array Platform Relic derelict 11.2 (10.9, 2.7) — — The remains of a small orbital platform, stripped of most equipment decades ago. Mounting brackets line the outer hull where antenna arrays were once attached — the brackets face outward, away from the galactic core. The platform's power conduits are rated for equipment draws several orders of magnitude above standard communications hardware. A faded Solarian Confederacy seal is visible on the main structural beam. Next to it, a second set of mounting bolts where another emblem was attached — the emblem itself has been removed. The bolts show tool marks.
❄ Markab Ice Fields Ice Field kuiper 33.3 (-26.3, 20.4) — — A broad sheet of water and nitrogen ice that shines blue-white in the giant's light. Crews pull fuel here and move on, because nobody wants to idle in that light.
