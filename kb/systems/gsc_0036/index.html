☀ GSC-0036 Star Sun M6V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — —
☀ The Furnace Sun — 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A Wolf-Rayet star in terminal collapse, shedding its outer atmosphere in superheated shells that sterilize everything within two AU. The Crimson Pact surveyed this system once — the probe lasted eleven seconds before its hull sublimated. The report was filed as 'uninhabitable, permanently' and nobody has argued the point since. The stellar wind carries enough ionizing radiation to blank navigation computers and cook unshielded crew at distances most captains consider safe.
● GSC-0036 I Planet scorched 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — A scorched husk orbiting too close to The Furnace. Whatever this world used to be, it's now a slag ball of melted silicates with a surface temperature that glows visibly in the dark.
● GSC-0036 II Planet arid 2.5 (2.0, -1.5) — — A rocky world far enough from The Furnace to retain a crust, but not far enough to retain an atmosphere. The surface is cratered and lifeless, baked clean by periodic radiation bursts.
● GSC-0036 III Planet terran 4.1 (3.0, 2.8) — — The outermost world. Just barely outside the kill zone. Automated survey beacons placed here have a median lifespan of four months before a flare event fries their electronics.
