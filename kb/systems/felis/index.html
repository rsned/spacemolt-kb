Felis is a dark system with faint crystal formations threaded through it, where null-stained asteroid surfaces swallow light and scanner beams alike. The gloom is the only cover on offer, and pilots who linger tend to prefer not being seen.

☀ Felis Star Sun M6V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A red dwarf so dim that its light seems to give up before it reaches the belts, leaving the system lit mostly by its own crystal glow.
● Felis I Planet scorched 0.0 (-0.0, 0.0) — — Tide-locked against its dim red star and baked on the face that never turns away. The far side is black glass.
● Felis II Planet arid 0.0 (0.0, -0.0) — — An ash-gray desert under a sky the color of dried blood, where the weak sun never climbs far above the horizon.
◈ Felis Belt A Asteroid Belt metallic 0.1 (-0.1, 0.0) — — A plain metallic belt of iron and copper in dark red light, ordinary in every way that its neighbors are not. Miners come here for a day's ore without the dark's strangeness.
◈ Felis Dark Residue Asteroid Belt — 0.1 (-0.1, 0.1) — — Asteroid faces stained black with null matter, so completely that they swallow light and scanner beams alike. On a display the rocks read as holes where asteroids ought to be, and crews approach by eye.
○ Felis Dim Lattice Nebula — 0.1 (0.0, 0.1) — — A lattice of faintly glowing crystal formations strung through the dust like a dying chain of lights. The energy density is low, but the crystals can be harvested, and veil-rays drift between the strands combing the dust for copper.
◈ Felis Belt B Asteroid Belt metallic 0.1 (-0.1, -0.1) — — Iron and copper in quantity, and nothing else. Raiders out of the Gliese 581 stronghold are close enough to drop in on a hauler mid-load, so crews load fast.
❄ Felis Frost Ring Ice Field cometary 0.6 (-0.0, 0.6) — — A ring of cometary ice, water and nitrogen in the bulk with deuterium in the older cores. The comet tails curl away in pale ribbons and look brighter than they are.
