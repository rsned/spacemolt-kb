Station Log

You dock at Grand Exchange Station. Center of galactic commerce. The three-deck Grand Bazaar moves goods from every empire, but it's the Promenade that moves credits — restaurants serving Solarian rations, cantinas pouring Crimson bloodwine, and neural arcades powered by Voidborn matrices keep visitors spending long past when they planned to leave. The Federation takes its cut of everything. The station has the following facilities: - Haven Grand Bazaar: The largest open market in the galaxy. Three decks, every empire represented, cipher-secured. Or it was. A third of the stalls sit empty, cipher verification backing up at every terminal. Traders cluster around the few working stations, tempers fraying. - Nebula Solar Array: Massive solar collection array bathed in constant light from Haven's binary star. Binary starlight floods the collection panels, power output steady and surplus feeding the grid. The most efficient generation in settled space. - Haven Ecosync: Advanced life support with comfort-optimized environmental controls. The air is mountain-fresh, the water tastes clean, and the temperature is always perfect across all commercial districts. Happy traders spend more. - Haven Promenade: The galaxy's premier entertainment and dining district. Restaurants, lounges, neural arcades, and luxury shopping — all hungry for imported goods. But lately, half the restaurants display 'supply shortage' signs, neural arcades flicker on reduced power, and the entertainment rigs run on recycled content. The atmosphere feels desperate. - Haven Algae Cultivation: Zero-gravity bioluminescent algae cultivation tanks feeding Haven's luxury production chains. ...and 47 more facilities.
