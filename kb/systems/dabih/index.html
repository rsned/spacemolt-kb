Dabih is among the fuller systems of the far west, with enough rock, gas, and ice to tempt a settlement. None has lasted, and the planets keep the evidence.

☀ Dabih Star Sun K2V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A K-type star the colour of banked coals, giving every surface in the system a long, warm shadow.
● Dabih I Planet scorched 0.2 (0.2, -0.0) — — Hot enough to glass its own rock and too small to hold any air. Survey drones logged it once and moved on.
● Dabih II Planet arid 0.4 (-0.3, -0.3) — — Pale dunes and salt flats under a thin, dry sky. The dust is fine enough to creep into seals, which is the main thing travellers remember about it.
☁ Dabih Gas Plume Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 0.6 (0.0, 0.6) — — Hydrogen and argon drift in broad sheets, lit pink with neon where ion currents cross, thickest at the core where krypton and a trace of nebula gas gather.
● Dabih III Planet terran 0.6 (0.0, 0.6) — — A heavy atmosphere keeps shallow seas liquid this far from a cool star, and the water looks nearly black from orbit. A colony frame, begun on the largest continent and never closed in, stands against the weather like a skeleton waiting for a body.
● Dabih IV Planet tundra 0.9 (-0.8, 0.3) — — Frozen plains and wind-polished ridges under a sky so clear that stars show through the day.
◈ Dabih Belt Asteroid Belt metallic 0.9 (-0.2, -0.9) — — Iron and copper with a rich seam of aluminum, scattered along a wide lane of orbit. Mirror-skates glide over the rubble, bright scales flashing along their backs as they turn.
❄ Dabih Frost Ring Ice Field kuiper 1.4 (1.1, 0.9) — — A broad, quiet ring of water ice with a rarer vein of deuterium running through it, pale blue where the light catches it and black in the shadow of every boulder.
