☀ Algol Star Sun B8V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — The Demon Star — Beta Persei, a three-body system whose two inner components eclipse each other every 2.87 days, producing a visible dimming that ancient astronomers found ominous. The bright B-type primary is slowly cannibalizing its subgiant companion, pulling mass across in a stream visible in X-ray light.
⬡ Dross Citadel Station — 2.7 (-1.0, -2.5) — — Imperator Dross's command post. Built from the wreckage of three different warships, salvaged and welded together.
☁ Algol Gas Pocket Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 6.0 (5.9, -1.3) — — Ionized outflow from the mass-transfer stream between the eclipsing pair collects in pockets across the outer system, and this one is thick with hydrogen, argon, neon, xenon, and nebula gas. Nobody works it, and the dimming of the inner stars gives its glow a slow, bruised pulse.
