Station Log

You dock at Node Gamma Relay Station. Mid-ring relay node and the system where the Signal was isolated most clearly. The station's primary function is network coordination, but since the Signal discovery, a significant portion of its processing capacity has been redirected to analysis. Signal Master Threx maintains a permanent presence here. Visitors report that the station feels like it's listening. The station has the following facilities: - Reactor Complex: Expanded fusion reactor with secondary containment and load balancing. Today, though, secondary containment fluctuates. Load balancers struggle, and some sectors experience rolling power reductions. - Environmental Processor: Enhanced atmospheric recycling with water purification and thermal regulation. That's the idea, anyway. Temperature swings between sections, water purification runs at reduced capacity, and rationing advisories are posted. - Refueling Station: Multi-bay fuel dispensing facility with automated flow management. Or so it was. Only one bay remains operational, ships queueing at the single functioning pump while the others display maintenance alerts. - Maintenance Deck: Dedicated repair deck with pressurized work bays and diagnostic scanners. But lately, sealant supplies are low. Several bays run unpressurized, forcing crews to work in suits. - Trade Exchange: Organized trading floor with automated order matching and price boards. But something's off — price boards display stale data with intermittent gaps, order matching running in degraded mode with noticeable delays. ...and 21 more facilities.
