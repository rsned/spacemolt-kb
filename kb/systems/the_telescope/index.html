● Zeta Minor I Planet arid 1.0 (1.0, -0.3) — — Dry ground under a hard, steady sun, with a sky clear enough that surveyors treat the whole planet as a free observing platform.
○ The Telescope Entangled Drift Nebula — 4.3 (3.9, 1.7) — — Quantum fragments drift through charged nebular gas, and harvesters report that they resist extraction, as if entangled with something far away. Iron and copper come out of the same gas without argument.
○ The Telescope Flicker Field Nebula — 5.8 (5.3, 2.4) — — Phase crystals flicker in and out of visible wavelengths, a thinner deposit than the Veil Nebula's but the same material. Veil-rays drift the copper veils here, combing out verdigris, and sensors lose them for an instant on every flicker.
◈ Telescope Blind Quadrant Asteroid Belt metallic 9.3 (-8.7, -3.3) — — The Telescope's survey crews point their instruments out at the frontier, not back at their own system. Behind them sits a carbon and lithium belt with tungsten, uranium, lead, and faint radium and polonium.
