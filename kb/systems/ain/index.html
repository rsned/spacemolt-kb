Ain

Ain is one of the rare giant stars in an open cluster known to carry a planet, and the survey crews who confirmed it still bring it up with some pride. Last Light is a single jump away, so Rim explorers treat Ain as the first real step past their last dependable port. Nobody governs it and nobody has tried.

☀ Ain Star Sun K2III 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — Epsilon Tauri — a cool orange giant and member of the Hyades cluster, the nearest open star cluster to Sol. Has a confirmed massive planet discovered by precise Doppler monitoring — one of the few planets known around a giant star in an open cluster.
● Ain B Planet jovian 2.5 (2.4, 0.6) — — Over seven Jupiters of gas, heavy enough that its pull swept the inner orbits clean and flung the debris outward. The empty space it left behind has stayed empty ever since.
❄ Ain Ice Belt Ice Field kuiper 6.2 (6.2, 0.3) — — A wide, cold ring of grey water ice, dull except where fresh breaks catch the orange light. Hollow pilgrims pick along the ridges on their stilt legs, leaving neat paired dimples in the frost.
◈ Ain Tidal Fields Asteroid Belt metallic 9.8 (-9.7, -1.0) Yes 60 Ain B's gravity cleared the inner system long ago and threw much of the debris outward. What survived out here is carbon and aluminum rock, with tungsten, thorium, and a faint trace of dark matter residue.
