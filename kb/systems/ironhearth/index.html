System

Ironhearth

Security 55 Medium
Empire Crimson
Position 5377.7, 2360.1
Ironhearth is the Crimson Pact's population center and closest thing to a civilian homeworld, where families, farms, mines, and refineries sustain both daily life and the war machine. The generals call it the reason they can afford to be generals: what the war machine eats is grown, raised, and forged here. Policing here is a hometown affair, loud and personal, carried out by officers who went to school with the people they stop.

☀ Hearthfire Sun G5V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A warm yellow star, unusual for Crimson space. The locals call it Hearthfire — the one concession to comfort in an empire built on iron. The Pact's civilians chose this system for its habitable zone.
☁ Ironhearth Thermal Vents Gas Cloud atmospheric 0.8 (0.7, -0.2) — — Hydrogen and xenon vent from geological activity on Ironhearth Prime's largest moon. The gas harvests supplement the population's energy needs.
● Ironhearth Prime Planet terran 0.8 (0.7, -0.2) — — The closest thing the Crimson Pact has to a homeworld. Three billion people live here — technically all enlisted, practically a functioning civilization. Schools teach metallurgy alongside weapons drill, and the harvest festivals celebrate ore yields.
⬡ Ironhearth Station Station — 0.8 (0.8, -0.2) — — The hearth the war machine comes home to. Behind the military docking protocols and weapons inspections, Ironhearth is the Pact's most human place — families live here, children are born here, and veterans retire here. The market stocks food and clothing alongside ammunition, and the cantina pours something that isn't technically regulation but nobody's been court-martialed for it yet.
● Ironhearth II Planet terran 1.0 (0.6, 0.8) — — Agricultural world. Hydroponic farms and livestock operations feed the system's population. Crimson agriculture is as regimented as everything else — crop rotations run on military schedules.
● Ironhearth III Planet tundra 1.5 (-1.3, -0.9) — — Gravity 1.3 standard. The Pact uses it for physical conditioning. Recruits who survive a rotation here don't complain about station gravity again.
◈ Ironhearth Fields Asteroid Belt mixed 2.0 (-1.9, 0.7) — — Ironhearth was built to need no one else, and these fields are why: carbon and vanadium for the steelworks, platinum and palladium for finer work, and a trace of rhodium besides. Crews here finish a shift and go home to a planet.
◈ The Old Seam Asteroid Belt metallic 2.6 (-0.5, 2.6) — — The original iron deposit that drew the first Crimson settlers to this system. They built their hearth around it — smelting ore by starlight before the station was even pressurized. The seam still produces, and old-timers say the iron here forges cleaner than anywhere else in the Pact.
❄ Ironhearth Ice Ring Ice Field kuiper 16.3 (2.8, 16.1) — — Nitrogen ice ring at the system's outer edge. Cracked and processed for atmospheric maintenance across the system's habitats.
