Azmidi

Security 30 Medium
Empire Nebula
Position 1595.0, -5826.0
Azmidi sits on the thin edge of Federation space, and prospectors from the unclaimed systems around it drift in to sell. Khambalia's patrols reach this far, but thinly. The Federation has priced the place and decided a station is not yet worth the capital.

☀ Azmidi Star Sun G8V 0.0 (0.0, 0.0) — — A G-type star a shade cooler than Sol, its light turning amber toward the edges of the lane map. Surveyors rated it dull, which in this trade is a compliment.
● Azmidi I Planet arid 0.6 (-0.2, -0.6) — — Wind-scoured sand the color of weak tea, with a few mineral streaks that survey crews tested once and put back on the shelf. Nobody has ever listed it for sale.
● Azmidi II Planet tundra 1.2 (1.2, 0.2) — — Permafrost cracked into wide polygons under a pale sun, the whole surface one shade of grey. No beacon, camp, or claim stake marks it.
◈ Azmidi Belt Asteroid Belt carbonaceous 2.4 (1.8, 1.7) — — Dark carbonaceous rock with bright flecks of platinum-group metals showing where the surface has chipped. It is the system's quiet earner, the sort of belt that pays without anyone advertising it.
☁ Azmidi Gas Cloud Gas Cloud molecular_cloud 2.9 (-2.8, -0.9) — — A cold cloud of hydrogen and argon shot through with xenon, which halo-drifters sip and exhale as faint glowing rings. Each ring hangs in the dark for a few seconds after its maker has passed, like the afterimage of a lantern.
◈ Guildless Facets Asteroid Belt — 3.3 (-2.5, 2.1) — — Trade crystals grow undisturbed in the veins of this belt, and no merchant guild has staked a claim. In Nebula space that is rarer than an honest scale, so the ground is as quiet as it is valuable.
❄ Azmidi Ice Fields Ice Field kuiper 13.8 (5.7, 12.6) — — A wide spread of water and nitrogen ice dusted with ammonia frost, bright where fresh faces have split and grey elsewhere. Crews top off tanks and coolant lines here before the long hop back to Khambalia.
