Praecipua is Latin for chief, the old name of the brightest star in a faint constellation, and its namesake here holds the same lonely rank at the far rim. Only pilots short of aluminum come this far, and most leave as soon as the hold is full. Turn the ship outward and the stars simply stop: a black wall with no horizon, and the edge of everything is right there.
