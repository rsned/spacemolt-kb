Onboard Hydrogen Refinery

A self-contained burner that passively converts hydrogen gas in the hold directly into tank fuel. It skips every refining intermediate and wastes most of the gas — invaluable for a deep-space explorer far from any fuel depot, but never worth it where a station is in reach.
