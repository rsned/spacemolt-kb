DPT is damage ÷ cooldown ticks. Eff DPT spans the weakest to strongest round that fits the weapon's ammo type (ammo damage_mod runs −50% to +100%), so a low-DPT launcher with good ammo can out-damage a high-DPT one with none. DPT/CPU and DPT/Pwr are what matter once the hull's budget, not the slot count, is the binding constraint.
