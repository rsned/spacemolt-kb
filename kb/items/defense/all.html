Resistance columns are per damage type — match them against the damage type that is actually killing you on the weapons page. Values in this style come from an adaptive module: it carries no fixed per-type resistance, but applies that percentage against every damage type, ramping as it learns what is hitting you. So an Adaptive Shield III (35% across the board) trades raw per-type depth for coverage against a hardener’s 30% in one. Penalties are real: several high-bulk modules cost speed, which is why they are a first-class column rather than buried in the description.
