Outpost Kit

A flat-packed deep-space outpost: a hardened lockbox, a fuel bladder, and the mooring spars to anchor them beside a landmark. Deploying one in lawless space plants a members-only faction outpost — a shared storage vault and fuel reserve, nothing more. Far less than a station, and far cheaper to field.
