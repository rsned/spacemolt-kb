How to read the two prices

MKT-AVG is the sell-side volume-weighted average of past trades (price × quantity). It shows a value for any component that has ever traded, whether or not it is for sale right now, and does not check current supply — a "typical cost" estimate.

Galaxy walks the current resting sell orders pooled across every station, filling cheapest-first until the required quantity is met. It is all-or-nothing per component: when a component cannot be fully sourced from live depth it shows —, and the build-cost line shows N/M covered. A large gap between the two columns means a facility is priceable in theory but not actually sourceable from the market right now.

Category stats

Median MKT-AVG build cost by facility level, with the full (min–max) range in parentheses, and how many facilities are currently buildable (inputs fully sourceable from live galaxy depth via BoM or Recipe). The median with a min–max range is shown rather than mean ± sd because costs within a level are strongly right-skewed — most facilities cluster low, with a thin tail of far pricier end-game variants — so a mean would sit well above the typical facility. Cost stats cover facilities with a fully-priced bill, so a level's count may exceed the number priced; — means none at that level are fully priced.
