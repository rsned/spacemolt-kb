Lore
The victory photographs usually show the boarding ship. The recovery manifests show who brought the fuel. Repatriators were commissioned by factions whose captured vessels kept reaching port days after the crews that took them. The ship gives most of its volume to tankage, spares, and working accommodation. Its service connections can keep a damaged prize moving; at a station, the small internal bay takes delivered hulls for the next leg of their journey. The bridge carries two clocks, one for the fleet schedule and one for the last ship still expected home. Crews keep the second clock running until the berth is occupied.
