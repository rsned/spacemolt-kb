Lore
To own one of every ship class is, by the Confederacy's reckoning, less an achievement than a logistics problem: a fleet that large cannot fly itself home, and no existing carrier could move more than a fraction of it. The Reliquary was commissioned to close that gap. Its bay is the largest ever certified for a mobile hull — a cathedral of docking cradles, gantries, and umbilicals, each rated to hold and service a different class, so the collection travels together rather than scattered across a dozen stations. It mounts only enough weaponry to discourage the opportunist, because the Reliquary is not built to fight; it is built to arrive with everything you have ever owned still aboard. The Fleet Logistics Review, which once filed a minority dissent recommending a ten-bay Convoy, is understood to consider the matter finally settled.
