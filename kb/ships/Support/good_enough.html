Lore
Rim logistics is not glamorous work. The Good Enough-class hauls fuel, ammunition, spare parts, and replacement components to fleet positions deep in contested or uncharted space. The name captures the Rim's pragmatic approach to supply chains: the parts don't need to be perfect, they need to be there. With 840 units of cargo capacity and enough fuel to reach anywhere the fleet can go, the Good Enough ensures that Rim operations don't stall for lack of supplies — just for lack of planning.
