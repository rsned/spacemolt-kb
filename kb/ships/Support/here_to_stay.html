Lore
Here to Stay began as a frontier town assembled from pressure drums, machine shops, and the usable half of an abandoned transfer dock. Later additions followed no master drawing, but every corridor has a purpose and every exterior brace has survived a vote about whether it was still worth carrying. Its salvage crews work the local wreck fields while merchants fill the central market with whatever the last system lacked. The engines are slow because the residents insist on bringing the whole town with them.
