Lore
The Pleroma's wards are arranged around a protected clinical archive. Incoming casualty records, neural telemetry, and treatment responses are compared across the fleet so that each receiving team begins with the experience of every team before it. Shielded transfer galleries allow smaller ships to exchange patients and supplies without interrupting the hospital's work. Collective physicians call the design Pleroma because no injury is treated as an isolated event; the whole deployment contributes to the recovery of each patient.
