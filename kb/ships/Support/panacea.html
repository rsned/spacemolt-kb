Lore
Every Panacea carries an accredited teaching hospital. Surgical, recovery, quarantine, and laboratory decks have independent atmosphere loops, allowing clinical trials to continue without displacing casualties. Confederacy medical boards rotate residents through the ships because remote deployments produce cases no university ward can schedule. Their findings return in public reports; patient records do not.
