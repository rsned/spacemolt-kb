Lore
An Emanation begins as a crystalline seed attended by a circle of minds. Habitats, docks, and workshops unfold around it as the circle reaches consensus on the shape required, then settle behind overlapping shields powerful enough to make the new volume feel permanent. The station moves without silencing its markets or interrupting its work. Only during a jump does the outer universe disappear, leaving residents inside a city whose horizon is the Collective's own luminous boundary.
