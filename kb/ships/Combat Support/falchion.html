Lore
The Crimson Fleet nearly didn't build an electronic warfare ship. The concept of attacking something you can't see felt uncomfortably close to sneaking. The compromise: reframing EW as "attacking their sensors." The Falchion doesn't hide or deceive — it burns out enemy electronics with focused interference powerful enough to physically damage sensor arrays. Crimson EW is less subtle than Voidborn shadow games but considerably more destructive. A Falchion's jamming suite doesn't confuse your targeting computer — it sets your targeting computer on fire. Fleet doctrine permits this as a legitimate form of direct combat.
