Lore
The Consensus-class command ship does not relay orders — it shares intent. Fleet vessels within its coordination radius report acting on information they have not yet received through conventional channels. The seven utility slots support a neural-link architecture that the Collective describes as "distributed volition." Admirals of other empires describe Voidborn fleet actions as "choreographed by something that isn't the commander." They are not wrong. They are not seeing the whole picture either.
