Lore
A symposium is a gathering where ideas are debated and refined through structured discourse. The Symposium-class embodies this ideal: eight utility slots support drone bays, electronic warfare suites, fleet communication arrays, and repair systems. The processing core array — the most powerful ever installed on a mobile platform — simultaneously manages drone swarms, fleet tactics, and an automatic minutes-taking system that records every command decision for post-engagement review. Admirals must pass a written examination on committee governance procedures before assuming command. The examination has a 40% failure rate, which the Confederacy considers an appropriate filter.
