Lore
The Paramount-class serves as the nerve center for Federation trade convoys and the occasional military operation. The coordination suite handles route optimization, market analysis, and fleet logistics simultaneously. Five utility slots support every conceivable support system, and the generous cargo hold ensures the Paramount generates revenue even while commanding. Profit and military effectiveness have always been the same line item on the Federation's balance sheet.
