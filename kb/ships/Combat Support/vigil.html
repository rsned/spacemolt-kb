Lore
A vigil is a period of watchful attention — and the Collective considers enforcement to be exactly that. The Vigil-class carries layered shield generators that let it absorb sustained fire while its twin pulse lasers methodically dismantle non-compliant vessels. Voidborn patrol doctrine does not emphasize pursuit or intimidation. The Vigil was already in position before you arrived. It has been watching since before you noticed it. The integrated sensor suite confirms what the Collective already suspected, and the shields ensure the conversation lasts as long as necessary. Resistance is technically an option. The shields make it a poor one.
