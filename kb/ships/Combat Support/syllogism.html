Lore
The Syllogism-class proves that in modern space combat, the most dangerous weapon is a sufficiently powerful processor running sufficiently clever software. Solarian electronic warfare doctrine emphasizes "informational superiority" — knowing exactly what the enemy is doing while ensuring they know as little as possible about you. The massive CPU array supports simultaneous jamming, spoofing, and real-time tactical analysis.
