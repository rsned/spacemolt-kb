Lore
The Controlled Chaos-class is the Rim's answer to "what if we built a ship that carries all the other ships?" Its flight deck accommodates fighters, drones, shuttles, and anything else that fits through the bay doors — which Rim crews have been known to widen with cutting torches when something doesn't quite fit. The "controlled" part of the name is aspirational. Launch operations resemble a busy frontier spaceport more than a military carrier, but the sheer volume of small craft it deploys makes it devastatingly effective.
