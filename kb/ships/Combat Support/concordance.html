Lore
The Concordance-class is the Confederacy's answer to the question of how to coordinate a fleet of ships whose captains all believe they know best. The answer, naturally, is more processing power. The massive CPU array runs tactical models, logistics simulations, and diplomatic communication channels simultaneously. Fleet commanders report that the Concordance's tactical recommendations are "usually correct and always exhaustively documented."
