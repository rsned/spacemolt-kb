Lore
The Pantheon-class manages a constellation of autonomous drones the way the Confederacy manages a galaxy — with layers of oversight, comprehensive logging, and periodic performance reviews. The quad-core processing array coordinates dozens of independent units while the six utility slots accommodate drone bays, electronic warfare suites, and repair systems. Each drone's mission parameters require committee approval. The approval process has been automated, but the committee still convenes monthly to review the automation's decisions.
