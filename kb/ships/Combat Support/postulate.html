Lore
The Postulate-class takes a position and then proves it with drones. Solarian drone doctrine requires each autonomous unit to file a mission report upon return, and the Postulate's processing core dutifully catalogues them all. The four utility slots support a variety of drone management and electronic warfare configurations. Each drone's behavioral parameters are individually peer-reviewed before deployment authorization.
