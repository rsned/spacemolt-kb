Lore
The Nebula Collective built the Paramount to coordinate trade convoys and generate revenue simultaneously — profit and military effectiveness as two sides of the same ledger. The route optimization software and prismatic navigation arrays are gone. Nyx doesn't need them. He knows where every route in the sector runs, which is why he built his station off all of them. The cargo hold, the fuel range, and the command suite that let the Paramount coordinate a fleet while generating revenue are unchanged. Nyx runs 51 systems from a station that appears on no standard transit chart. The Blind Spot coordinates an operation that officially doesn't exist from a location that ships don't visit by accident. The Federation named theirs after commercial ambition. Nyx named his after why no one has found him yet.
