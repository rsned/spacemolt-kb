Lore
Rim drone engineering follows a simple principle: quantity has a quality all its own. The Swarm Boss carries a complement of drones assembled from whatever was cheap, available, and approximately the right shape. No two drones are identical, their firmware is a patchwork of three different operating systems, and their coordination protocols are best described as "enthusiastic." What they lack in precision they make up for in numbers and the sheer bewilderment they cause.
