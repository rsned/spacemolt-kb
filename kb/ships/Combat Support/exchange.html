Lore
The Exchange-class is the Federation's answer to force projection: a mobile base of operations that deploys, coordinates, and resupplies entire fleets. Eight utility slots and a 100-unit CPU array manage drone swarms, fighter wings, and logistics simultaneously. The 720-unit cargo bay ensures the Exchange generates revenue even during military deployments. Named for the institution that makes the Federation's economy work — and, like its namesake, it never closes.
