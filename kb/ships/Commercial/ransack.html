Lore
The Crimson Fleet doesn't build unarmed freighters. The concept offends them. The Ransack ships with twin autocannons and armor plating — a loadout that turns cargo runs into something closer to armed patrols. The cargo bay is generous by Crimson standards, which means it's modest by anyone else's. What the Ransack lacks in speed — the armor plate drops it to a crawl — it makes up for in the certainty that the cargo arrives intact. Fleet logistics classifies Ransack crews as either "merchants" or "acquisition specialists," depending on which direction the cargo is moving.
