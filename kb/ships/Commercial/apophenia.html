Lore
Named for the tendency to perceive meaningful patterns in random data — which is precisely what non-Voidborn cargo inspectors experience when trying to audit an Apophenia's hold. The organic hull creates a micro-environment that other empires' trade goods find inexplicably beneficial. Perishables last longer, crystalline structures become more ordered, and volatile compounds stabilize. The dual shield arrays protect the cargo with the same quiet persistence the Collective applies to everything. The navigation system suggests trade routes based on probability fields rather than market data, and its recommendations are correct more often than chance allows.
