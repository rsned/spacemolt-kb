Lore
The Junk Convoy is the only bulk hauler in production that runs at speed 3 — and with the default afterburner, speed 4. The Rim figured out that for time-sensitive cargo, two fast trips beat one slow trip even if the hold is half the size. The hull is thin, the armor is minimal, and the cargo hold is the smallest in class, but the Junk Convoy delivers goods while heavier haulers are still in transit. Removing the afterburner trades speed for range — the fuel-efficient drive stretches the tank across thirty-three jumps at speed 3. Cheapest T3 bulk hauler on the market, and the fastest by a margin nobody else is willing to pay for.
