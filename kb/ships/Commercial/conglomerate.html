Lore
The Conglomerate-class is the Federation's ultimate long-haul trade platform — a mobile warehouse with the fuel range to reach markets that shorter-legged haulers write off as unprofitable. Slow, but speed is less important when each trip generates enough revenue to fund a small fleet. The five utility slots support logistics management, automated inventory systems, and the Federation's "Total Commercial Awareness" suite. Insurance premiums for a fully loaded Conglomerate have their own financial instrument category.
