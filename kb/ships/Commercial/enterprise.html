Lore
The Enterprise-class hauls more cargo per trip than any other bulk hauler in production. Triple cargo expanders push the hold past a thousand effective units — enough to supply a station or clear a market in a single run. No weapons. No meaningful armor. The tonnage that would have gone to defense systems went to cargo infrastructure instead, because the Federation calculated that hiring an escort is cheaper than building a smaller ship. If someone is shooting at an Enterprise, the quarterly projection has already been revised downward and the insurance claim is being filed. The scanner identifies profitable stops along the route. Everything else about this ship exists to move goods from where they are to where they're worth more.
