Lore
The Logistics Prime-class applies the Confederacy's optimization expertise to cargo transport at capital scale. Self-organizing cargo bays use automated systems to pack, track, and rearrange cargo for optimal mass distribution — the ship literally rearranges its own cargo hold in transit to maintain perfect trim. Less raw tonnage than the mega-haulers, but the CPU runs logistics optimization that calculates multi-stop routes, dynamic pricing, and load balancing in real time — making every cubic meter count. Every other empire builds bigger holds. The Confederacy built a smarter one.
