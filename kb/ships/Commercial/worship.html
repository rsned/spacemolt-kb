Lore
Usually ordered because of poor handwriting, the Worship turns out to be a surprisingly effective cargo hauler — provided you decline to consider the loss rate. It is generally considered uninsurable, and yet it still makes up a substantial fraction of the cargo traffic in the rougher parts of the Rim, moving goods nobody was willing to risk a real ship on. It is often mistaken for four Prayers welded together. This is inaccurate. It is actually just tape.
