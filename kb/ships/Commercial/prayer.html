Lore
The Prayer-class is not a ship. It is cargo containers welded to an engine with a seat bolted to the outside. There is no cockpit. There is no life support. There is no shield generator, no weapon mount, no defense system, and no module slot of any kind. The pilot wears a spacesuit and holds on. Five hundred and forty units of cargo capacity at tier one — more than most ships three tiers higher. The catch is everything else: 40 hull means a stiff breeze is lethal, and there is literally nothing you can do to improve the ship's survivability. It cannot be upgraded. It cannot be armed. It can only carry things from one place to another and hope nothing goes wrong along the way. Named for what the pilot does before undocking.
