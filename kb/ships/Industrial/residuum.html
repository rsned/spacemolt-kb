Lore
The Residuum-class arrives at debris fields the way Voidborn do most things — without announcing itself. The integrated cloaking system and scan-resistant hull mean the Residuum is already sorting wreckage by the time competing salvagers detect the field. What it lacks in cargo volume it compensates for with the strongest towing rig in its weight class and shields thick enough to keep working under fire. Filament-thin tendrils extend into the debris, anchoring to hull fragments with a grip strength that startles engineers who inspect the moorings afterward. Pilots report the ship runs warmer during towing operations, as though the effort is metabolic. By the time anyone else arrives, the Residuum has dragged the best pieces somewhere difficult to find.
