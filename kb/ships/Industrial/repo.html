Lore
Pirates keep making wrecks, and the materials don't haul themselves. The Repo is a stolen Reclaim with the Federation's processing arrays ripped out and the cargo bays expanded — the biggest hold of any salvager in its class. The tow system and extended fuel range that made the original worth stealing are intact. The real-time asset valuation software is gone — salvage components don't replicate Nebula electronics, and pirates don't need a ship to tell them what a wreck is worth. They already know. They made it.
