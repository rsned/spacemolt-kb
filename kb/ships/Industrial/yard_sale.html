Lore
The Yard Sale is what happens when the Rim needs to clean up after a capital engagement and none of the core empires are willing to send their own salvage fleets into disputed space. The strongest tow rating and fastest engines of any T4 salvager mean the Yard Sale strips a wreck field before anyone else arrives to contest it. The cargo hold is respectable rather than enormous — Rim operators compensate by making more trips, faster. Everything recovered is for sale, to anyone, immediately. Price negotiation happens on the flight home.
