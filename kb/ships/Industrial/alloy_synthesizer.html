Lore
The Alloy Synthesizer-class combines multiple raw ore types into advanced alloys through dimensional compression that skips intermediate refining stages entirely. Feed it iron, titanium, and null matter, and the synthesis chambers produce alloys with crystal structures that should require planetary-scale foundries. The Collective's materials scientists describe the process as "showing matter the shape it wants to take." Processing cycles are slow — each synthesis requires dimensional stabilization that takes time — but a single step replaces three conventional refining stages. Other empires cannot replicate the process because it requires dimensional phase states they cannot generate.
