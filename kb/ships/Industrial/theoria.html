Lore
Every Solarian pilot's journey begins with a Theoria. Named for the ancient Greek concept of contemplative knowledge, this vessel embodies the Confederacy's belief that understanding precedes action. The 300-page operations manual (mandatory reading, examination required) covers everything from optimal ore extraction angles to emergency hull breach poetry recitation protocols. Over-engineered by three separate review committees, the Theoria has more diagnostic systems than most ships have systems. The extended utility bus accommodates the Confederacy's insistence that every vessel carry redundant sensor packages. Comes with a two-year comprehensive warranty that covers everything except "pilot-initiated misadventure."
