Lore
All Mine is an entire mining operation welded onto a jump drive. Held together by structural bracing, duct tape, and frontier stubbornness. The cheapest T5 industrial vessel by a wide margin, because the Rim builds capital ships the same way it builds everything: from whatever's available. Twenty-six hundred and forty cargo capacity hauls everything a remote mining operation produces. Two weapon mounts because the frontier is the frontier, and mining bases attract pirates. All Mine can operate independently for extended periods — no station docking required. It is, in every sense, a self-contained mining colony that happens to have engines.
