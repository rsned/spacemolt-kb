Lore
The Liquidator-class is the Federation's heavy salvage platform, designed to strip capital-class wrecks down to their component atoms and haul the results to market. The largest cargo hold of any T4 salvager and a tow rating that keeps pace with the best in class ensure that nothing worth recovering gets left behind. Onboard scanners assess wreck value before the cutting beams fire — the Federation does not spend resources on materials that won't cover extraction costs. The salvage division motto: "The market finds a use for everything." The Liquidator is how they prove it.
