Lore
Resonance mining generates dimensional feedback that would tear an unshielded hull apart. The Deep Resonance solves this by wrapping itself in the heaviest shield array of any mining vessel in production — layered emitters regenerating faster than the feedback can deplete them. The hull underneath is paper-thin and deliberately so; the Collective considers armor dead weight on a ship that never expects to lose its shields. The resonance extraction process pulls twenty-five percent more usable ore from each asteroid than conventional lasers, but the cargo hold is modest — the shield generators and defense hardpoints claimed the space. Pilots who lose their shields in a Deep Resonance learn very quickly how little hull sits between them and vacuum.
