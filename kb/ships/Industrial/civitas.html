Lore
A Civitas is commissioned as territory, not christened as a vessel. Its charter halls, clinics, workshops, markets, and residential districts are assembled around a propulsion spine engineered for relocation rather than flight. When the Confederacy approves a move, traffic controllers spend days revising routes while civil servants transfer the station's legal address one system at a time. The population notices the change chiefly through the viewports; municipal services remain punctual throughout.
