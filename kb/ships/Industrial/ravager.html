Lore
The Ravager-class is what happens when the Crimson Fleet applies capital ship engineering to asteroid mining. Twelve hundred points of hull and fifty-five points of armor make it nearly indestructible by anything short of a dedicated assault force. Two weapon mounts keep contested mining fields clear while five utility slots drive industrial-scale extraction — the Fleet maintains a firm distinction between the tools that mine and the tools that fight. Fourteen hundred forty cargo capacity fills over sustained extraction operations that other empires would call a siege. The Ravager doesn't mine a system — it claims it.
