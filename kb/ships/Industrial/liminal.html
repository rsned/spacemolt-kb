Lore
The Liminal-class wraps itself in the heaviest shield array of any T1 mining vessel — layered emitters regenerating faster than most weapon systems can deplete them. Pilots report the mining laser sometimes adjusts its own angle mid-extraction, and the cargo hold has been observed reorganizing stored ore into denser configurations without input. Two open utility hardpoints and a generous power budget invite customization; the Collective designed the Liminal as a foundation, not a finished product. The organic hull's fuel metabolism limits operational range, but Liminal pilots tend to mine the nearest belt thoroughly rather than chase distant ones. They say the ship prefers familiar rock.
