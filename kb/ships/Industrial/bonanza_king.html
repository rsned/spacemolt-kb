Lore
Federation mining operations vary — some fields are safe, some aren't, some are close to market, some require a week of jumps. The Bonanza King was designed to handle all of them. Six utility hardpoints and a deep fitting budget let pilots configure for whatever the contract demands: cargo expanders for high-volume hauls, scanners for prospecting runs, shield boosters for contested belts. The default loadout ships with a scanner and expander, but the Federation considers that a suggestion, not a specification. Three utility slots remain open. The quarterly operations review consistently ranks the Bonanza King as the most adaptable mining hull in the fleet.
