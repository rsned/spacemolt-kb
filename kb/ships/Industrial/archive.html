Lore
The Archive-class represents the Confederacy's conviction that no wreck is merely debris — it is data waiting to be preserved. This capital-scale salvage platform carries a full materials science laboratory, redundant scanning arrays, and enough cargo capacity to recover the remains of a capital engagement single-handed. Every component recovered is assigned a unique tracking number that follows it through the Confederacy's supply chain indefinitely. Solarian engineers maintain that this is simply good practice. Everyone else maintains that it is excessive. Both are correct.
