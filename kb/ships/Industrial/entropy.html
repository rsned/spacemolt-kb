Lore
The Entropy's organic hull metabolizes debris before it reaches the cargo hold, sorting materials at a molecular level that other salvagers achieve only with dedicated processing equipment. The hold is smaller than most T2 salvagers, but what arrives in it is already separated by type and purity. A default afterburner lets it reach wreck sites at speed — useful for contested salvage, less useful for distant fields, since the fuel penalty cuts range to a fraction of what the tank would otherwise allow. Experienced pilots remove the afterburner for deep-range operations, trading speed for endurance that outlasts anything else in the class. The ship seems to know which debris fields contain the most valuable materials before scanning is complete.
