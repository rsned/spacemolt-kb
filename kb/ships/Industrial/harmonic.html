Lore
The Harmonic-class extends Voidborn harmonic extraction to capital scale. The dimensional frequency generators vibrate entire asteroid fields at their resonant frequency, separating ore from rock across multiple targets simultaneously. Nine hundred shield points protect against resonance feedback — when you vibrate asteroids at frequencies that shouldn't exist, the universe occasionally objects. Eight utility slots accommodate the sensor arrays, harmonic tuners, and dimensional stabilizers needed for safe operation. The Collective insists the process is "cooperative" rather than "destructive." The asteroids have not been asked for comment.
