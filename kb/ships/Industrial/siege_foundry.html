Lore
The Siege Foundry was designed to turn a distant campaign into home ground. Its docks receive warships, its production decks replenish a faction's chosen industries, and its drill halls train replacements beneath the same armored roof. Crimson doctrine treats relocation as an operation in its own right: stores are counted, the destination is declared, and the fortress advances behind its fleet. Once the furnaces are lit again, the campaign's supply line ends at the Foundry's gates.
