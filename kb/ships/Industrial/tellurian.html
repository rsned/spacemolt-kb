Lore
The Tellurian represents the pinnacle of Solarian industrial engineering. Its utility suite — the most extensive on any ship in the galaxy — supports autonomous mining drone deployment, real-time geological surveying, on-board ore processing, and quality assurance systems that operate without crew intervention. The ship identifies deposits, deploys extraction drones, processes the raw ore into refined materials, and stores the results in the 2,400-unit cargo bay. The entire operation runs autonomously according to algorithms refined over decades of geological research. The Confederacy considers the Tellurian its greatest industrial achievement. It is a mining operation in a hull.
