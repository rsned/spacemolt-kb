Lore
The Verglas-class harvests ice through dimensional phase-shifting at capital scale. The extraction system reaches through the surface of ice bodies without disturbing them, pulling target materials directly from deep cores that conventional equipment cannot access. Nine hundred shield points protect against the dimensional stress of sustained phase-extraction operations. The technique is unique to the Voidborn — other empires lack the dimensional technology to reach into solid matter without breaking it. The Collective considers this approach both more efficient and more respectful of the ice body's structural integrity.
