Lore
The Crimson Fleet produces more wrecks than any other empire, and leaving salvage on the field wastes materiel. The Warhammer ships with dual cargo expanders for raw haul volume and the fitting budget left over gives pilots real choices — mount a weapon, add armor, extend the fuel range. That flexibility matters because the stock fuel tank is short. This is a local operations ship, built to strip wreck sites in Crimson space and haul everything home in one trip. The tow rating won't win any races, but when a wreck field has more loot than a lighter salvager can carry, the Warhammer earns its name. Fleet recycling doctrine: what was theirs is ours.
