Lore
The Nebulous extracts gas through resonant membrane arrays that vibrate at frequencies unique to each cloud's composition — adapting in real time without pilot input. The hull shimmers faintly during extraction, condensation forming patterns that Voidborn engineers photograph but don't explain. With only a harvester and shield booster fitted by default, the remaining fitting capacity dwarfs anything else in the gas harvester class. The shield array regenerates damage faster than most gas clouds can inflict it, which is fortunate — the hull underneath is tissue-thin by T2 standards. The best yield bonus in class comes standard. What pilots do with the rest of the space is their problem.
