Lore
Rule 45, as the old traders tell it, says to expand or die. The corollary, which the traders do not write down, is that someone has to collect what the others leave behind. The Write-Off does that work with no ceremony. It does not care who won. It arrives when the field is quiet, takes the wreck by the bow, and has the hold sorted before it reaches the scrapyard.
