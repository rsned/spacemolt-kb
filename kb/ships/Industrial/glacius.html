Lore
The Glacius-class was designed to go where the ice is and stay until the ice isn't. Dual cryo-extraction systems run continuously while the onboard spectrometer catalogs compositions with Confederacy-standard twelve-decimal precision — redundant for most operations, mandatory for all of them. The fuel reserves are conspicuously oversized for a mining vessel, a design choice the committee justified with a seventy-page operational range study that nobody disputes and nobody has finished reading. Certified for independent extended deployment in all known ice field conditions. The sixty-page operations manual covers everything from extraction protocols to emergency shelter construction. Compliance is verified annually.
