Lore
The Bonesaw-class is what happens when the Crimson Fleet applies warship design philosophy to industrial vessels. Three weapon mounts protect a cutting array that can disassemble a cruiser hull in under an hour. The cargo bay is reinforced to mil-spec — not for structural reasons, but because Bonesaw crews have a documented tendency to ram debris that proves difficult to cut. Fleet Command classifies the Bonesaw as a support vessel. Bonesaw crews classify themselves as apex predators of the wreck field.
