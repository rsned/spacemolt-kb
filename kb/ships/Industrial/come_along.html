Lore
Come Alongs grew out of salvage disputes where both crews arrived with tow cables and neither brought patience. Their docking collars accept more hull shapes than the manuals admit, and the bunkroom can empty through the collar in seconds. Frontier courts recognize possession, registry, and prior claim in roughly that order, depending on which judge is awake.
