Lore
The Prospect comes with the Federation's compliments — and a pre-loaded database of current commodity prices across 200 stations, a loyalty chip tied to your Federation trade account, and a glossy brochure outlining upgrade paths starting at just 1,999 credits. The expanded cargo hold isn't generosity; it's an investment in future transaction volume. The fuel-efficient drive was a practical decision — every credit spent on fuel is a credit not spent at a Federation marketplace. Includes a 90-day trial of Federation Premium Trade Intelligence (auto-renews at competitive rates).
