Lore
The Siege Foundry combines mining, smelting, and munitions production into one hull. Feed it asteroids, and the internal forges turn raw ore into ammunition, weapon components, and replacement parts. Three weapon mounts on a T5 industrial ship — more guns than most dedicated warships two tiers below. The hull — 2,500 points backed by 120 armor — is built to sustain operations in hostile space indefinitely. The Crimson Fleet designed the Siege Foundry to operate behind enemy lines: mine local resources, produce ordnance, and keep fighting without ever returning to a supply base. A mobile weapons factory that turns asteroids into ordnance. The galaxy has not yet determined whether this is brilliant or terrifying. The Crimson Fleet considers it both.
