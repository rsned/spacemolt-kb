Lore
The Colossus is the Mace scaled to apocalyptic proportions. A ship designed to physically dock with enemy stations and deliver a small army through breaching systems that can cut through anything short of neutronium. Eight weapon mounts suppress station defenses during approach. The armor is designed to absorb sustained point-blank fire from fixed emplacements. The boarding capacity can seize and hold a medium-sized station in a single operation. Building a Colossus is a statement of intent — the Crimson Fleet doesn't build one unless it plans to take something worth taking. And the Crimson Fleet always plans to take something worth taking.
