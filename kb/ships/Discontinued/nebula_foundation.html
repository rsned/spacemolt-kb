Lore
The Foundation-class is the Federation's largest construction platform, capable of assembling entire stations from raw materials in the field. 960 units of cargo capacity store prefabricated components, and eight utility slots support fabrication arrays, structural scanners, and project coordination systems. Where a Foundation parks, a new chapter of Federation commerce begins. The three weapon mounts are described as "site security" — because a half-built station full of rare materials attracts unwanted attention.
