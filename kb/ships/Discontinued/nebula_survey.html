Lore
The Survey-class is the Federation's answer to the question: "What's out there that we haven't monetized yet?" Extended fuel reserves for months-long expeditions, a high-capacity CPU array for mapping and analysis, and five utility slots for every conceivable survey instrument. The comfortable crew quarters are a practical decision — well-rested explorers discover more profitable resources.
