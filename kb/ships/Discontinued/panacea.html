Lore
The Panacea-class is a fully accredited hospital ship with research capabilities that rival most planetary medical centers. Four independent life support units maintain separate sterile environments for surgical, recovery, quarantine, and research operations. The medical AI holds honorary degrees from three Solarian universities and maintains a diagnostic accuracy rate the Confederacy Medical Board describes as "statistically indistinguishable from perfect." Patients receive a comprehensive discharge summary whether they want one or not.
