Lore
The Shiv has no weapons because it IS the weapon. Twelve tons of tungsten-reinforced prow designed to breach hull plating and deliver a boarding party directly into the enemy ship's interior. The design is deliberately cheap — Crimson Fleet accountants classify Shivs as ammunition, not vessels. The armor rating is excellent for its size because the entire ship is essentially armor wrapped around a cockpit and a marine compartment. No weapons, minimal fuel, maximum speed toward the target. Shiv pilots either become heroes or statistics, and the Crimson Fleet honors both.
