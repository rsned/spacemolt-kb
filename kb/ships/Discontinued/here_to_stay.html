Lore
The Here to Stay-class is what the Rim builds when it wants to plant a flag somewhere and make it stick. Part command post, part supply depot, part repair facility, part community center — it's a mobile station for frontier settlements too new or too stubborn for permanent infrastructure. It moves at speed 1, which by Rim standards is practically stationary, but the hull is the toughest thing the Rim has ever built. When a Here to Stay arrives in a system, it means the Rim isn't just visiting. The name isn't a description — it's a promise.
