Lore
The Cauterizer's name tells you everything about Crimson medical philosophy: close the wound, stop the bleeding, get them back in the fight. The five utility slots accommodate medical bays, trauma systems, and pharmaceutical fabricators optimized for rapid treatment rather than comprehensive care. Crimson combat medics train in triage under fire — literally, since the Cauterizer operates in active combat zones. Two weapon mounts are "standard Crimson medical equipment" according to fleet procurement. Three defense slots keep the hospital functional. The cargo hold carries medical supplies and replacement crew — because the Crimson Fleet treats both as combat consumables.
