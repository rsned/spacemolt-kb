Lore
The Crimson Fleet explores for strategic advantage, not scientific curiosity. The Partisan ships with twin autocannons because Crimson doctrine does not recognize the concept of an unarmed reconnaissance mission — the first report from an unknown system should include what you had to shoot on the way in. The fuel tank is generous for a combat hull, sized for deep-penetration patrols into neutral space and back without refueling. Sensor arrays are military-grade, optimized for threat detection rather than mineral surveys, though they work for that too. Partisan captains have standing orders to report defensible positions, choke points, and resource deposits worth fighting over. The exploration report template has a section for "recommended force composition for seizure."
