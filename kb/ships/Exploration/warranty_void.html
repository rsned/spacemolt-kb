Lore
Named for its relationship with every manufacturer whose parts went into its construction, the Warranty Void is built for pilots who believe the map's edge is a suggestion. The fuel system extracts more range from every cell through techniques that void approximately seventeen different safety certifications, and the default afterburner lets pilots choose: burn fast at Speed 6 for short dashes, or pull the afterburner and cruise at Speed 5 for runs that reach the far side of the galaxy. No defense slots and minimal armor — the survival strategy is not getting caught, and at Speed 6, most things can't catch you. Rim explorers don't report their modifications. They report their discoveries.
