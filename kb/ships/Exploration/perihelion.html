Lore
Every cubic meter aboard the Perihelion serves double duty as laboratory space. The fitting capacity accommodates research instruments that would strain smaller vessels — xenobiology sample containment, spectrographic arrays, gravitational anomaly mapping rigs — and the processing power to analyze everything in the field rather than hauling raw data home. Expedition-grade fuel reserves keep the ship operational deep in uncharted space. Published more peer-reviewed papers per ship-year than any other vessel class in service, which the crews attribute to equipment and the equipment manufacturers attribute to luck.
