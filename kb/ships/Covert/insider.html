Lore
The Federation publicly denies operating intelligence vessels. The Insider-class is officially classified as a "market research platform." Its massive CPU array, long-range sensors, and electronic warfare suite are described as "competitive analysis tools." The high speed for its tonnage is explained as "time-sensitive delivery requirements." The Federation's intelligence division has the best quarterly returns of any department, and nobody asks how.
