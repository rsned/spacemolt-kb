Lore
Even the Crimson Fleet needs things delivered quietly. The Dirk carries a cloaking device, an autocannon, and 110 units of cargo space behind scan-resistant plating — enough to move contraband through a customs checkpoint without slowing down. The reinforced hull absorbs the kind of attention that finds smugglers on contested routes, and the single weapon mount discourages anyone who gets curious. The tradeoff is range: 24 jumps means the Dirk works best on short, planned runs where the pilot knows every refueling stop. Crimson command does not issue flight plans for the Dirk. They issue deniable suggestions.
