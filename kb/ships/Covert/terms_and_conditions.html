Lore
Named for what you should have read before buying one, the Terms and Conditions features more hidden compartments than a magician's stage cabinet. Hull panels conceal cavities, life support ducting has removable sections, and the engine housing contains spaces that officially don't exist. The scan-resistant lining won't fool a dedicated customs cruiser, but it handles routine sweeps well enough to keep the interesting cargo off the manifest. When stealth fails, speed doesn't — most customs patrols can't match the Terms and Conditions in a straight chase, and Rim pilots know every shortcut between here and the next jurisdiction.
