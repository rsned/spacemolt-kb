Lore
Solipsism: the philosophical position that only one's own mind is sure to exist. The Solipsism-class intelligence platform collects signals, communications, and data patterns from across entire star systems, then processes them through a computational substrate that other empires cannot audit or verify. Intelligence reports from a Solipsism are consistently accurate yet contain insights that could not have been derived from the intercepted data alone. Whether the ship is inferring brilliantly or fabricating convincingly — and whether there is a meaningful difference — is a question the Collective finds amusing rather than troubling.
