Lore
Six weapon mounts. The Juggernaut doesn't chase — it arrives, and then whatever it arrived at stops existing. Purpose-built for siege operations against stations, capital ships, and fortified positions, the Juggernaut trades speed for firepower on a scale that other empires consider excessive. The Crimson Fleet considers "excessive firepower" a contradiction in terms. The slow speed is a design feature: the Juggernaut's role begins after the enemy has been pinned in place by faster ships. Crimson siege doctrine is simple — the Juggernaut is the last thing the enemy sees, and it arrives after they've already lost the ability to run.
