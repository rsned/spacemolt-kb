Lore
Seven weapon mounts. The Devastator doesn't arrive at battles — it ends them. The Crimson Fleet's primary capital warship carries enough firepower to engage multiple cruiser-class targets simultaneously, and the armor to survive while doing it. The power grid is purpose-built to run all seven weapons at sustained fire without thermal throttling. Crimson fleet doctrine positions the Devastator as the centerpiece of any serious operation: everything else in the fleet exists to get the Devastator into position and keep it there. Enemy fleet commanders have standing orders to prioritize Devastators. They rarely succeed.
