Lore
Speed 5 bare hull — 4 with the standard armor plate — catches anything short of a dedicated interceptor. An autocannon and missile launcher put out enough firepower to make most T1 merchants cooperative, and the 80-unit hold means a successful intercept actually pays. The problem is everything the Rim didn't budget for: 75 hull, 40 shields, 3 base armor. A prolonged fight in the Five Finger Discount is a short flight home in a pod. Thirty jumps on a tank before refueling, so pick your targets within range.
