Lore
The Crimson Fleet built the Broadaxe to answer one question: what does a professional warship look like? Four weapon mounts. Armor thick enough to absorb return fire while all four are running. Pirates who captured them asked a different question: how many of these pieces do we actually need? The armor went to the parts counter. Four mounts stayed on a steel frame that takes hits harder without Crimson alloys absorbing the impact. Nobody has suggested running them in rotation. Nobody has suggested anything. The guns run when you tell them to and the engagement ends when they don't need to anymore. Four on the floor. The answer to the Crimson Fleet's question turned out to be: just the guns.
