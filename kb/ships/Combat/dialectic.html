Lore
The Dialectic-class resolves disagreements through superior argumentation — specifically, five weapon mounts delivering synchronized volleys computed by a dedicated fire-sequencing processor. The Confederacy's Tactical Doctrine Board spent two years debating whether concentrated or distributed firepower was philosophically superior. The Dialectic represents their conclusion: why not both, with extensive documentation supporting either configuration.
