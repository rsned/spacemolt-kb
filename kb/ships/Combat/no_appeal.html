Lore
The walls of Voss Redoubt are patched with hull plating from a dozen different ship classes. Every piece came from a ship that tried to leave Alhena space without permission. Commandant Voss has been running the same operation for long enough that the patch history is visible in the architecture. The No Appeal is the operational expression of Voss's doctrine: it doesn't chase, it doesn't negotiate, and it doesn't leave. The flight deck deploys assault craft while the weapon mounts suppress point defense, and the boarding bays process whatever remains. The fury alloy and crimson siege plating that gave the original its armor rating are gone — the rebuilt hull takes hits harder. Voss considers this appropriate. His crew earns their place. The ship earns its reputation the same way.
