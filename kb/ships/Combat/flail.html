Lore
The Flail carries the same autocannon-and-missile loadout as a frontline fighter, mounted on a hull with four times the cargo space. The tradeoff is thinner shields — 35 points won't absorb sustained fire from anything serious. The standard armor plate drops speed from 4 to 3 but adds enough plating to keep the hull intact through a smash-and-grab. One free utility hardpoint. Crimson logistics tracks Flail sorties as revenue operations.
