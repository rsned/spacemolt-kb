Lore
The Singularity-class carries shield arrays that produce readings other empires' sensors classify as "errors." Four defense slots support a layered shield architecture that the Collective describes as "recursive probability reduction." Enemy weapons officers report that targeting solutions decay faster than they can be computed. Crew members occasionally experience mild deja vu, which the ship's manual lists under "features" rather than "known issues."
