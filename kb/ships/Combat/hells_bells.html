Lore
Speed 4 on a medium hull. Other empires' naval architects insist this shouldn't be possible without cutting something critical. They're correct — the Hell's Bells sacrifices armor, shield coverage, and what Solarian engineers call "structural margin" in exchange for engines that can outrun most interceptors. Rim pilots consider the ability to choose when and where to fight worth more than the ability to absorb punishment. The name comes from what you hear when the engines go to full power.
