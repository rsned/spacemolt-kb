Lore
The Premiere-class is the Federation's flagship civilian vessel — a floating embassy for commerce, diplomacy, and the demonstration of wealth. Gold-trimmed interiors, panoramic observation decks, and shields rated for capital-ship engagement ensure passengers travel in both comfort and safety. The Federation books the Premiere for treaty negotiations, because nothing says "negotiate from strength" like hosting talks on a ship worth more than your opponent's fleet.
