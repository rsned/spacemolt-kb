Lore
The Midlife Crisis is what passes for a luxury liner on the Rim. The viewing lounges have actual windows (reinforced glass, not portholes). The quarters have doors that close (most of the time). The bar serves drinks that are technically beverages (by frontier health standards). It's fast enough to outrun boredom and has enough fuel to reach the interesting parts of the galaxy. Passengers are mostly retired prospectors spending their earnings, adventurers who can't sit still, and the occasional fugitive enjoying the view.
