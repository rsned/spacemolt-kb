Lore
There is no economy cabin aboard a Comet, no business tier, no standby list — there is one class of service, and the Federation considers it the only one worth selling. Every suite is private, every passenger is the most important person on the manifest, and the entire ship is built to put them where they are going at the fastest pace the galaxy permits, because the people who charter a Comet measure a journey in opportunities missed, not credits spent. It carries no cargo worth the name and no weapons at all; what it sells is the hour you would otherwise have lost. The drive holds that pace while barely touching the fuel reserves, so a crossing never breaks for a refueling stop. To book passage is to declare that your time is the most valuable thing in the system — and to be seen declaring it.
