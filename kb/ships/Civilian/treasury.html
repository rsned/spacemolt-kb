Lore
The Treasury-class is the Federation's rolling advertisement for the rewards of commercial success. Panoramic viewports, climate-controlled cargo bays (for "delicate acquisitions"), and a passenger suite that would be excessive in a hotel. The three defense slots protect not just the hull but the lifestyle investment within. Five utility slots accommodate everything from entertainment systems to portfolio management terminals. The Federation offers financing at preferred customer rates.
