Lore
The Promenade-class is the Confederacy's idea of luxury travel: comfortable cabins, excellent life support, and a fully equipped lecture hall. Passenger amenities include a research-grade library, three seminar rooms, and a lounge with panoramic viewports certified to withstand "all anticipated environmental conditions." Popular for inter-system academic conferences, faculty relocations, and the occasional diplomatic function where everyone pretends the after-dinner talk is optional.
