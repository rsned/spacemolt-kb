Building a Player Station

A player station is a base your faction owns outright, founded in lawless space from a Station Core. Owning one means no rent at NPC stations, your own storage and services, and a market other pilots can use if your station sits on a crossroads.

The official guides leave the important parts out: what a new station actually comes with, what each facility consumes to keep running, and in what order to build. This guide records what we measured while founding Conjunction Junction in Cloverfield on 2026-10-25. Build costs and upkeep below come from the game catalog. Our own observations are labelled measured, other players' reports player-reported, and the official docs doc-quoted.

The one rule: a facility whose upkeep isn't in faction storage stops working, and one unsatisfied facility takes the whole station from thriving to critical (measured). Stock every input before the build finishes.

Reading your station

The official guides don't say how to tell what a station needs. These are the places the server does tell you, all measured at Conjunction Junction and Grand Exchange in October 2026.

Question Command Field to read
Is the station healthy overall? get_base, or the reply when you dock condition (thriving … critical), satisfied_count / total_service_infra, satisfaction_pct
Which facility is starving? facility list maintenance_satisfied (true/false) and maintenance_level (0–1) per facility; the description switches to its degraded text
What does a facility consume? facility types --facility_type <id> maintenance_per_cycle (also shown on some facilities in facility list)
What is life support short of right now? facility list or get_base life_support.starved and life_support.remediation (only present when short), against supply, demand, maintenance
Is there enough power? facility list power.supply vs power.current_draw, battery_stored / battery_capacity, efficiency
What is a build still waiting on? facility list construction.pending[].status (waiting_for_materials) and materials[].quantity_missing
What does it cost the treasury? facility list labor_per_cycle per facility; faction_rent (0 at your own station)

What the server doesn't tell you: for a service facility (the Locker Bay, the Faction Workshop) there is no "starved" list, only maintenance_satisfied: false. To know what to haul, compare the facility's maintenance_per_cycle (the upkeep tables in the steps above) against view_faction_storage at the station. One cycle is 100 ticks (about 16.7 minutes); 86.4 cycles make a day.

What happens when it runs dry (measured): the Locker Bay went maintenance_satisfied: false with maintenance_level 0.4, the station dropped from thriving to critical ("Critical infrastructure failure. Emergency lighting only."), and get_base listed no services at all, so personal storage stopped working.

Station administration

Run these while docked at your own station. info is open to any member; every other action needs the ManageBases permission. Outposts support only info, set_name and set_description. Rows marked ✅ have been run live.

Where the official docs are wrong

  • What a station comes with. The factions guide says market, refuel, repair, shipyard, crafting, power and life support are included. A fresh station has faction storage only, with 0 power, 0 life support and no services (measured / player-reported).
  • Founding command. base-builder says facility action=found_station; what works is build_base (measured).
  • Storage capacity units. base-builder lists faction storage in total items (500 → 50,000); the factions guide lists per item type (100k → 500k). The station's 500k is per item type (player-reported).
