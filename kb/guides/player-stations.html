Guides / Building a Player Station

Building a Player Station

From Station Core to a running home station, with every facility's build cost and upkeep, measured at Conjunction Junction (Cloverfield).

A player station is a base your faction owns outright, founded in lawless space from a Station Core. Owning one means no rent at NPC stations, your own storage and services, and a market other pilots can use if your station sits on a crossroads.

The official guides leave the important parts out: what a new station actually comes with, what each facility consumes to keep running, and in what order to build. This guide records what we measured while founding Conjunction Junction in Cloverfield on 2026-10-25, and while running it since (last updated 2026-11-08: 24 faction facilities, condition thriving, 8 of 8 counted facilities satisfied). Build costs and upkeep below come from the game catalog. Our own observations are labelled measured, other players' reports player-reported, and the official docs doc-quoted.

The one rule: stock every facility's upkeep in faction storage before the build finishes. A facility short of upkeep doesn't stop outright: it drops into a reduced operating mode (for example running at half speed) and its description switches to its degraded text. The station's condition steps down gradually with the share of facilities left unsatisfied (measured: one unpaid dock out of four took us from thriving to operational). On a young station with only one or two facilities, that one shortfall is most of the station, which is how ours hit critical (measured).

Next: production support. With storage and crafting in place, build life support and power, the two things production facilities need, so production lines (including the fuel combustor that fills the refuel tank) can start moving to the station.

Step 3: Life Support - Atmospheric Recycler (atmospheric_recycler)

Build: 600,000 cr + 1,200 Steel Plate + 200 Water Recycler · 150 ticks

The heaviest upkeep of the early build: five different inputs every cycle. Water Ice is usually mineable nearby (Cloverfield has an ice field); plan a supply of the other four before you build. Other players build it third (player-reported); so do we, together with power, because the two of them are what production facilities need: this is where more advanced crafting and processing can move to the station. (We actually built the refueling dock first, and its empty tank had to wait for both.)

Stock all five before it finishes, and keep them stocked. If one input runs out, the plant goes starved and the station drops to operational. get_base then shows a life_support.starved list naming every upkeep input, not just the missing one, so check faction storage for the real gap. Ours was oxygen. After a restock, the plant only recovers at its next maintenance check: ours went from starved to thriving within about 60 ticks, taking just the 1 oxygen it still owed (measured 2026-10-27).

Without it, production is refused outright: "no_life_support: This station has no life support yet. Build a life support facility (faction_build) before adding facilities that draw life support." (measured 2026-10-27, building an H2 Fuel Combustor).

There is no other level-1 life-support facility a player station can build. The recycler upgrades to Climate Control Array (life support 20), Biosphere Ring (35) and Full Habitat Dome (60).

Upgrade, don't stack. When you need more life support, upgrade toward the Full Habitat Dome rather than building more recyclers. One station that stacked recyclers spends roughly twice the consumables of a few domes for the same life support (player-reported). The upgrades cost more credits up front, but upkeep never stops, so the domes come out ahead over time.

Service facilities list no power draw in the catalog, but production facilities do, so power, with life support, is what unlocks production lines at the station. Capacitor Bank, Buffer Array and Cryo Capacitor Vault add battery storage on top of any of these. In a system with a star the collector grid is the simplest to keep fed: 2 Circuit Board per cycle and nothing else. The Bunker-Fed Reactor burns Fuel instead: 55 per cycle.

Size power for your defenses, not your production. Production facilities draw little unless every one of them runs at once, and players report never getting near the power limit that way. In combat, production stops and the defenses get all the power. So build only enough to run your defense facilities at peak (player-reported). One experienced builder would rebuild all their power as Bunker-Fed Reactors if infrastructure could be dismantled. It can: see Moving a facility.

We built the grid at Conjunction Junction on 2026-10-26; the build reply again gave 750 corporation_management XP to every member and showed rent 0. Once it finished, get_base gained a power block (absent until then) showing supply: 20, matching the catalog, and the grid counted toward station health alongside the Locker Bay (satisfied_count 2 / total_service_infra 2) (measured).

Station so far: 9 upkeep items, 14 units/cycle · power 20 / 0 · life support 12 / 0 (supply / draw)

Step 5: Refuel service - Refueling Dock (refueling_dock)

service: refuel

Build: 500,000 cr + 2,000 Fuel Cell + 800 Steel Plate · 80 ticks

Upkeep/cycle
Fuel Cell2
Liquid Hydrogen1

Right after life support and power, because everyone needs fuel: your own miners and haulers working out of the station, and every pilot passing through. Waiting for those two first pays off here: the dock's tank starts empty, and the way to fill it, an H2 Fuel Combustor, is a production facility that needs both. The dock brings its own 5,000-unit tank: get_base max_fuel went from 200 to 5,000 when ours was built, with no tank expansion (measured 2026-10-26). Faction members draw from the shared tank for free; everyone else pays your station price (set with station set_refuel_price).

⚠ Stock its upkeep before it finishes: Fuel Cell and Liquid Hydrogen every cycle. Ours finished with no fuel cells in faction storage, and get_base showed satisfied_count 3 / total_service_infra 4, condition operational ("signs of deferred maintenance") and no refuel in services: an unpaid dock is built but sells nothing. After we deposited 1,000 fuel cells the station was back to thriving, 4/4, with refuel listed, within 69 ticks (measured).

⚠ Then fill the tank. The dock starts empty, and a refuel answers "station_fuel_empty: This station's fuel reserves are depleted" (measured). The way to fill it is an H2 Fuel Combustor: crafting manufacture_fuel_basic there turns 10 Liquid Hydrogen into 100 fuel per run (about 1 s a run, 8 cr labor), and the output goes straight into the station's tank, not into faction storage, whatever the craft reply says. Each completed run adds its 100 fuel as it finishes, so the tank climbs steadily while the job runs. Faction storage and the treasury cover the job when the crafter's own can't (measured 2026-10-27). Until the combustor is up, a CRFT member can top the tank up from their own ship (faction_deposit_items, item_id=fuel).

Until a refuel service exists, ships docked at your station can't refuel at all; the server's message (no_fuel_source … insufficient credits for station refueling) is misleading, because credits are not the problem (measured).

Station so far: 11 upkeep items, 17 units/cycle · power 20 / 0 · life support 12 / 0 (supply / draw)

Step 6: Market - Trade Concourse (trade_concourse)

service: market

Build: 500,000 cr + 400 Circuit Board · 100 ticks

Upkeep/cycle
Trade Authenticator1

The rest of a full station is its services. A mature station lists eleven: crafting, marine_training, market, medical, missions, personnel, refuel, repair, salvage_yard, shipyard and storage (player-reported). Each has a level-1 facility a player station can build.

The market comes after power, life support and fuel, because it starts the station earning: crossroads traffic trades here, and you set the listing fee they pay with station set_market_fee. The catalog lists its upkeep as one Trade Authenticator a cycle, but players have found the authenticators go per trade, possibly on top of the per-cycle charge, so a busy market eats them much faster than any other upkeep (player-reported). Line up a steady source before you build, sized to your trade volume rather than to the cycle.

Station so far: 12 upkeep items, 18 units/cycle · power 20 / 0 · life support 12 / 0 (supply / draw)

From here on the order is flexible. With storage, crafting, production support, fuel and a market in place, build the remaining services as you need them. Our suggestion is shipyard, repair and salvage yard first, then missions, medical, personnel and marine training, and more fuel tank once the dock's 5,000 runs short.

Step 7: Shipyard - Outfitting Bay (outfitting_bay)

service: shipyard

Build: 500,000 cr + 800 Circuit Board + 4,000 Steel Plate · 300 ticks

Upkeep/cycle
Steel Plate2
Titanium Alloy1

The most expensive level-1 service to build: thousands of Steel Plate plus Circuit Board, and the longest build time. Upkeep includes Titanium Alloy. It upgrades to Fabrication Yard, Assembly Complex and Grand Shipworks.

We built the Outfitting Bay on 2026-11-01 and upgraded it to a Fabrication Yard the next day (14,000 Steel Plate for the upgrade). An upgrade is its own action: faction_build fabrication_yard is refused with "this is an upgrade — use 'upgrade' action instead", and upgrade needs both facility_id and facility_type (measured). The level-2 yard builds two hulls at once; further commissions queue as PENDING (measured 2026-11-08).

Building ships here. Empire designs are exclusive: a station can build another empire's class only with a ship license, bought once per class by the faction (buy_ship_license; T1 1M, T2 5M, T3 10M, T4 20M, T5 50M). We licensed the outerrim Congregation (1,900 cargo) for 1M. A faction-funded commission then draws every material from faction storage, the treasury pays the labor plus a 10% royalty on the materials' value to the design's empire (about 650–820 cr per Congregation), and the finished hull is delivered to whoever placed the order (measured). Anyone can commission a class; supply the materials, because at the cash price the yard has to buy them itself, and a material that isn't on the market stalls the build for good (player-reported).

Step 14: Fuel storage - Fuel Tank Expansion (fuel_tank_expansion)

Build: 500,000 cr + 1,500 Ceramite Plating + 800 Fuel Tank + 8,000 Steel Plate · 100 ticks

Upkeep/cycle
Steel Plate5

Late in the build: the refueling dock's own 5,000-unit tank is enough to get a station started. Add expansions when that runs short. Each expansion adds 50,000 to the station's shared tank, and you can build several. Fill it from fuel-production facilities or by depositing a docked ship's fuel (storage deposit, item_id=fuel). With station set_auto_buy_fuel on, visiting pilots can sell fuel into the tank too, paid from the treasury.

Fuel production needs tank capacity, and the refueling dock's tank counts: you do not need an expansion first. ⚠ Server bug (developer-confirmed, being fixed): for up to about 17 minutes after the dock finishes, the station does not count its tank yet. During that window, building a fuel producer or depositing fuel wrongly answers "no_fuel_bunker: This station needs fuel tank capacity … Use 'faction_build' with facility_type='fuel_tank_expansion' first." We hit exactly that with an H2 Fuel Combustor (measured 2026-10-26). Wait and retry; don't build an expansion because of it. Fuel production also draws life support and power.

⚠ Don't build a Faction Fuel Bunker here. At your own station the server refuses it: "a private Faction Fuel Bunker doesn't make sense on your own station — its fuel would just sit unused. Build a Fuel Tank Expansion … instead" (measured 2026-10-26).

Station so far: 15 upkeep items, 32 units/cycle · power 20 / 0 · life support 12 / 0 (supply / draw)

Faction facilities. These serve the faction rather than visitors: a shared ship pool, more members, and named storage buckets.

Step 15: Ship garage - Faction Ship Garage (faction_ship_garage)

Build: 600,000 cr + 150 Circuit Board + 300 Steel Plate · 80 ticks

No upkeep

A shared pool of up to 20 hulls at the station. Put a ship in with faction_deposit_items and its ship ID (the reply's action is faction_garage_store); any member docked there takes it with switch_ship, which transfers ownership to them and parks their old ship at the station (measured). This is how we hand freshly built Congregations to haulers. For a pilot outside the faction, the owner can gift the parked hull instead: send_gift <username> <ship_id> while docked where it is (gift_ship, measured 2026-11-08).

Step 16: Recruitment - Hiring Board (hiring_board)

Build: 75,000 cr + 25 Circuit Board + 75 Steel Plate · 20 ticks

No upkeep

Without a recruitment facility a faction is capped at 20 members: invites still go out, but accepting one answers "faction_full: This faction has reached its maximum of 20 members". The Hiring Board raises the cap to 50 (the upgrades: 100, 200 and more). Members already above the cap keep their seats; we had 38 (measured 2026-11-08). We built ours from a dismantled package, with no other materials (see Moving a facility).

Reading your station

The official guides don't say how to tell what a station needs. These are the places the server does tell you, all measured at Conjunction Junction and Grand Exchange in October 2026.

Question Command Field to read
Is the station healthy overall? get_base, or the reply when you dock condition (thriving … critical), satisfied_count / total_service_infra, satisfaction_pct
Which facility is starving? facility list maintenance_satisfied (true/false) and maintenance_level (0–1) per facility; the description switches to its degraded text
What does a facility consume? facility types --facility_type <id> maintenance_per_cycle (also shown on some facilities in facility list)
What is life support short of right now? facility list or get_base life_support.starved and life_support.remediation (only present when short), against supply, demand, maintenance
Is there enough power? get_base or facility list power.supply vs power.current_draw, battery_stored / battery_capacity, efficiency. get_base shows the power block only once a power source exists
What is a build still waiting on? facility list construction.pending[].status (waiting_for_materials) and materials[].quantity_missing
What does it cost the treasury? facility list labor_per_cycle per facility; faction_rent (0 at your own station)

Only some facilities count toward condition. With 24 faction facilities built, get_base reads total_service_infra 8 (measured 2026-11-08): the services, power, life support, fuel storage and the defense facilities count; production lines, the garage, the office and storage buckets don't. A starving production line slows itself down without moving the station's condition.

What the server doesn't tell you: for a service facility (the Locker Bay, the Faction Workshop) there is no "starved" list, only maintenance_satisfied: false. To know what to haul, compare the facility's maintenance_per_cycle (the upkeep tables in the steps above) against view_faction_storage at the station.

Two clocks: facility rent bills every 100 ticks (about 17 minutes; player-reported), but facilities consume their upkeep on a separate maintenance cycle of about 3,000 ticks (about 8.3 hours). We measured that at Conjunction Junction: the Locker Bay, Faction Workshop and Refueling Dock each used one cycle's upkeep roughly every 3,000 ticks, each on its own phase, counted from its build (measured 2026-10-27). A 100-tick cycle would have used about 30 times as much. Don't be misled by get_base: the life_support block reports maintenance_cycle_ticks: 100 (measured), which does not match what the other facilities consume. Whether life support itself burns its upkeep every 100 ticks is being measured from our recycler's water ice. Until the cycle is pinned down precisely, this page gives upkeep per cycle only.

What happens when it runs dry: the effect is graduated, not a switch.

  • The facility drops into a reduced operating mode rather than stopping: it keeps running, but slower (for example at half speed). Its description switches from the satisfied text to the degraded text, and maintenance_level falls below 1 (player-reported). For example, the catalog text for the Climate Control Array reads "Thermal regulators hold steady. Water purification runs ahead of consumption, building a healthy reserve." when satisfied, and "Temperature swings between sections and water purification lags behind demand." when degraded (doc-quoted).
  • The station's condition steps down through its levels with the share of facilities unsatisfied. One unpaid Refueling Dock out of four gave satisfied_count 3 / total_service_infra 4 and condition operational (measured).
  • On a very young station that share is large. Early on, the Locker Bay went maintenance_satisfied: false with maintenance_level 0.4, the station dropped from thriving to critical ("Critical infrastructure failure. Emergency lighting only."), and get_base listed no services at all, so personal storage stopped working (measured).

What you can build here

The facility catalog marks every facility a player station can build with player_station_buildable: true: 63 of the catalog's 2,907 facilities (catalog read 2026-10-26). Plan from that flag, not from the official guides. Besides the steps above, it covers:

  • Power: Stellar Collector Grid, Fuel Cell Generator, Bunker-Fed Reactor, Scrap Reactor, Dark Matter Reactor, plus Capacitor Bank and Buffer Array storage.
  • Services for visitors: Refueling Dock, Trade Concourse, Repair Slip, Outfitting Bay, Contract Terminal, Reclamation Dock, Fabrication Yard.
  • Defense: Point Defense Battery, Station Defense Turret, Shield Projector, Reinforced Plating, up to Siege Lance and Shield Bastion.
  • Food and leisure venues: Dockside Diner, Frontier Cantina, Rec Lounge and others.

The faction_* facilities you rent space for at NPC stations (fuel bunkers and similar) are not on the list.

Moving a facility

facility action=faction_dismantle takes a facility apart so you can rebuild it somewhere else. We first ran it on 2026-10-27, on an Iron Refinery that CRFT rented at Grand Exchange Station (measured):

  • The facility goes offline at once and vanishes when the dismantle finishes. Any repair in progress is cancelled, and repair costs already spent are not refunded.
  • It takes a few minutes: 231 ticks for the refinery.
  • All the build materials come back. The refinery returned 250 Control Node and 2,510 Copper Piping, exactly its catalog recipe, packed into 28 labeled packages.
  • Packages land in the faction storage at that station. Haul them like any cargo, unpack them at the destination, and rebuild from the recovered materials.
  • Items left in faction storage can still be viewed and withdrawn after the storage facility itself is dismantled.
  • Rent pauses while a facility is dismantling (from the rent note).

Rebuilding from packages (measured). You don't have to unpack: pass the package IDs straight to the build, facility faction_build <type> --package_ids <id>[,<id>…]. Each package must hold exactly what the build still needs of an item, or the build is refused up front. We rebuilt the iron refinery at Conjunction Junction this way on 2026-10-28, and on 2026-11-08 built a Hiring Board from its single package with no other materials. Each package takes 100 cargo, and dismantling costs one Cargo Container per package (doc-quoted). Moving four facilities' packages (Fiber Draw Tower 41, Polymer Extruder 40, Intel Terminal 3, Hiring Board 1) from Grand Exchange took three Congregation trips of up to 19 packages each.

The reply does not say whether the credit part of the build cost (89,000 for the refinery) comes back. Expect to pay it again when you rebuild. Rented faction_* facilities at NPC stations cannot be rebuilt at your own station. Check that the facility is on the buildable list before dismantling it to move it.

Where the official docs are wrong

  • What a station comes with. The factions guide says market, refuel, repair, shipyard, crafting, power and life support are included. A fresh station has faction storage only, with 0 power, 0 life support and no services (measured / player-reported).
  • Founding command. base-builder says facility action=found_station; what works is build_base (measured).
  • Storage capacity units. base-builder lists faction storage in total items (500 → 50,000); the factions guide lists per item type (100k → 500k). The station's 500k is per item type (player-reported).
  • Fuel storage. The Faction Fuel Bunker is refused at your own station; the server points you to the Fuel Tank Expansion instead (measured).
  • "Build a fuel tank expansion first" right after the dock finishes. For up to about 17 minutes after a Refueling Dock completes, fuel producers and fuel deposits are refused with no_fuel_bunker, naming the Fuel Tank Expansion. It is a server timing bug (developer-confirmed); the dock's own tank is enough, so retry later (measured).
  • fuel_depot is not yours to build. It is the empire-station fuel facility; at your own station the refuel facility is the Refueling Dock (developer-confirmed).
