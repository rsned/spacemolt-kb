Codex entry

Thrives in caustic chlorine clouds where nothing else can breathe, storing the corrosive gas in a pressurized internal sac. Its skin has no scale, no shell — just a hide thick enough to hold the gas inside. Other fauna avoid its haunts; it has them to itself.
