Field notes (KB lore, unofficial)

A pale, papery grazer of the helium fields, met as a shoal resting on the ice and remembered as the shoal leaving.

Changed
the wing is a skimming surface, not an aerofoil — broad pale membranes that ride the ice on a film of boiled-off helium the way a hovercraft rides its skirt; the abdomen became a sac that takes the superfluid straight in and swells visibly with it.
Feeds
skims helium ice, drawing the superfluid up into the abdomen until it is round and taut, then rests on the field to digest.
Defends
the shoal erupts. Not fast — nothing on the ice is — but in every direction at once, which for a predator that came for one amounts to the same thing.
