Codex entry

A bloated bladder-creature that crawls nitrogen-ice fields, boiling the ice inside a pressurized sac until it swells pale and taut. Frontier miners call them pressblisters — a blister that might pop at any moment, walking toward your boot. When threatened, it vents the gas in a defensive burst and bolts.

Field notes (KB lore, unofficial)

A walking blister, the miners' name and the accurate one.

Changed
the gut became a pressurized sac that boils nitrogen ice into gas and holds it; the hide stretched over it pale and taut, domed and blistered; the limbs became rows of stubby feet for crawling the field.
Feeds
crawls nitrogen-ice fields, taking the ice in and boiling it inside the sac until the whole animal swells, then lives on the gas.
Defends
pops on purpose — vents the sac in a defensive burst of nitrogen that throws off a predator's aim, and bolts, deflated, while the cloud clears.
