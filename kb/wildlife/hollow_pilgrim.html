Codex entry

Walks ice ridges on hollow stilts, drawing meltwater up through each leg and filtering it through internal membrane stacks. It moves between fields in slow, purposeful processions that early explorers mistook for migrations of something far more intelligent. Each pass leaves a small, clear water crystal on the ice behind it.

Field notes (KB lore, unofficial)

The tall, cowled walker of the ridges, and the reason early survey logs are full of "processions".

Changed
legs became hollow stilts that are also drinking straws — meltwater is drawn up through each one into stacks of filter membranes inside the shell-body; the body itself is a hollow cowl of pale bone plates around those stacks, which is why it scans empty.
Feeds
walks the ridges where ice is thinnest, drawing meltwater up through its stilts and filtering it clean; what it cannot use it leaves behind as a small, clear water crystal on the ice, one per pass.
Defends
procession. It does not run and does not fight; it keeps walking, slow and purposeful, in single file with its kind, and looks enough like something intelligent that most things leave it alone.
