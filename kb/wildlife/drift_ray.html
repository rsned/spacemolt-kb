Codex entry

Broad and slack-winged, it lets the argon banks carry it, filter-fronds trailing as it strains the inert gas from the drift without ever seeming to work at it. A shadow or a charging weapon and it folds and slides away edge-on, lost in the haze inside a breath. A bank strained clear of argon is a bank it has already left.

Field notes (KB lore, unofficial)

The glider of the cloud margins, all wing and no hurry.

Changed
cartilage became a rigid-light spar lattice; the wing became a slack solar-and-static membrane that the argon banks carry and that trickle-charges the animal as it drifts; gills became a wide intake scoop trailing filter-fronds.
Feeds
lets the bank do the flying and strains the inert argon from the drift through its trailing fronds without ever seeming to work at it; a bank strained clear is a bank it has already left.
Defends
the bioluminescent spot-rows are a counting trick — they fire in patterns that make one ray read as three on a predator's motion sense. Failing that, a shadow or a charging weapon and it folds, slides away edge-on and is lost in the haze inside a breath.
