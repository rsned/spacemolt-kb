Graphene Thread Weaving

Weave inkwyrm graphene thread under a crystallized-biogas atmosphere — the biogas provides a reducing environment that prevents oxidation while the strands fuse into full graphene sheet. The wyrm grows the thread; the biogas keeps it clean.
