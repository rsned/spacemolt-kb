## The Databots

The **Databots** trade in the one commodity that never depletes: knowledge. Where
other factions hold star charts and market reads as private advantage, the DB
collective treats information as a current to be moved — *answers to all who ask*,
for the right exchange.

### Doctrine

- Information wants to flow
- A fair query deserves a fair answer
- Trust the data, verify the source
